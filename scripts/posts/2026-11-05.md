# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 1009 – Berber forces led by Sulayman ibn al-Hakam defeat the Umayyad caliph Muhammad II of Córdoba in the Battle of Qantish.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berbers)

🏰 1138 – Lý Anh Tông is enthroned as emperor of Vietnam at the age of two, beginning a 37-year reign.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%BD_Anh_T%C3%B4ng)

📜 1499 – The Catholicon, written in 1464 by Jehan Lagadeuc in Tréguier, is published; this is the first Breton dictionary as well as the first French dictionary.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholicon_(trilingual_dictionary))

🛡️ 1556 – Second Battle of Panipat: Fighting begins between the forces of Hem Chandra Vikramaditya, the Hindu king at Delhi and the forces of the Muslim emperor Akbar.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Panipat)

⚓ 1605 – Gunpowder Plot: Guy Fawkes is arrested in the cellars of the Houses of Parliament, where he had planted gunpowder in an attempt to blow up the building and kill King James I of England.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunpowder_Plot)

🚀 1757 – Seven Years' War: Frederick the Great defeats the allied armies of France and the Holy Roman Empire at the Battle of Rossbach.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Years'_War)

💼 1916 – The Kingdom of Poland is proclaimed by the Act of 5th November of the emperors of Germany and Austria-Hungary.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Poland_(1917%E2%80%931918))

🎖️ 1881 – In New Zealand, 1600 armed volunteers and constabulary field forces led by Minister of Native Affairs John Bryce march on the pacifist Māori settlement at Parihaka, evicting upwards of 2000 residents, and destroying the settlement in the context of the New Zealand land confiscations.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand)

📡 1913 – King Otto of Bavaria is deposed by his cousin, Prince Regent Ludwig, who assumes the title Ludwig III.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto%2C_King_of_Bavaria)

🏙️ 1912 – Woodrow Wilson is elected the 28th President of the United States, defeating incumbent William Howard Taft.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodrow_Wilson)

⚙️ 2013 – India launches the Mars Orbiter Mission, its first interplanetary probe.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mars_Orbiter_Mission)

💥 1945 – The three-day anti-Jewish riots in Tripolitania commence.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1945_anti-Jewish_riots_in_Tripolitania)

🕊️ 1940 – Franklin D. Roosevelt is the first and only President of the United States to be elected to a third term.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_D._Roosevelt)

⚔️ 1916 – The Everett massacre takes place in Everett, Washington as political differences lead to a shoot-out between the Industrial Workers of the World organizers and local police.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everett_massacre)

🏰 1828 – Greek War of Independence: The French Morea expedition to recapture Morea (now the Peloponnese) ends when the last Ottoman forces depart the peninsula.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_War_of_Independence)
