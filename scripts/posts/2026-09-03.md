# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -36 – In the Battle of Naulochus, Marcus Vipsanius Agrippa, admiral of Octavian, defeats Sextus Pompey, son of Pompey, thus ending Pompeian resistance to the Second Triumvirate.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Naulochus)

🏰 301 – San Marino, one of the smallest nations in the world and the world's oldest republic still in existence, is founded by Saint Marinus.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Marino)

📜 590 – Consecration of Pope Gregory I (Gregory the Great).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Gregory_I)

🛡️ 673 – King Wamba of the Visigoths puts down a revolt by Hilderic, governor of Nîmes (France) and rival for the throne.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wamba_(king))

⚓ 863 – Major Byzantine victory under general Petronas at the Battle of Lalakaon against an Arab raid under emir Umar al-Aqta, who is killed in battle.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_Empire)

🚀 1189 – Richard I of England (a.k.a. Richard "the Lionheart") is crowned at Westminster.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_I_of_England)

💼 1260 – The Mamluks defeat the Mongols at the Battle of Ain Jalut in Palestine, marking their first decisive defeat and the point of maximum expansion of the Mongol Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamluk)

🎖️ 1335 – At the congress of Visegrád Charles I of Hungary mediates a reconciliation between two neighboring monarchs, John of Bohemia and Casimir III of Poland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_I_of_Hungary)

📡 1411 – The Treaty of Selymbria is concluded between the Ottoman Empire and the Republic of Venice.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Selymbria)

🏙️ 1650 – Victory over the royalists in the Battle of Dunbar opens the way to Edinburgh for the New Model Army during the English invasion of Scotland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Dunbar_(1650))

⚙️ 1777 – American Revolutionary War: During the Battle of Cooch's Bridge, the Flag of the United States is flown in battle for the first time.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War)

💥 1939 – World War II: The United Kingdom and France begin a naval blockade of Germany that lasts until the end of the war. This also marks the beginning of the Battle of the Atlantic.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blockade_of_Germany_(1939%E2%80%931945))

🕊️ 1941 – The Holocaust: Karl Fritzsch, deputy camp commandant of the Auschwitz concentration camp, experiments with the use of Zyklon B in the gassing of Soviet POWs.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Holocaust)

⚔️ 1861 – American Civil War: Confederate General Leonidas Polk invades neutral Kentucky, prompting the state legislature to ask for Union assistance.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War)

🏰 1925 – USS Shenandoah, the United States' first American-built rigid airship, was destroyed in a squall line over Noble County, Ohio. Fourteen of her 42-man crew perished, including her commander, Zachary Lansdowne.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Shenandoah_(ZR-1))
