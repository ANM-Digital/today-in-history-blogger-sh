# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 484 – Pope Felix III excommunicated patriarch Acacius of Constantinople for his support of the Henoticon and his support of the removal of patriarch John Talaia, leading to the Acacian schism.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Felix_III)

🏰 1364 – Troops of the Republic of Pisa and the Republic of Florence clash in the Battle of Cascina.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Pisa)

📜 1402 – Ottoman-Timurid Wars: Battle of Ankara: Timur, ruler of Timurid Empire, defeats forces of the Ottoman Empire sultan Bayezid I.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Ankara)

🛡️ 1540 – Henry VIII of England marries his fifth wife, Catherine Howard.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_VIII)

⚓ 1635 – Eighty Years' War: The Spanish capture the strategic Dutch fortress of Schenkenschans.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eighty_Years'_War)

🚀 1778 – Constitution of the province of Cantabria ratified at the Assembly Hall in Bárcena la Puente, Reocín, Spain.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantabria)

💼 1962 – Beginning of the 8th World Festival of Youth and Students.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8th_World_Festival_of_Youth_and_Students)

🎖️ 2001 – Australian Ian Thorpe becomes the first swimmer to win six gold medals at a single World Championship meeting.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Thorpe)

📡 1938 – Hawaii Clipper disappears between Guam and Manila as the first loss of an airliner in trans-Pacific China Clipper service.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan_Am_Flight_229)

🏙️ 2002 – Nine coal miners trapped in the flooded Quecreek Mine in Somerset County, Pennsylvania, are rescued after 77 hours underground.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal_mining)

⚙️ 1939 – The Sutton Hoo helmet is discovered.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sutton_Hoo_helmet)

💥 1973 – Summer Jam at Watkins Glen: Nearly 600,000 people attend a rock festival at the Watkins Glen International Raceway.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summer_Jam_at_Watkins_Glen)

🕊️ 2018 – Australian Wendy Tuck becomes the first female skipper to win the Clipper Round the World Yacht Race.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wendy_Tuck)

⚔️ 1808 – Mahmud II became Sultan of the Ottoman Empire and Caliph of Islam.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahmud_II)

🏰 2022 – Catastrophic floods devastate Eastern Kentucky, resulting in 45 fatalities and causing damage to thousands of homes and businesses.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Appalachian_floods)
