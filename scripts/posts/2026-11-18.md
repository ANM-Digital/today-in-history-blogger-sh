# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 326 – The old St. Peter's Basilica is consecrated by Pope Sylvester I.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_St._Peter's_Basilica)

🏰 401 – The Visigoths, led by king Alaric I, cross the Alps and invade northern Italy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visigoths)

📜 1095 – The Council of Clermont begins: called by Pope Urban II, it led to the First Crusade to the Holy Land.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Clermont)

🛡️ 1105 – Maginulfo is elected Antipope Sylvester IV in opposition to Pope Paschal II.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipope)

⚓ 1210 – Pope Innocent III excommunicates Holy Roman Emperor Otto IV for invading the Kingdom of Sicily after promising to recognize papal control over it.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Innocent_III)

🚀 1302 – Pope Boniface VIII issues the Papal bull Unam sanctam, claiming spiritual supremacy for the papacy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Boniface_VIII)

💼 1421 – St Elizabeth's flood: A dike in the Grote Hollandse Waard in the Netherlands breaks, killing about 10,000 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Elizabeth's_flood_(1421))

🎖️ 1601 – Tiryaki Hasan Pasha, an Ottoman provincial governor, routs the Habsburg forces commanded by Archduke Ferdinand II of Austria who were besieging Nagykanizsa.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiryaki_Hasan_Pasha)

📡 1730 – The future Frederick the Great of Prussia is granted a pardon by his father and is released from confinement.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_the_Great)

🏙️ 1905 – Prince Carl of Denmark becomes King Haakon VII of Norway.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haakon_VII)

⚙️ 1971 – Oman declares its independence from the United Kingdom.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oman)

💥 1991 – After an 87-day siege, the Croatian city of Vukovar capitulates to the besieging Yugoslav People's Army and allied Serb paramilitary forces.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Vukovar)

🕊️ 1944 – The Popular Socialist Youth is founded in Cuba.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_Socialist_Youth)

⚔️ 1872 – Susan B. Anthony and 14 other women are arrested for voting illegally in the United States presidential election of 1872.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_B._Anthony)

🏰 1970 – U.S. President Richard Nixon asks the U.S. Congress for $155 million in supplemental aid for the Cambodian government.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Nixon)
