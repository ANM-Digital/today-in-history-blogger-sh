# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -164 – Judas Maccabeus, son of Mattathias of the Hasmonean family, rededicates the Temple in Jerusalem, an event that is commemorated each year by the festival of Hanukkah. (25 Kislev 3597 in the Hebrew calendar.). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judas_Maccabeus)

🏰 235 – Pope Anterus succeeds Pontian as the nineteenth pope.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Anterus)

📜 1386 – Timur of Samarkand captures and sacks the Georgian capital of Tbilisi, taking King Bagrat V of Georgia captive.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timur)

🛡️ 1620 – Plymouth Colony settlers sign the Mayflower Compact (November 11, O.S.). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plymouth_Colony)

⚓ 1739 – The fortress of Portobello, Panama, surrenders to a Royal Navy squadron under Edward Vernon during the War of Jenkins' Ear.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Porto_Bello_(1739))

🚀 2019 – Tesla launches the SUV Cybertruck. A gaffe occurs during the launch event when its "unbreakable" windows shatter during demonstration.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesla%2C_Inc.)

💼 2004 – The Paris Club agrees to write off 80% (up to $100 billion) of Iraq's external debt.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Club)

🎖️ 1985 – United States Navy intelligence analyst Jonathan Pollard is arrested for spying after being caught giving Israel classified information on Arab nations. He is subsequently sentenced to life in prison.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Pollard)

📡 2019 – Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is indicted on charges of bribery, fraud, and breach of trust.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Netanyahu)

🏙️ 2015 – The government of Belgium imposes a security lockdown on Brussels, including the closure of shops, schools, and public transportation, due to potential terrorist attacks.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Government_of_Belgium)

⚙️ 1992 – A major tornado strikes the Houston, Texas area during the afternoon. Over the next two days the largest tornado outbreak ever to occur in the US during November spawns over 100 tornadoes.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado)

💥 1980 – A deadly fire breaks out at the MGM Grand Hotel in Paradise, Nevada (now Bally's Las Vegas). Eighty-five people are killed and more than 650 are injured in the worst disaster in Nevada history.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MGM_Grand_fire)

🕊️ 1998 – Finnish satanist Jarno Elg kills a 23-year-old man and performs a ritual-like cutting and eating of body parts in Hyvinkää, Finland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satanism)

⚔️ 1970 – Vietnam War: Operation Ivory Coast: A joint United States Air Force and Army team raids the Sơn Tây prisoner-of-war camp in an attempt to free American prisoners of war thought to be held there.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Ivory_Coast)

🏰 1977 – Minister of Internal Affairs Allan Highet announces that the national anthems of New Zealand shall be the traditional anthem "God Save the Queen" and "God Defend New Zealand".. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Internal_Affairs_(New_Zealand))
