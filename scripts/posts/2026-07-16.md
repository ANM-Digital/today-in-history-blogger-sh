# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 622 – The Hijrah of Muhammad begins, marking the beginning of the Islamic calendar.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hijrah)

🏰 997 – Battle of Spercheios: Bulgarian forces of Tsar Samuel are defeated by a Byzantine army under general Nikephoros Ouranos at the Spercheios River in Greece.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Spercheios)

📜 1054 – Three Roman legates place - acting illegally on their own authority - a bull of excommunication against Michael I Cerularius, the patriarch of Constantinople, and his followers on the altar of Hagia Sophia during Saturday afternoon divine liturgy. Historians frequently describe the event as the formal start of the East–West Schism.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papal_legate)

🛡️ 1212 – Battle of Las Navas de Tolosa: After Pope Innocent III calls European knights to a crusade, the forces of kings Alfonso VIII of Castile, Sancho VII of Navarre, Peter II of Aragon and Afonso II of Portugal defeat those of the Berber Muslim leader Almohad, thus marking a significant turning point in the Reconquista and in the medieval history of Spain.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Las_Navas_de_Tolosa)

⚓ 1377 – King Richard II of England is crowned.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_II_of_England)

🚀 1536 – Jacques Cartier, navigator and explorer, returns home to St. Malo after claiming Stadacona (Quebec), Hochelaga (Montreal) and the River of Canada (St. Lawrence River) region for France.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Cartier)

💼 1661 – The first banknotes in Europe are issued by the Swedish bank Stockholms Banco.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banknote)

🎖️ 1769 – Father Junípero Serra founds California's first mission, Mission San Diego de Alcalá. Over the following decades, it evolves into the city of San Diego, California.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jun%C3%ADpero_Serra)

📡 1945 – Manhattan Project: The Atomic Age begins when the United States successfully detonates a plutonium-based test nuclear weapon near Alamogordo, New Mexico.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan_Project)

🏙️ 1950 – Uruguay beats hosts Brazil 2–1 to win the World Cup in a match dubbed as the Maracanazo.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uruguay_national_football_team)

⚙️ 1994 – The comet Shoemaker-Levy 9 is destroyed in a head-on collision with Jupiter.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comet_Shoemaker%E2%80%93Levy_9)

💥 1950 – Chaplain–Medic massacre: American POWs are massacred by North Korean Army.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaplain%E2%80%93Medic_massacre)

🕊️ 1941 – Joe DiMaggio hits safely for the 56th consecutive game, a streak that still stands as an MLB record.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_DiMaggio)

⚔️ 1915 – Henry James becomes a British citizen to highlight his commitment to Britain during the first World War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_James)

🏰 1945 – World War II: The heavy cruiser USS Indianapolis leaves San Francisco with parts for the atomic bomb "Little Boy" bound for Tinian Island.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_cruiser)
