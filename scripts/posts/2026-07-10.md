# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 138 – Emperor Hadrian of Rome dies of heart failure at his residence on the bay of Naples, Baiae; he is buried at Rome in the Tomb of Hadrian beside his late wife, Vibia Sabina.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_138)

🏰 420 – Having usurped the throne of Emperor Gong of Jin, Liu Yu proclaims himself Emperor of the Liu Song dynasty.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_420)

📜 518 – Justin I is crowned Byzantine Emperor by the patriarch of Constantinople the day after the death of emperor Anastasius.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_I)

🛡️ 645 – Isshi Incident: Prince Naka-no-Ōe and Fujiwara no Kamatari assassinate Soga no Iruka during a coup d'état at the imperial palace.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isshi_incident)

⚓ 988 – The Norse King Glúniairn recognises Máel Sechnaill mac Domnaill, High King of Ireland, and agrees to pay taxes and accept Brehon Law; the event is considered to be the founding of the city of Dublin.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gl%C3%BAniairn)

🚀 1212 – The most severe of several early fires of London burns most of the city to the ground.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_fires_of_London)

💼 1460 – Richard Neville, 16th Earl of Warwick, defeats the king's Lancastrian forces and takes King Henry VI prisoner in the Battle of Northampton.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Neville%2C_16th_Earl_of_Warwick)

🎖️ 1512 – The Spanish conquest of Iberian Navarre commences with the capture of Goizueta.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_conquest_of_Iberian_Navarre)

📡 1645 – English Civil War: The Battle of Langport takes place.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Civil_War)

🏙️ 1778 – American Revolution: Louis XVI of France declares war on the Kingdom of Great Britain.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution)

⚙️ 1883 – War of the Pacific: Chileans led by Alejandro Gorostiaga defeat Andrés Avelino Cáceres's Peruvian army at the Battle of Huamachuco, hastening the end of the war.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_Pacific)

💥 1941 – Jedwabne pogrom: Massacre of Polish Jews living in and near the village of Jedwabne.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jedwabne_pogrom)

🕊️ 1850 – U.S. President Millard Fillmore is sworn in, a day after becoming president upon Zachary Taylor's death.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millard_Fillmore)

⚔️ 2000 – EADS, the world's second-largest aerospace group is formed by the merger of Aérospatiale-Matra, DASA, and CASA.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus)

🏰 1877 – The then-villa of Mayagüez, Puerto Rico, formally receives its city charter from the Royal Crown of Spain.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villa)
