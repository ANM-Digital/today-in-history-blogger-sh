# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 306 – Constantine I is proclaimed Roman emperor by his troops.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine_the_Great)

🏰 677 – Climax of the Siege of Thessalonica by the Slavs in a three-day assault on the city walls.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Thessalonica_(676%E2%80%93678))

📜 864 – The Edict of Pistres of Charles the Bald orders defensive measures against the Vikings.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edict_of_P%C3%AEtres)

🛡️ 918 – Wang Geon becomes King of Goryeo after overthrowing Gung Ye in a coup the previous day. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taejo_of_Goryeo)

⚓ 1137 – Eleanor of Aquitaine marries Prince Louis, later King Louis VII of France, at the Cathedral of Saint-André in Bordeaux.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eleanor_of_Aquitaine)

🚀 1261 – The city of Constantinople is recaptured by Nicaean forces under the command of Alexios Strategopoulos, re-establishing the Byzantine Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantinople)

💼 1441 – King Henry VI of England lays the foundation stone of King's College Chapel, Cambridge.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_VI_of_England)

🎖️ 1536 – Sebastián de Belalcázar on his search of El Dorado founds the city of Santiago de Cali.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebasti%C3%A1n_de_Belalc%C3%A1zar)

📡 1603 – James VI and I and Anne of Denmark are crowned in Westminster Abbey.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_VI_and_I)

🏙️ 1718 – At the behest of Tsar Peter the Great, the construction of Kadriorg Palace, dedicated to his wife Catherine, begins in Tallinn.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_the_Great)

⚙️ 2001 – Phoolan Devi, a serving Member of Parliament, was assassinated by shooting in New Delhi, India.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoolan_Devi)

💥 1824 – Costa Rica annexes Guanacaste from Nicaragua.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Rica)

🕊️ 1983 – Black July: Thirty-seven Tamil political prisoners at the Welikada high security prison in Colombo are massacred by the fellow Sinhalese prisoners.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_July)

⚔️ 1996 – In a military coup in Burundi, Pierre Buyoya deposes Sylvestre Ntibantunganya.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996_Burundian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat)

🏰 1943 – World War II: Benito Mussolini is forced out of office by the King (encouraged by the Grand Council of Fascism) and is replaced by Pietro Badoglio.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II)
