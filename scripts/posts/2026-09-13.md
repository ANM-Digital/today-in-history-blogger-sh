# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -585 – Lucius Tarquinius Priscus, king of Rome, celebrates a triumph for his victories over the Sabines, and the surrender of Collatia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucius_Tarquinius_Priscus)

🏰 531 – Khosrow I becomes king of the Sassanid Empire following the death of his father Kavad I. He starts negotiations with the Byzantine Empire to end the Iberian War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khosrow_I)

📜 604 – Consecration of pope Sabinian following the death of pope Gregory I earlier that year.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consecration_in_Christianity)

🛡️ 1229 – Ögedei Khan is proclaimed Khagan of the Mongol Empire in Kodoe Aral, Khentii: Mongolia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%96gedei_Khan)

⚓ 1437 – Battle of Tangier: a Portuguese expeditionary force initiates a failed attempt to seize the Moroccan citadel of Tangier.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Tangier_(1437))

🚀 1500 – Pedro Álvares Cabral and his Portuguese fleet arrive in Calicut, having crossed both the Atlantic and Indian Ocean.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_%C3%81lvares_Cabral)

💼 1609 – Henry Hudson reaches the river that would later be named after him – the Hudson River.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Hudson)

🎖️ 1743 – Great Britain, Austria and the Kingdom of Sardinia sign the Treaty of Worms.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Sardinia)

📡 1906 – The Santos-Dumont 14-bis makes a short hop, the first flight of a fixed-wing aircraft in Europe.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santos-Dumont_14-bis)

🏙️ 2007 – The McLaren F1 team are found guilty of possessing confidential information from the Ferrari team, fined $100 million, and excluded from the constructors' championship standings.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McLaren)

⚙️ 1862 – American Civil War: Union soldiers find a copy of Robert E. Lee's battle plans in a field outside Frederick, Maryland. It is the prelude to the Battle of Antietam.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War)

💥 1933 – Elizabeth McCombs becomes the first woman elected to the New Zealand Parliament.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_McCombs)

🕊️ 2013 – Taliban insurgents attack the United States consulate in Herat, Afghanistan, with two members of the Afghan National Police reported dead and about 20 civilians injured.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taliban)

⚔️ 1899 – Mackinder, Ollier and Brocherel make the first ascent of Batian (5,199 m – 17,058 ft), the highest peak of Mount Kenya.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halford_Mackinder)

🏰 1953 – Nikita Khrushchev is appointed General Secretary of the Communist Party of the Soviet Union.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Khrushchev)
