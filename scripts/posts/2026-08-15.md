# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 636 – Arab–Byzantine wars: The Battle of Yarmouk between the Byzantine Empire and the Rashidun Caliphate begins.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab%E2%80%93Byzantine_wars)

🏰 717 – Arab–Byzantine wars: Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik begins the Second Arab Siege of Constantinople, which will last for nearly a year.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maslama_ibn_Abd_al-Malik)

📜 805 – Noble Erchana of Dahauua grants the Bavarian town of Dachau to the Diocese of Freising.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavaria)

🛡️ 927 – The Saracens conquer and destroy Taranto.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saracen)

⚓ 1018 – Byzantine general Eustathios Daphnomeles blinds and captures Ibatzes of Bulgaria by a ruse, thereby ending Bulgarian resistance against Emperor Basil II's conquest of Bulgaria.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eustathios_Daphnomeles)

🚀 1136 – Seljuk Sultan Ghiyath ad-Din Mas'ud enters Baghdad after he besieged it for fifty days and the flight of Abbasid caliph al-Rashid Billah the day before.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghiyath_ad-Din_Mas'ud)

💼 1209 – Albigensian Crusade: The Cathar stronghold of Carcassonne falls to the crusaders.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albigensian_Crusade)

🎖️ 1310 – The city of Rhodes surrenders to the forces of the Knights of St. John, completing their conquest of Rhodes. The knights establish their headquarters on the island and rename themselves the Knights of Rhodes.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodes_(city))

📡 1430 – Francesco Sforza, lord of Milan, conquers Lucca.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_I_Sforza)

🏙️ 1511 – Afonso de Albuquerque of Portugal conquers Malacca, the capital of the Malacca Sultanate.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afonso_de_Albuquerque)

⚙️ 1645 – A Royalist army under the Marquess of Montrose defeats a Covenanter army in the battle of Kilsyth.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Graham%2C_1st_Marquess_of_Montrose)

💥 1760 – Seven Years' War: Battle of Liegnitz: Frederick the Great's victory over the Austrians under Ernst Gideon von Laudon.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Years'_War)

🕊️ 1963 – Execution of Henry John Burnett, the last man to be hanged in Scotland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_John_Burnett)

⚔️ 1941 – Corporal Josef Jakobs is executed by firing squad for espionage at the Tower of London at 07:12, making him the last person to be executed at the Tower.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Jakobs)

🏰 1984 – The Kurdistan Workers' Party in Turkey starts a campaign of armed attacks upon the Turkish Armed Forces with an attack on police and gendarmerie bases in Şemdinli and Eruh.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdistan_Workers'_Party)
