# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 180 – Twelve inhabitants of Scillium (near Kasserine, modern-day Tunisia) in North Africa are executed for being Christians. This is the earliest record of Christianity in that part of the world.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scillitan_Martyrs)

🏰 561 – Consecration of Pope John III following the death of Pope Pelagius I earlier that year.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_John_III)

📜 1048 – Damasus II is elected pope, and dies 23 days later.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Damasus_II)

🛡️ 1203 – The Fourth Crusade assaults Constantinople. The Byzantine emperor Alexios III Angelos flees from his capital into exile.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Crusade)

⚓ 1402 – Zhu Di, better known by his era name as the Yongle Emperor, assumes the throne over the Ming dynasty of China.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regnal_name)

🚀 1717 – King George I of Great Britain sails down the River Thames with a barge of 50 musicians, where George Frideric Handel's Water Music is premiered.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_I_of_Great_Britain)

💼 1997 – After takeoff from Husein Sastranegara International Airport, Sempati Air Flight 304 crashes into a residential neighborhood in Bandung, killing 28 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Husein_Sastranegara_Airport)

🎖️ 2014 – Eric Garner is killed by police officer Daniel Pantaleo in New York City, after the latter put him in a prohibited chokehold while arresting him.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Eric_Garner)

📡 2006 – The 7.7 Mw Pangandaran tsunami earthquake severely affects the Indonesian island of Java, killing 668 people, and leaving more than 9,000 injured.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Pangandaran_earthquake_and_tsunami)

🏙️ 1998 – The 7.0 Mw Papua New Guinea earthquake triggers a tsunami that destroys ten villages in Papua New Guinea, killing up to 2,700 people, and leaving several thousand injured.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Papua_New_Guinea_earthquake)

⚙️ 2014 – Malaysia Airlines Flight 17, a Boeing 777, crashes near the border of Ukraine and Russia after being shot down. All 298 people on board are killed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysia_Airlines_Flight_17)

💥 2014 – A French regional train on the Pau-Bayonne line crashes into a high-speed train near the town of Denguin, resulting in at least 25 injuries.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_express_r%C3%A9gional)

🕊️ 2015 – At least 120 people are killed and 130 injured by a suicide bombing in Diyala Governorate, Iraq.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Khan_Bani_Saad_bombing)

⚔️ 1954 – First Indochina War: Viet Minh troops successfully ambush the armoured French column 'G.M. 42' in the Battle of Chu Dreh Pass in the Central Highlands. It is the last battle of the war.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Indochina_War)

🏰 1918 – Tsar Nicholas II of Russia and his immediate family and retainers are executed by Bolshevik Chekists at the Ipatiev House in Yekaterinburg, Russia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_II)
