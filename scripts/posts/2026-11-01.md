# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 365 – Roman Emperor Valentinian I learns the Alemanni have crossed the Rhine and invaded Gaul.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_365)

🏰 996 – Emperor Otto III issues a deed to Gottschalk, Bishop of Freising, which is the oldest known document using the name Ostarrîchi (Austria in Old High German).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_III%2C_Holy_Roman_Emperor)

📜 1141 – Empress Matilda's reign as "Lady of the English" ends as Stephen of Blois regains the title of "King of England".. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empress_Matilda)

🛡️ 1214 – The port city of Sinope surrenders to the Seljuq Turks.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinop%2C_Turkey)

⚓ 1348 – The anti-royalist Union of Valencia attacks the Jews of Murviedro on the pretext that they are serfs of the King of Valencia and thus "royalists".. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_Valencia)

🚀 1463 – David of Trebizond, the last emperor of Trebizond, is executed with various members of his family by the Ottomans near the Golden Gate of Constantinople.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_of_Trebizond)

💼 1503 – Cardinal Giuliano della Rovere is elected Pope and takes the name Julius II.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_popes)

🎖️ 1612 – During the Time of Troubles, Polish troops are expelled from Moscow's Kitay-gorod by Russian troops under the command of Dmitry Pozharsky (22 October O.S.).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_of_Troubles)

📡 1755 – In Portugal, Lisbon is totally devastated by a massive earthquake and tsunami, killing an estimated 40,000 to 60,000 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisbon)

🏙️ 1982 – Honda becomes the first Asian automobile company to produce cars in the United States with the opening of its factory in Marysville, Ohio; a Honda Accord is the first car produced there.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda)

⚙️ 1956 – The Springhill mining disaster in Springhill, Nova Scotia kills 39 miners; 88 are rescued.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springhill_mining_disasters)

💥 1905 – Lahti, a city in Finland, is granted city rights by Tsar Nicholas II of Russia, the last Grand Duke of Finland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahti)

🕊️ 2011 – Mario Draghi succeeds Jean-Claude Trichet and becomes the third president of the European Central Bank.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Draghi)

⚔️ 1968 – The Motion Picture Association of America's film rating system is officially introduced, originating with the ratings G, M, R, and X.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association)

🏰 1948 – Athenagoras I, Ecumenical Patriarch of Constantinople, is enthroned.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athenagoras_I_of_Constantinople)
