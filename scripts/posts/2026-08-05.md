# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 25 – Guangwu claims the throne as Emperor of China, restoring the Han dynasty after the collapse of the short-lived Xin dynasty.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20s)

🏰 642 – Battle of Maserfield: Penda of Mercia defeats and kills Oswald of Northumbria.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Maserfield)

📜 910 – The last major Danish army to raid England for nearly a century is defeated at the Battle of Tettenhall by the allied forces of Mercia and Wessex, led by King Edward the Elder and Æthelred, Lord of the Mercians.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_England)

🛡️ 1068 – Byzantine–Norman wars: Italo-Normans begin a nearly-three-year siege of Bari.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine%E2%80%93Norman_wars)

⚓ 1100 – Henry I is crowned King of England in Westminster Abbey.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_I_of_England)

🚀 1278 – Spanish Reconquista: the forces of the Kingdom of Castile initiate the ultimately futile Siege of Algeciras against the Emirate of Granada.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reconquista)

💼 1305 – First Scottish War of Independence: Sir John Stewart of Menteith, the pro-English Sheriff of Dumbarton, successfully manages to capture Sir William Wallace of Scotland, leading to Wallace's subsequent execution by hanging, evisceration, drawing and quartering, and beheading 18 days later.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_War_of_Scottish_Independence)

🎖️ 1460 – The Kingdom of Scotland captures Roxburgh, one of the last English strongholds in Scotland, following a siege.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capture_of_Roxburgh_(1460))

📡 1506 – The Grand Duchy of Lithuania defeats the Crimean Khanate in the Battle of Kletsk.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duchy_of_Lithuania)

🏙️ 1600 – The Gowrie Conspiracy against King James VI of Scotland (later to become King James I of England) takes place at Gowrie House (Perth, Scotland).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Ruthven%2C_3rd_Earl_of_Gowrie)

⚙️ 1716 – Austro-Turkish War (1716–1718):The Austrians defeat the Ottomans in the Battle of Petrovaradin. One-fifth of a Turkish army and the Grand Vizier are killed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austro-Turkish_War_(1716%E2%80%931718))

💥 2012 – The Wisconsin Sikh temple shooting took place in Oak Creek, Wisconsin, killing six victims; the perpetrator committed suicide after being wounded by police.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wisconsin_Sikh_temple_shooting)

🕊️ 1864 – American Civil War: The Battle of Mobile Bay begins at Mobile Bay near Mobile, Alabama, Admiral David Farragut leads a Union flotilla through Confederate defenses and seals one of the last major Southern ports.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Mobile_Bay)

⚔️ 1914 – World War I: The guns of Point Nepean fort at Port Phillip Heads in Victoria (Australia) fire across the bows of the Norddeutscher Lloyd steamer SS Pfalz which is attempting to leave the Port of Melbourne in ignorance of the declaration of war and she is detained; this is said to be the first Allied shot of the War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Pfalz_(1913))

🏰 2019 – The revocation of the special status of Jammu and Kashmir (state) occurred and the state was bifurcated into two union territories (Jammu and Kashmir (union territory) and Ladakh).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revocation_of_the_special_status_of_Jammu_and_Kashmir)
