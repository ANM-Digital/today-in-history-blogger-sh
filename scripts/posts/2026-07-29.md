# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -587 – The Neo-Babylonian Empire sacks Jerusalem and destroys the First Temple.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-Babylonian_Empire)

🏰 238 – The Praetorian guard revolts against emperors Balbinus and Pupienus and arrests them.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/238)

📜 615 – Pakal ascends the throne of Palenque at the age of 12.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%CA%BCinich_Janaab%CA%BC_Pakal)

🛡️ 904 – Sack of Thessalonica: Saracen raiders under Leo of Tripoli sack Thessaloniki, the Byzantine Empire's second-largest city, after a short siege, and plunder it for a week.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sack_of_Thessalonica_(904))

⚓ 1014 – Byzantine–Bulgarian wars: Battle of Kleidion: Byzantine emperor Basil II inflicts a decisive defeat on the Bulgarian army, and his subsequent treatment of 15,000 prisoners reportedly causes Tsar Samuil of Bulgaria to die of a heart attack less than three months later, on October 6.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine%E2%80%93Bulgarian_wars)

🚀 1148 – The Siege of Damascus ends in a decisive crusader defeat and leads to the disintegration of the Second Crusade.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Damascus_(1148))

💼 1565 – The widowed Mary, Queen of Scots marries Henry Stuart, Lord Darnley, Duke of Albany, at Holyrood Palace, Edinburgh, Scotland, in a Catholic ceremony.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary%2C_Queen_of_Scots)

🎖️ 1602 – Elizabeth I visits Harefield Place at Hillingdon and the Harefield Entertainment is performed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_I)

📡 1775 – Founding of the U.S. Army Judge Advocate General's Corps: General George Washington appoints William Tudor as Judge Advocate of the Continental Army.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_Judge_Advocate_General's_Corps)

🏙️ 1818 – French physicist Augustin Fresnel submits his prizewinning "Memoir on the Diffraction of Light", precisely accounting for the limited extent to which light spreads into shadows, and thereby demolishing the oldest objection to the wave theory of light.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustin-Jean_Fresnel)

⚙️ 1967 – Vietnam War: Off the coast of North Vietnam the USS Forrestal catches on fire in the worst U.S. naval disaster since World War II, killing 134.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Vietnam)

💥 1851 – Annibale de Gasparis discovers asteroid 15 Eunomia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annibale_de_Gasparis)

🕊️ 1973 – Greeks vote to abolish the monarchy, beginning the first period of the Metapolitefsi.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1973_Greek_republic_referendum)

⚔️ 1957 – Tonight Starring Jack Paar premieres on NBC with Jack Paar beginning the modern day talk show.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonight_Starring_Jack_Paar)

🏰 1981 – After impeachment on June 21, Abolhassan Banisadr flees with Massoud Rajavi to Paris, in an Iranian Air Force Boeing 707, piloted by Colonel Behzad Moezzi, to form the National Council of Resistance of Iran.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abolhassan_Banisadr)
