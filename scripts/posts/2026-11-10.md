# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 474 – Emperor Leo II dies after a reign of ten months. He is succeeded by his father Zeno, who becomes sole ruler of the Byzantine Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_II_(emperor))

🏰 937 – Ten Kingdoms: Li Bian usurps the throne and deposes Emperor Yang Pu. The Wu State is replaced by Li (now called "Xu Zhigao"), who becomes the first ruler of Southern Tang.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_Dynasties_and_Ten_Kingdoms_period)

📜 1160 – The assassination of Maio of Bari, chancelor of the kingdom of Sicily, leads to a crisis and the arrest of king William I of Sicily.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maio_of_Bari)

🛡️ 1202 – Fourth Crusade: Despite letters from Pope Innocent III forbidding it and threatening excommunication, Catholic crusaders begin a siege of Zara (now Zadar, Croatia).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Crusade)

⚓ 1444 – Battle of Varna: The crusading forces of King Władysław III of Poland (aka Ulaszlo I of Hungary and Władysław III of Varna) are defeated by the Turks under Sultan Murad II and Władysław is killed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Varna)

🚀 1599 – Åbo Bloodbath: Fourteen noblemen who opposed Duke Charles are decapitated in the Old Great Square of Turku (Swedish: Åbo) for their involvement in the War against Sigismund.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%85bo_Bloodbath)

💼 1659 – Chattrapati Shivaji Maharaj, Maratha King kills Afzal Khan, Adilshahi in the battle popularly known as Battle of Pratapgarh.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shivaji)

🎖️ 1702 – English colonists under the command of James Moore besiege Spanish St. Augustine during Queen Anne's War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Moore_Sr.)

📡 1979 – A 106-car Canadian Pacific freight train carrying explosive and poisonous chemicals from Windsor, Ontario, Canada derails in Mississauga, Ontario.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Pacific_Railway)

🏙️ 1985 – A Dassault Falcon 50 and a Piper PA-28 Cherokee collide in mid-air over Fairview, New Jersey, killing six people and injuring eight.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dassault_Falcon_50)

⚙️ 1951 – With the rollout of the North American Numbering Plan, direct-dial coast-to-coast telephone service begins in the United States.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_Numbering_Plan)

💥 1969 – National Educational Television (the predecessor to the Public Broadcasting Service) in the United States debuts Sesame Street.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Educational_Television)

🕊️ 1898 – White supremacists seized power and massacred black Americans during the Wilmington massacre, the only instance of a municipal government being overthrown in United States history.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_supremacy)

⚔️ 1983 – Bill Gates introduces Windows 1.0.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Gates)

🏰 2009 – Ships of the South and North Korean navies skirmish off Daecheong Island in the Yellow Sea.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daecheong_incident)
