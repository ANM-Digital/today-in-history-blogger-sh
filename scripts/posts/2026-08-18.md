# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 684 – Battle of Marj Rahit: Umayyad partisans defeat the supporters of Ibn al-Zubayr and cement Umayyad control of Syria.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Marj_Rahit_(684))

🏰 707 – Princess Abe accedes to the imperial Japanese throne as Empress Genmei.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_707)

📜 1136 – Following the surrender of Baghdad, Seljuk Sultan Mas'ud replaces Abbasid caliph Al-Rashid Billah with his uncle Al-Muqtafi.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Baghdad_(1136))

🛡️ 1304 – The Battle of Mons-en-Pévèle is fought to a draw between the French army and the Flemish militias.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Mons-en-P%C3%A9v%C3%A8le)

⚓ 1487 – The Siege of Málaga ends with the taking of the city by Castilian and Aragonese forces.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_M%C3%A1laga_(1487))

🚀 1572 – The Huguenot King Henry III of Navarre marries the Catholic Margaret of Valois, ostensibly to reconcile the feuding Protestants and Catholics of France.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huguenots)

💼 1612 – The trials of the Pendle witches and Samlesbury witches, one of England's most famous witch trials, begin at the Lancaster Assizes.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pendle_witches)

🎖️ 1721 – The city of Shamakhi in Safavid Shirvan is sacked.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shamakhi)

📡 1966 – Vietnam War: The Battle of Long Tan ensues after a patrol from the 6th Battalion, Royal Australian Regiment clashes with a Viet Cong force in Phước Tuy Province.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Long_Tan)

🏙️ 1976 – The Korean axe murder incident in Panmunjom results in the deaths of two US Army officers.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panmunjom_axe_murder_incident)

⚙️ 1938 – The Thousand Islands Bridge, connecting New York, United States, with Ontario, Canada, over the Saint Lawrence River, is dedicated by U.S. President Franklin D. Roosevelt.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thousand_Islands_Bridge)

💥 1940 – World War II: The Hardest Day air battle, part of the Battle of Britain, takes place. At that point, it is the largest aerial engagement in history with heavy losses sustained on both sides.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II)

🕊️ 1983 – Hurricane Alicia hits the Texas coast, killing 21 people and causing over US$1 billion in damage (1983 dollars).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Alicia)

⚔️ 1917 – A Great Fire in Thessaloniki, Greece, destroys 32% of the city leaving 70,000 individuals homeless.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Thessaloniki_Fire_of_1917)

🏰 2003 – One-year-old Zachary Turner is murdered in Newfoundland by his mother, who was awarded custody despite facing trial for the murder of Zachary's father. The case was documented in the film Dear Zachary and led to reform of Canada's bail laws.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murders_of_Andrew_Bagby_and_Zachary_Turner)
