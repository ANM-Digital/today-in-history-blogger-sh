# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 284 – Diocletian is chosen as Roman emperor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocletian)

🏰 762 – During the An Shi Rebellion, the Tang dynasty, with the help of Huihe tribe, recaptures Luoyang from the rebels.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An_Lushan_rebellion)

📜 1194 – Palermo is conquered by Henry VI, Holy Roman Emperor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palermo)

🛡️ 1272 – Edward I becomes king of England following the death of his father Henry III of England.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_I)

⚓ 1407 – John the Fearless, Duke of Burgundy, and Louis of Valois, Duke of Orléans, agree to a truce, but Burgundy would kill Orléans three days later.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_the_Fearless)

🚀 1695 – Zumbi, the last of the leaders of Quilombo dos Palmares in early Brazil, is executed by the forces of Portuguese bandeirante Domingos Jorge Velho.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zumbi)

💼 1710 – Begin of the Russo-Turkish War of 1710–1713, after tensions between the two states escalate as the Ottomans provide shelter to King Charles XII of Sweden in their fortress of Bender.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo-Turkish_War_(1710%E2%80%931713))

🎖️ 1900 – The French actress Sarah Bernhardt receives the press at the Savoy Hotel in New York at the outset of her first visit since 1896. She talked about her impending tour with a troupe of more than 50 performers and her plans to play the title role in Hamlet.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Bernhardt)

📡 2016 – Jimmie Johnson wins his seventh NASCAR Cup Series championship to tie Richard Petty and Dale Earnhardt for the most all-time.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmie_Johnson)

🏙️ 1979 – Grand Mosque seizure: About 200 Sunni Muslims revolt in Saudi Arabia at the site of the Kaaba in Mecca during the pilgrimage and take about 6,000 hostages. The Saudi government receives help from French special forces to put down the uprising.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Mosque_seizure)

⚙️ 1959 – The Declaration of the Rights of the Child is adopted by the United Nations.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Declaration_of_the_Rights_of_the_Child)

💥 1974 – The United States Department of Justice files its final anti-trust suit against AT&T Corporation. This suit later leads to the breakup of AT&T and its Bell System.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Justice)

🕊️ 2003 – After the November 15 bombings, a second day of the 2003 Istanbul bombings occurs in Istanbul, Turkey, destroying the Turkish head office of HSBC Bank AS and the British consulate.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/November_15)

⚔️ 1969 – Vietnam War: The Plain Dealer (Cleveland, Ohio) publishes explicit photographs of dead villagers from the My Lai Massacre in Vietnam.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_War)

🏰 1805 – Beethoven's only opera, Fidelio, premieres in Vienna.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_van_Beethoven)
