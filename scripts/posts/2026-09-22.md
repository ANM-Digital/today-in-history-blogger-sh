# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 904 – The warlord Zhu Quanzhong kills Emperor Zhaozong, the penultimate emperor of the Tang dynasty, after seizing control of the imperial government.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhu_Wen)

🏰 1236 – The Samogitians defeat the Livonian Brothers of the Sword in the Battle of Saule.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Saule)

📜 1359 – An Aragonese cavalry force defeats a superior Castilian cavalry force in the Battle of Araviana during the War of the Two Peters.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Araviana)

🛡️ 1499 – The Treaty of Basel concludes the Swabian War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Basel_(1499))

⚓ 1586 – Eighty Years' War: A Spanish force led by the Marquis del Vasto successfully fights its way past a joint English/Dutch ambush in the Battle of Zutphen.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eighty_Years'_War)

🚀 1692 – Martha Corey, Mary Eastey, Alice Parker, Mary Parker, Ann Pudeator, Wilmot Redd, Margaret Scott, and Samuel Wardwell are hanged, the last of those to be executed in the Salem witch trials.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martha_Corey)

💼 1711 – The first attacks of the Tuscarora War begin in present-day North Carolina.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuscarora_War)

🎖️ 1857 – The Russian warship Lefort capsizes and sinks during a storm in the Gulf of Finland, killing all 826 aboard.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_ship_of_the_line_Lefort)

📡 1980 – Iraq invades Iran, sparking the nearly eight year Iran–Iraq War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_invasion_of_Iran)

🏙️ 1991 – The Dead Sea Scrolls are made available to the public for the first time.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Sea_Scrolls)

⚙️ 1934 – The Gresford disaster in Wales kills 266 miners and rescuers.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gresford_disaster)

💥 1862 – A preliminary version of the Emancipation Proclamation is released by Abraham Lincoln.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emancipation_Proclamation)

🕊️ 1948 – Israeli-Palestine conflict: The All-Palestine Government is established by the Arab League.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli%E2%80%93Palestinian_conflict)

⚔️ 1919 – The steel strike of 1919, led by the Amalgamated Association of Iron and Steel Workers, begins in Pennsylvania before spreading across the United States.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1919_General_Steel_Strike)

🏰 2024 – Anura Kumara Dissanayake is elected as the 9th President Of Sri Lanka.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anura_Kumara_Dissanayake)
