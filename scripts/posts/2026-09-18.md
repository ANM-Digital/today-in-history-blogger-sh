# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 96 – Emperor Domitian is assassinated as a result of a plot by his wife Domitia and two Praetorian prefects. Nerva is then proclaimed as his successor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_96)

🏰 324 – Constantine the Great decisively defeats Licinius in the Battle of Chrysopolis, establishing Constantine's sole control over the Roman Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine_the_Great)

📜 1048 – Battle of Kapetron between a combined Byzantine-Georgian army and a Seljuq army.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kapetron)

🛡️ 1180 – Philip Augustus becomes king of France at the age of fifteen.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_II_of_France)

⚓ 1454 – Thirteen Years' War: In the Battle of Chojnice, the Polish army is defeated by the Teutonic knights.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteen_Years'_War_(1454%E2%80%931466))

🚀 1544 – The expedition of Juan Bautista Pastene makes landfall in San Pedro Bay, southern Chile, claiming the territory for Spain.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Bautista_Pastene)

💼 1618 – The twelfth baktun in the Mesoamerican Long Count calendar begins.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baktun)

🎖️ 1714 – George I arrives in Great Britain after becoming king on August 1.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_I_of_Great_Britain)

📡 1981 – The Assemblée Nationale votes to abolish capital punishment in France.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_(France))

🏙️ 1837 – Tiffany & Co. (first named Tiffany & Young) is founded by Charles Lewis Tiffany and Teddy Young in New York City. The store is called a "stationery and fancy goods emporium".. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiffany_%26_Co.)

⚙️ 1863 – American Civil War: The Battle of Chickamauga begins between Confederate and Union forces. It involves the second highest amount of casualties for any American Civil War battle apart from Gettysburg.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War)

💥 1984 – Joe Kittinger completes the first solo balloon crossing of the Atlantic.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Kittinger)

🕊️ 1934 – The Soviet Union is admitted to the League of Nations.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union)

⚔️ 1992 – An explosion rocks Giant Mine at the height of a labor dispute, killing nine replacement workers in Yellowknife, Canada.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Mine)

🏰 2014 – Scotland votes against independence from the United Kingdom, by 55% to 45%.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Scottish_independence_referendum)
