# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -48 – Caesar's Civil War: Battle of Pharsalus: Julius Caesar decisively defeats Pompey at Pharsalus and Pompey flees to Egypt.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caesar's_civil_war)

🏰 378 – Gothic War: Battle of Adrianople: A large Roman army led by Emperor Valens is defeated by the Visigoths. Valens is killed along with over half of his army.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_War_(376%E2%80%93382))

📜 1173 – Construction of the campanile of the Cathedral of Pisa (now known as the Leaning Tower of Pisa) begins; it will take two centuries to complete.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_tower)

🛡️ 1329 – Quilon, the first Indian Christian Diocese, is erected by Pope John XXII; the French-born Jordanus is appointed the first Bishop.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_Quilon)

⚓ 1500 – Ottoman–Venetian War (1499–1503): The Ottomans capture Methoni, Messenia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman%E2%80%93Venetian_War_(1499%E2%80%931503))

🚀 1610 – The First Anglo-Powhatan War begins in colonial Virginia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Powhatan_Wars)

💼 1945 – The Red Army invades Japanese-occupied Manchuria.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Army)

🎖️ 1942 – Dmitri Shostakovich's 7th symphony premiers in a besieged Leningrad.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Shostakovich)

📡 1842 – The Webster–Ashburton Treaty is signed, establishing the United States–Canada border east of the Rocky Mountains.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Webster%E2%80%93Ashburton_Treaty)

🏙️ 1830 – Louis Philippe becomes the king of the French following abdication of Charles X.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Philippe_I)

⚙️ 2006 – At least 21 suspected terrorists are arrested in the 2006 transatlantic aircraft plot that happened in the United Kingdom. The arrests are made in London, Birmingham, and High Wycombe in an overnight operation.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_transatlantic_aircraft_plot)

💥 1999 – Russian president Boris Yeltsin fires his prime minister, Sergei Stepashin, and for the fourth time fires his entire cabinet.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Yeltsin)

🕊️ 1897 – The first International Congress of Mathematicians is held in Zürich, Switzerland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congress_of_Mathematicians)

⚔️ 2024 – Voepass Linhas Aéreas Flight 2283 crashes near Vinhedo, São Paulo, killing all 62 people on board.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voepass_Flight_2283)

🏰 1936 – Summer Olympics: Jesse Owens wins his fourth gold medal at the games.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1936_Summer_Olympics)
