# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 512 – Following Byzantine emperor Anastasius' deposition of Chalcedonian patriarchs and attempts to make Monophysite changes to liturgy, riots break out in Constantinople with a mob trying to proclaim Areobindus as emperor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Byzantine_emperors)

🏰 1354 – War of the Straits: The Genoese fleet under Paganino Doria defeats and captures the entire Venetian fleet under Niccolò Pisani at the Battle of Sapienza.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_Straits)

📜 1429 – Armagnac–Burgundian Civil War: Joan of Arc liberates Saint-Pierre-le-Moûtier.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armagnac%E2%80%93Burgundian_Civil_War)

🛡️ 1501 – Catherine of Aragon (later Henry VIII's first wife) meets Arthur, Prince of Wales, Henry VIII's older brother – they would later marry.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_of_Aragon)

⚓ 1677 – The future Mary II marries William, Prince of Orange; they later jointly reign over England, Scotland, and Ireland as William and Mary.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_II)

🚀 1737 – The Teatro di San Carlo, the oldest working opera house in Europe, is inaugurated in Naples, Italy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teatro_di_San_Carlo)

💼 1921 – The Saalschutz Abteilung (hall defense detachment) of the Nazi Party is renamed the Sturmabteilung (storm detachment) after a large riot in Munich.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Party)

🎖️ 1967 – Iberia Flight 062 crashes in Blackdown, West Sussex, killing all 37 people on board including British actress June Thorburn.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberia_Flight_062)

📡 1852 – Camillo Benso, Count of Cavour, becomes the prime minister of Piedmont-Sardinia, which soon expands to become Italy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camillo_Benso%2C_Count_of_Cavour)

🏙️ 1995 – Israel–Palestinian conflict: Israeli prime minister Yitzhak Rabin is assassinated by Yigal Amir, an extremist Israeli.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli%E2%80%93Palestinian_conflict)

⚙️ 1979 – Iran hostage crisis: A group of Iranian college students overruns the U.S. embassy in Tehran and takes 90 hostages.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_hostage_crisis)

💥 2002 – Chinese authorities arrest cyber-dissident He Depu for signing a pro-democracy letter to the 16th Communist Party Congress.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/He_Depu)

🕊️ 1864 – American Civil War: Confederate troops bombard a Union supply base and destroy millions of dollars in materiel at the Battle of Johnsonville.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War)

⚔️ 1942 – World War II: Disobeying a direct order by Adolf Hitler, General Field Marshal Erwin Rommel begins a retreat of his forces after a costly defeat during the Second Battle of El Alamein. The retreat would ultimately last five months.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_Hitler)

🏰 1847 – Sir James Young Simpson, a Scottish medical doctor, discovers the anaesthetic properties of chloroform.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Young_Simpson)
