# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -70 – The trial against Gaius Verres for corruption is opened, with Marcus Tullius Cicero as prosecutor and renowned orator Quintus Hortensius as defending lawyer.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaius_Verres)

🏰 598 – Goguryeo-Sui War: In response to a Goguryeo (Korean) incursion into Liaoxi, Emperor Wéndi of Sui orders his youngest son, Yang Liang (assisted by the co-prime minister Gao Jiong), to conquer Goguryeo during the Manchurian rainy season, with a Chinese army and navy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goguryeo%E2%80%93Sui_War)

📜 1265 – Second Barons' War: Battle of Evesham: The army of Prince Edward (the future king Edward I of England) defeats the forces of rebellious barons led by Simon de Montfort, 6th Earl of Leicester, killing de Montfort and many of his allies.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Barons'_War)

🛡️ 1320 – The so-called Black Parliament at Scone tries William II de Soules and other nobles who had plotted against Robert I of Scotland and intended to place William on the Scottish throne.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scone_Palace)

⚓ 1578 – Battle of Al Kasr al Kebir: The Moroccans defeat the Portuguese but both the Moroccan king Abd al-Malik and the Portuguese king Sebastian die. Ahmad al-Mansur follows Abd al-Malik as sultan of Morocco while the death of Sebastian leads to a succession crisis in Portugal.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Alc%C3%A1cer_Quibir)

🚀 1693 – Date traditionally ascribed to Dom Perignon's invention of champagne; it is not clear whether he actually invented champagne, however he has been credited as an innovator who developed the techniques used to perfect sparkling wine.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dom_P%C3%A9rignon_(monk))

💼 1701 – Great Peace of Montreal between New France and First Nations is signed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Peace_of_Montreal)

🎖️ 1974 – A bomb explodes in the Italicus Express train at San Benedetto Val di Sambro, Italy, killing 12 people and wounding 22.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italicus_Express_bombing)

📡 1975 – The Japanese Red Army takes more than 50 hostages at the AIA Building housing several embassies in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. The hostages include the U.S. consul and the Swedish Chargé d'affaires. The gunmen win the release of five imprisoned comrades and fly with them to Libya.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_Red_Army)

🏙️ 1887 – Granny, a sea anemone, died in Edinburgh after nearly 60 years in captivity. Her death was reported in The Scotsman and The New York Times.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granny_(sea_anemone))

⚙️ 1892 – The father and stepmother of Lizzie Borden are found murdered in their Fall River, Massachusetts home. She will be tried and acquitted for the crimes a year later.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lizzie_Borden)

💥 1854 – The Hinomaru is established as the official flag to be flown from Japanese ships.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Japan)

🕊️ 1921 – Bolshevik–Makhnovist conflict: Mikhail Frunze declares victory over the Makhnovshchina.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolshevik%E2%80%93Makhnovist_conflict)

⚔️ 1936 – Prime Minister of Greece Ioannis Metaxas suspends parliament and the Constitution and establishes the 4th of August Regime.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_prime_ministers_of_Greece)

🏰 1977 – U.S. President Jimmy Carter signs legislation creating the United States Department of Energy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_United_States)
