# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 476 – Romulus Augustulus is deposed when Odoacer proclaims himself "King of Italy", thus ending the Western Roman Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romulus_Augustulus)

🏰 626 – Li Shimin, posthumously known as Emperor Taizong of Tang, assumes the throne over the Tang dynasty of China.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Posthumous_name)

📜 929 – Battle of Lenzen: Slavic forces (the Redarii and the Obotrites) are defeated by a Saxon army near the fortified stronghold of Lenzen in Brandenburg.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Lenzen)

🛡️ 1260 – The Sienese Ghibellines, supported by the forces of Manfred, King of Sicily, defeat the Florentine Guelphs at Montaperti.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siena)

⚓ 1479 – The Treaty of Alcáçovas is signed by the Catholic Monarchs of Castile and Aragon on one side and Afonso V and his son, Prince John of Portugal.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Alc%C3%A1%C3%A7ovas)

🚀 1607 – The Flight of the Earls takes place in Ireland (14 September N.S.).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_of_the_Earls)

💼 1746 – The Treaty of Kerden concludes the Ottoman–Persian War (1743–1746).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Kerden)

🎖️ 1985 – The discovery of Buckminsterfullerene, the first fullerene molecule of carbon.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckminsterfullerene)

📡 1939 – World War II: William J. Murphy commands the first Royal Air Force attack on Germany.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II)

🏙️ 1998 – Google is founded by Larry Page and Sergey Brin, two PhD students at Stanford University.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google)

⚙️ 1967 – Vietnam War: Operation Swift begins when U.S. Marines engage the North Vietnamese in battle in the Que Son Valley.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_War)

💥 1886 – American Indian Wars: After almost 30 years of fighting, Apache leader Geronimo, with his remaining warriors,  surrenders to General Nelson Miles in Arizona.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Indian_Wars)

🕊️ 1936 – Spanish Civil War: Largo Caballero forms a war cabinet to direct the republican war effort.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Civil_War)

⚔️ 1950 – Darlington Raceway is the site of the inaugural Southern 500, the first 500-mile NASCAR race.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darlington_Raceway)

🏰 1995 – The 1995 Okinawa rape occurred, when 3 US servicemen abducted and raped a schoolchild.  This would cause widespread protest against the US military presence on the prefecture.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_Okinawa_rape_incident)
