# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 361 – Emperor Constantius II dies of a fever at Mopsuestia in Cilicia; on his deathbed he is baptised and declares his cousin Julian rightful successor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantius_II)

🏰 1090 – The Rouen Riot, an attempt by English king William Rufus to take possession of Rouen, the capital city of his brother Robert, duke of Normandy, fails.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rouen_Riot)

📜 1333 – The River Arno floods causing massive damage in Florence as recorded by the Florentine chronicler Giovanni Villani.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arno)

🛡️ 1468 – Liège is sacked by the troops of Duke Charles the Bold of Burgundy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li%C3%A8ge)

⚓ 1534 – English Parliament passes the first Act of Supremacy, making King Henry VIII head of the Anglican Church, supplanting the pope and the Roman Catholic Church.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acts_of_Supremacy)

🚀 1783 – The American Continental Army is disbanded.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Army)

💼 1943 – World War II: Five hundred aircraft of the U.S. 8th Air Force devastate Wilhelmshaven harbor in Germany.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombing_of_Wilhelmshaven_in_World_War_II)

🎖️ 1986 – Iran–Contra affair: The Lebanese magazine Ash-Shiraa reports that the United States has been secretly selling weapons to Iran in order to secure the release of seven American hostages held by pro-Iranian groups in Lebanon.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Contra_affair)

📡 1969 – Vietnam War: U.S. President Richard Nixon addresses the nation on television and radio, asking the "silent majority" to join him in solidarity on the Vietnam War effort and to support his policies.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Nixon)

🏙️ 1817 – The Bank of Montreal, Canada's oldest chartered bank, opens in Montreal.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_Montreal)

⚙️ 1979 – Greensboro massacre: Five members of the Communist Workers Party are shot dead and seven are wounded by a group of Klansmen and neo-Nazis during a "Death to the Klan" rally in Greensboro, North Carolina, United States.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greensboro_massacre)

💥 1932 – Panagis Tsaldaris becomes the 142nd Prime Minister of Greece.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panagis_Tsaldaris)

🕊️ 2014 – One World Trade Center officially opens in New York City, replacing the Twin Towers after they were destroyed during the September 11 attacks.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_World_Trade_Center)

⚔️ 1898 – France withdraws its troops from Fashoda (now in Sudan), ending the Fashoda Incident.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodok)

🏰 1838 – The Times of India, the world's largest circulated English language daily broadsheet newspaper is founded as The Bombay Times and Journal of Commerce.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times_of_India)
