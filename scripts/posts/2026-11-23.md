# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -534 – Thespis of Icaria becomes the first recorded actor to portray a character on stage.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thespis)

🏰 375 – 16-year-old Gratian is proclaimed augustus following the death of his father Valentinian I, though a couple of days later his half-brother Valentinian is also proclaimed augustus and becomes his co-ruler.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gratian)

📜 602 – Phocas is crowned Byzantine emperor after a rebellion of the Thracian army against reigning emperor Maurice and after Phocas' rival Germanus has been rebuffed by the Greens.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phocas)

🛡️ 1059 – Constantine X Doukas becomes Byzantine emperor, with his son Michael as nominal co-emperor and Michael Psellos as chief minister.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine_X_Doukas)

⚓ 1248 – Conquest of Seville by Christian troops under King Ferdinand III of Castile.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Seville)

🚀 1499 – Seven days after being convicted of treason, Perkin Warbeck, a pretender to the throne of England, is hanged for attempting to escape from the Tower of London; his supporter John Atwater is executed with him.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perkin_Warbeck)

💼 1589 – Marriage of king James VI of Scotland and Anne of Denmark.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_VI_and_I)

🎖️ 1644 – John Milton publishes Areopagitica, a pamphlet decrying censorship.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Milton)

📡 1733 – The start of the 1733 slave insurrection on St. John in what was then the Danish West Indies.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1733_slave_insurrection_on_St._John)

🏙️ 1934 – An Anglo-Ethiopian boundary commission in the Ogaden discovers an Italian garrison at Walwal, well within Ethiopian territory. This leads to the Abyssinia Crisis.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogaden)

⚙️ 1959 – French President Charles de Gaulle declares in a speech in Strasbourg his vision for "Europe, from the Atlantic to the Urals".. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_France)

💥 1944 – World War II: The Lotta Svärd Movement is disbanded under the terms of the armistice treaty in Finland after the Continuation War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lotta_Sv%C3%A4rd)

🕊️ 1808 – French and Poles defeat the Spanish at Battle of Tudela.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Tudela)

⚔️ 1943 – World War II: The Deutsche Opernhaus on Bismarckstraße in the Berlin neighborhood of Charlottenburg is destroyed. It will eventually be rebuilt in 1961 and be called the Deutsche Oper Berlin.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Oper_Berlin)

🏰 1980 – The 6.9 Mw Irpinia earthquake shakes southern Italy with a maximum Mercalli intensity of X (Extreme), killing 2,483–4,900, and injuring 7,700–8,934.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1980_Irpinia_earthquake)
