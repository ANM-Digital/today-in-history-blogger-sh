# 🏛️ Today in History

💼 1725 – Wedding of Louis XV and Maria Leszczyńska.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_XV)

🏙️ 1968 – End of the Congress of Carrara, one of the major 20th century anarchist congresses.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_Carrara)

⚙️ 1943 – World War II: In the Pacific Theater, the 503rd Parachute Infantry Regiment lands and occupies Lae Nadzab Airport, near Lae during the Salamaua–Lae campaign.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_War)

💥 1984 – Western Australia becomes the last Australian state to abolish capital punishment.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Australia)

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waziristan_Accord)

⚔️ 1969 – Mỹ Lai Massacre: U.S. Army Lieutenant William Calley is charged with six specifications of premeditated murder for the death of 109 Vietnamese civilians in My Lai.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Lai_massacre)
