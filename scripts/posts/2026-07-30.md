# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -30 – Mark Antony wins a final cavalry battle against Octavian in the battle of Alexandria, but his infantry is defeated and the next day his fleet and troops start to desert to Octavian.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Antony)

🏰 657 – Election of pope Vitalian following the death of pope Eugene I in the previous month.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Vitalian)

📜 762 – Baghdad is founded.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baghdad)

🛡️ 1183 – The Mor Bar Sauma Monastery is destroyed by a fire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mor_Bar_Sauma_Monastery)

⚓ 1419 – First Defenestration of Prague: A crowd of radical Hussites kill seven members of the Prague city council.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defenestrations_of_Prague)

🚀 1502 – Christopher Columbus lands at Guanaja in the Bay Islands off the coast of Honduras during his fourth voyage.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voyages_of_Christopher_Columbus)

💼 1609 – Beaver Wars: At Ticonderoga (now Crown Point, New York), Samuel de Champlain shoots and kills two Iroquois chiefs on behalf of his native allies.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beaver_Wars)

🎖️ 1729 – Founding of Baltimore, Maryland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore)

📡 1962 – The Trans-Canada Highway, the then-longest national highway in the world, is officially opened.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Canada_Highway)

🏙️ 1971 – Apollo program: On Apollo 15, David Scott and James Irwin in the Apollo Lunar Module Falcon land on the Moon with the first Lunar Rover.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_program)

⚙️ 2006 – An Israeli airstrike kills 28 Lebanese civilians, including 16 children.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Qana_airstrike)

💥 1864 – American Civil War: Battle of the Crater: Union forces attempt to break Confederate lines at Petersburg, Virginia by exploding a large bomb under their trenches.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War)

🕊️ 2024 – A series of landslides occurs in Kerala, India, causing over 420 fatalities.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Wayanad_landslides)

⚔️ 2011 – Marriage of Queen Elizabeth II's eldest granddaughter Zara Phillips to former rugby union footballer Mike Tindall.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zara_Tindall)

🏰 1916 – The Black Tom explosion in New York Harbor kills four and destroys some $20,000,000 worth of military goods.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Tom_explosion)
