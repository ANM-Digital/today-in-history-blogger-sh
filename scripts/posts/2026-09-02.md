💼 1856 – The Tianjing incident takes place in Nanjing, China.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tianjing_Incident)

🎖️ 1957 – President Ngô Đình Diệm of South Vietnam becomes the first foreign head of state to make a state visit to Australia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngo_Dinh_Diem)

📡 1944 – The last execution of a Finn in Finland takes place when soldier Olavi Laiho is executed by shooting in Oulu.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland)

🏙️ 1984 – Seven people are shot and killed and 12 wounded in the Milperra massacre, a shootout between the rival motorcycle gangs Bandidos and Comancheros in Sydney, Australia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milperra_massacre)

⚙️ 1987 – In Moscow, the trial begins for 19-year-old pilot Mathias Rust, who flew his Cessna airplane into Red Square in May.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathias_Rust)

💥 2009 – The Andhra Pradesh, India helicopter crash occurred near Rudrakonda Hill, 40 nautical miles (74 km) from Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh, India. Fatalities included Y. S. Rajasekhara Reddy, the Chief Minister of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Andhra_Pradesh_helicopter_crash)

🕊️ 2024 – Four people are killed in a mass shooting targeting homeless people on a Chicago Transit Authority train in Forest Park, Illinois, United States.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_%22L%22)

⚔️ 1870 – Franco-Prussian War: Battle of Sedan: Prussian forces take Napoleon III of France and 100,000 of his soldiers prisoner.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco-Prussian_War)
