# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 410 – The sacking of Rome by the Visigoths ends after three days.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sack_of_Rome_(410))

🏰 1172 – Henry the Young King and Margaret of France are crowned junior king and queen of England.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_the_Young_King)

📜 1232 – Shikken Hojo Yasutoki of the Kamakura shogunate promulgates the Goseibai Shikimoku, the first Japanese legal code governing the samurai class.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shikken)

🛡️ 1353 – War of the Straits and Sardinian–Aragonese war: The Battle of Alghero results in a crushing victory of the allied Aragonese and Venetian fleet over the Genoese fleet, most of which is captured.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_Straits)

⚓ 1557 – The Battle of St. Quentin results in Emmanuel Philibert becoming Duke of Savoy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_St._Quentin_(1557))

🚀 1600 – Ishida Mitsunari's Western Army commences the Siege of Fushimi Castle, which is lightly defended by a much smaller Tokugawa garrison led by Torii Mototada.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ishida_Mitsunari)

💼 1776 – American Revolutionary War: British forces under General William Howe flank and defeat the Continental Army under General George Washington during the Battle of Long Island, with the 1st Maryland Regiment sustaining heavy casualties to cover retreating American troops.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War)

🎖️ 1963 – An explosion at the Cane Creek potash mine near Moab, Utah kills 18 miners.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intrepid_Potash)

📡 1914 – World War I: Battle of Étreux: A British rearguard action by the Royal Munster Fusiliers during the Great Retreat.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I)

🏙️ 1918 – Mexican Revolution: Battle of Ambos Nogales: U.S. Army forces skirmish against Mexican Carrancistas as part of the Mexican Border War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Revolution)

⚙️ 1980 – South Korean presidential election: After successfully staging the Coup d'état of May Seventeenth, General Chun Doo-hwan, running unopposed, has the National Conference for Unification elect him President of the Fourth Republic of Korea.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1980_South_Korean_presidential_election)

💥 1896 – Anglo-Zanzibar War: The shortest war in world history (09:02 to 09:40), between the United Kingdom and Zanzibar.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Zanzibar_War)

🕊️ 1942 – First day of the Sarny Massacre, perpetrated by German military forces.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarny_massacre)

⚔️ 2009 – Internal conflict in Myanmar: The Burmese military junta and ethnic armies begin three days of violent clashes in the Kokang Special Region.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar_conflict)

🏰 1914 – World War I: Siege of Tsingtao: A Japanese fleet commanded by Vice Admiral Sadakichi Kato imposes a blockade along the whole coastline of German Tsingtao, initiating the Siege of Tsingtao.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Tsingtao)
