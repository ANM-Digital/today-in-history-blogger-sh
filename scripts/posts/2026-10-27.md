# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 312 – Constantine the Great is said to have received his famous Vision of the Cross.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine_the_Great)

🏰 625 – Election of pope Honorius I following the death of pope Boniface V two days prior.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Honorius_I)

📜 1275 – Traditional founding of the city of Amsterdam.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam)

🛡️ 1524 – French troops lay siege to Pavia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_campaign_of_1524%E2%80%931525)

⚓ 1644 – Second Battle of Newbury in the English Civil War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Newbury)

🚀 1726 – J. S. Bach leads the first performance of Ich will den Kreuzstab gerne tragen, BWV 56, one of few works he called a cantata.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Sebastian_Bach)

💼 1924 – The Uzbek SSR is founded in the Soviet Union.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbek_Soviet_Socialist_Republic)

🎖️ 2019 – Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant founder and leader Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi kills himself and three children by detonating a suicide vest during the U.S. military Barisha raid in northwestern Syria.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_State)

📡 1964 – Ronald Reagan delivers a speech on behalf of the Republican candidate for president, Barry Goldwater. The speech launches his political career and comes to be known as "A Time for Choosing".. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Reagan)

🏙️ 2017 – Catalonia declares independence from Spain.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalonia)

⚙️ 1919 – The Fourth Regional Congress of Peasants, Workers and Insurgents is held by the Makhnovshchina at Oleksandrivsk.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Congress_of_Peasants%2C_Workers_and_Insurgents)

💥 1999 – Gunmen open fire in the Armenian Parliament, killing the Prime Minister and seven others.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_parliament_shooting)

🕊️ 1870 – Franco-Prussian War: Marshal Bazaine surrenders to Prussian forces at the conclusion of the Siege of Metz along with 140,000 French soldiers.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco-Prussian_War)

⚔️ 1954 – Benjamin O. Davis, Jr. becomes the first African-American general in the United States Air Force.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_O._Davis_Jr.)

🏰 1958 – Iskander Mirza, the first President of Pakistan, is deposed by General Ayub Khan, who had been appointed the enforcer of martial law by Mirza 20 days earlier.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iskander_Mirza)
