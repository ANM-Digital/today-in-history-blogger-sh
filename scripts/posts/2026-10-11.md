# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 944 – Caliph al-Muttaqi is blinded and deposed by the Turkish general Tuzun.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Muttaqi)

🏰 1138 – A massive earthquake strikes Aleppo; it is one of the most destructive earthquakes ever.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1138_Aleppo_earthquake)

📜 1311 – The peerage and clergy restrict the authority of English kings with the Ordinances of 1311.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England)

🛡️ 1531 – The five Catholic cantons defeat the Protestant canton of Zurich in the battle of Kappel.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Zurich)

⚓ 1614 – The New Netherland Company applies to the States General of the Netherlands for exclusive trading rights in what is now the northeastern United States.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Netherland_Company)

🚀 1776 – American Revolution: A fleet of American boats on Lake Champlain is defeated by the Royal Navy, but delays the British advance until 1777.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution)

💼 1912 – First Balkan War: The day after the Battle of Sarantaporo, Greek troops liberate the city of Kozani.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Balkan_War)

🎖️ 1937 – The Duke and Duchess of Windsor tour Nazi Germany for 12 days and meet Adolf Hitler on 22 October.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_VIII)

📡 1862 – American Civil War: Confederate troops conduct a raid on Chambersburg, Pennsylvania.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War)

🏙️ 1910 – Piloted by Arch Hoxsey, Theodore Roosevelt becomes the first U.S. president to fly in an airplane.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archibald_Hoxsey)

⚙️ 2018 – Soyuz MS-10, launching an intended crew for the ISS, suffers an in-flight abort. The crew lands safely.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soyuz_MS-10)

💥 1865 – Hundreds of black men and women march in Jamaica, starting the Morant Bay rebellion.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morant_Bay_rebellion)

🕊️ 1987 – Start of Operation Pawan by Indian forces in Sri Lanka. Thousands of civilians, insurgents, and soldiers die.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Pawan)

⚔️ 1958 – NASA launches Pioneer 1, its first space probe, although it fails to achieve a stable orbit.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pioneer_1)

🏰 1840 – The Maronite leader Bashir Shihab II surrenders to the Ottoman Empire and later is sent to Malta in exile.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashir_Shihab_II)
