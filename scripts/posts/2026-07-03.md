# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 324 – Battle of Adrianople: Constantine I defeats Licinius, who flees to Byzantium.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Adrianople_(324))

🏰 987 – Hugh Capet is crowned King of France, the first of the Capetian dynasty that would rule France until the French Revolution in 1792.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Capet)

📜 1035 – William the Conqueror becomes the Duke of Normandy, reigning until 1087.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_the_Conqueror)

🛡️ 1450 – Jack Cade and his followers enter London unhindered by the government of king Henry VI.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Cade's_Rebellion)

⚓ 1535 – Diego de Almagro leaves the recently conquered Inca capital of Cuzco to lead an expedition to Chile.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_de_Almagro)

🚀 1608 – Québec City is founded by Samuel de Champlain.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quebec_City)

💼 1754 – French and Indian War: George Washington surrenders Fort Necessity to French forces.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_and_Indian_War)

🎖️ 1886 – Karl Benz officially unveils the Benz Patent-Motorwagen, the first purpose-built automobile.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Benz)

📡 1863 – American Civil War: The final day of the Battle of Gettysburg culminates with Pickett's Charge.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War)

🏙️ 1898 – A Spanish squadron, led by Pascual Cervera y Topete, is defeated by an American squadron under William T. Sampson in the Battle of Santiago de Cuba.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascual_Cervera_y_Topete)

⚙️ 1940 – World War II: The Royal Navy attacks the French naval squadron in Algeria, to ensure that it will not fall under German control. Of the four French battleships present, one is sunk, two are damaged, and one escapes back to France.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II)

💥 1814 – War of 1812: American forces capture Fort Erie from British troops in Upper Canada.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_1812)

🕊️ 1839 – The first state normal school in the United States, the forerunner to today's Framingham State University, opens in Lexington, Massachusetts with three students.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_school)

⚔️ 1938 – World speed record for a steam locomotive is set in England, by the Mallard, which reaches a speed of 125.88 miles per hour (202.58 km/h).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steam_locomotive)

🏰 2013 – President of Egypt Mohamed Morsi is removed from office by the military after four days of protests all over the country calling for his resignation, to which he did not respond. The president of the Supreme Constitutional Court of Egypt, Adly Mansour, is declared acting president until further elections are held.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Egypt)
