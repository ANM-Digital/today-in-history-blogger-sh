# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 959 – Eraclus becomes the 25th bishop of Liège.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eraclus)

🏰 1140 – Song dynasty general Yue Fei defeats an army led by Jin dynasty general Wuzhu at the Battle of Yancheng during the Jin–Song Wars.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Song_dynasty)

📜 1331 – King Stefan Uroš III, after months of anarchy, surrenders to his son and rival Stefan Dušan, who succeeds as King of Serbia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_De%C4%8Danski)

🛡️ 1415 – Henry the Navigator leads Portuguese forces to victory over the Marinids at the Conquest of Ceuta.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Henry_the_Navigator)

⚓ 1673 – The Dutch fleet defeats an Anglo-French fleet under Prince Rupert in the battle of Texel.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Rupert_of_the_Rhine)

🚀 1716 – Seventh Ottoman–Venetian War: The arrival of naval reinforcements and the news of the Battle of Petrovaradin force the Ottomans to abandon the Siege of Corfu, thus preserving the Ionian Islands under Venetian rule.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman%E2%80%93Venetian_War_(1714%E2%80%931718))

💼 2013 – Hundreds of people are reported killed by chemical attacks in the Ghouta region of Syria.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghouta_chemical_attack)

🎖️ 1810 – Jean-Baptiste Bernadotte, Marshal of France, is elected Crown Prince of Sweden by the Swedish Riksdag of the Estates.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_XIV_John)

📡 1878 – The American Bar Association is founded in Saratoga Springs, New York.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Bar_Association)

🏙️ 1863 – Lawrence, Kansas is destroyed by pro-Confederate guerrillas known as Quantrill's Raiders.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence%2C_Kansas)

⚙️ 1911 – The Mona Lisa is stolen by Vincenzo Peruggia, a Louvre employee.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mona_Lisa)

💥 1944 – Dumbarton Oaks Conference, prelude to the United Nations, begins.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumbarton_Oaks_Conference)

🕊️ 1945 – Physicist Harry Daghlian is fatally irradiated in a criticality accident during an experiment with the Demon core at Los Alamos National Laboratory.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Daghlian)

⚔️ 1959 – United States President Dwight D. Eisenhower signs an executive order proclaiming Hawaii the 50th state of the union. Hawaii's admission is currently commemorated by Hawaii Admission Day.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_United_States)

🏰 2000 – American golfer Tiger Woods wins the 82nd PGA Championship and becomes the first golfer since Ben Hogan in 1953 to win three majors in a calendar year.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiger_Woods)
