# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 475 – The Roman general Orestes forces western Roman Emperor Julius Nepos to flee his capital city, Ravenna.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire)

🏰 632 – Fatimah, daughter of the Islamic prophet Muhammad, dies, with her cause of death being a controversial topic among the Sunni Muslims and Shia Muslims.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatima)

📜 1189 – Third Crusade: The Crusaders begin the Siege of Acre under Guy of Lusignan.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Crusade)

🛡️ 1290 – The second treaty of Birgham is ratified in which Margaret, Maid of Norway and granddaughter of king Alexander III of Scotland is to marry the English prince Edward of Caernarfon.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Birgham)

⚓ 1521 – Ottoman wars in Europe: The Ottoman Turks occupy Belgrade.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_wars_in_Europe)

🚀 1609 – Henry Hudson discovers Delaware Bay.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Hudson)

💼 1709 – Meidingnu Pamheiba is crowned King of Manipur.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gharib_Niwaz_(Manipur))

🎖️ 1921 – Russian Civil War: The Red Army dissolves the Makhnovshchina after driving the Revolutionary Insurgent Army out of Ukraine.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Civil_War)

📡 1943 – Denmark in World War II: German authorities demand that Danish authorities crack down on acts of resistance. The next day, martial law is imposed on Denmark.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark_in_World_War_II)

🏙️ 1996 – Chicago Seven defendant David Dellinger, antiwar activist Bradford Lyttle, Civil Rights Movement historian Randy Kryn, and eight others are arrested by the Federal Protective Service while protesting in a demonstration at the Kluczynski Federal Building in downtown Chicago during that year's Democratic National Convention.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Seven)

⚙️ 1890 – The strongest storm in Finnish history kills at least three people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Storm_of_1890)

💥 1917 – Ten suffragists, members of the Silent Sentinels, are arrested while picketing the White House in favor of women's suffrage in the United States.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffrage)

🕊️ 1999 – The Russian space mission Soyuz TM-29 reaches completion, ending nearly 10 years of continuous occupation on the space station Mir as it approaches the end of its life.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soyuz_TM-29)

⚔️ 1924 – The Georgian opposition stages the August Uprising against the Soviet Union.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic)

🏰 1998 – Second Congo War: Loyalist troops backed by Angolan and Zimbabwean forces repulse the RCD and Rwandan offensive on Kinshasa.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Congo_War)
