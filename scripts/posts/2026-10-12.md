# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -539 – The army of Cyrus the Great of Persia conquer Babylon, ending the Babylonian empire. (Julian calendar). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrus_the_Great)

🏰 166 – Roman emperors Marcus Aurelius and Lucius Verus celebrate a triumph in Rome for the successful completion of their war with the Partians.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_166)

📜 633 – Battle of Hatfield Chase: King Edwin of Northumbria is defeated and killed by an alliance under Penda of Mercia and Cadwallon of Gwynedd.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Hatfield_Chase)

🛡️ 1279 – The Nichiren Shōshū branch of Buddhism is founded in Japan.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nichiren_Sh%C5%8Dsh%C5%AB)

⚓ 1398 – In the Treaty of Salynas, Lithuania cedes Samogitia to the Teutonic Knights.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Salynas)

🚀 1406 – Chen Yanxiang, the only person from Indonesia known to have visited dynastic Korea, reaches Seoul after having set out from Java four months before.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chen_Yanxiang)

💼 1654 – The Delft Explosion devastates the city in the Netherlands, killing more than 100 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft)

🎖️ 1748 – War of Jenkins' Ear: A British squadron wins a tactical victory over a Spanish squadron off Havana.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_Jenkins'_Ear)

📡 1871 – The British in India enact the Criminal Tribes Act, naming many local communities "Criminal Tribes".. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_Tribes_Act)

🏙️ 2019 – Typhoon Hagibis makes landfall in Japan, killing 10 and forcing the evacuation of one million people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Hagibis)

⚙️ 1963 – After nearly 23 years of imprisonment, Reverend Walter Ciszek, a Jesuit missionary, was released from the Soviet Union.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Ciszek)

💥 1944 – World War II: The Axis occupation of Athens comes to an end.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II)

🕊️ 2000 – Two Israeli reservists are lynched by a Palestinian mob at the police station of Ramallah.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Ramallah_lynching)

⚔️ 1945 – The Lao Issara took control of Laos' government and reaffirmed the country's independence.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lao_Issara)

🏰 1967 – A bomb explodes on board Cyprus Airways Flight 284 while flying over the Mediterranean Sea, killing 66.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus_Airways_Flight_284)
