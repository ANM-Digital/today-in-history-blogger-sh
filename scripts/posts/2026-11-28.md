# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 587 – Treaty of Andelot: King Guntram of Burgundy recognizes Childebert II as his heir.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Andelot)

🏰 936 – Shi Jingtang is enthroned as the first emperor of the Later Jin by Emperor Taizong of Liao, following a revolt against Emperor Fei of Later Tang.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shi_Jingtang)

📜 1240 – Mongol troops under Batu Khan begin with the siege of Kiev.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongol_Empire)

🛡️ 1443 – Skanderbeg and his forces liberate Kruja in central Albania and raise the Albanian flag.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skanderbeg)

⚓ 1520 – After 38 days, an expedition under the command of Ferdinand Magellan completes the first passage through the Strait of Magellan and enters the Pacific Ocean.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_Magellan)

🚀 1627 – The Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth Navy has its greatest and last naval victory in the Battle of Oliwa.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish%E2%80%93Lithuanian_Commonwealth_Navy)

💼 1785 – The first Treaty of Hopewell is signed, by which the United States acknowledges Cherokee lands in what is now East Tennessee.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Hopewell)

🎖️ 1943 – World War II: Tehran Conference: U.S. President Franklin D. Roosevelt, British Prime Minister Winston Churchill and Soviet Premier Joseph Stalin meet in Tehran, Iran, to discuss war strategy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II)

📡 1990 – British Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher resigns as leader of the Conservative Party and, therefore, as Prime Minister. She is succeeded in both positions by John Major.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_the_United_Kingdom)

🏙️ 1965 – Vietnam War: In response to U.S. President Lyndon B. Johnson's call for "more flags" in Vietnam, Philippine President-elect Ferdinand Marcos announces he will send troops to help fight in South Vietnam.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam)

⚙️ 1814 – The Times of London becomes the first newspaper to be produced on a steam-powered printing press, built by the German team of Koenig & Bauer.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times)

💥 1975 – Timor-Leste declares its independence from Portugal.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timor-Leste)

🕊️ 1899 – The Second Boer War: A British column is engaged by Boer forces at the Battle of Modder River; although the Boers withdraw, the British suffer heavy casualties.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Boer_War)

⚔️ 1980 – Iran–Iraq War: Operation Morvarid: The bulk of the Iraqi Navy is destroyed by the Iranian Navy in the Persian Gulf. (Commemorated in Iran as Navy Day.). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War)

🏰 1861 – American Civil War: The Confederate States of America accept a rival state government's pronouncement that declares Missouri to be the 12th state of the Confederacy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War)
