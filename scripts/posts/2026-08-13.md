# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -29 – Octavian holds the first of three consecutive triumphs in Rome to celebrate the victory over the Dalmatian tribes.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustus)

🏰 523 – John I becomes the new Pope after the death of Pope Hormisdas.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_John_I)

📜 871 – Emperor Louis II of Italy and Empress Engelberga are captured by Prince Adelchis of Benevento.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_II_of_Italy)

🛡️ 900 – Count Reginar I of Hainault rises against Zwentibold of Lotharingia and slays him near present-day Susteren.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reginar_Longneck)

⚓ 1099 – Election of Pope Paschal II following the death of pope Urban II in the previous month.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Paschal_II)

🚀 1415 – The English invasion of France begins when English king Henry V lands in France.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hundred_Years'_War%2C_1415%E2%80%931453)

💼 1516 – The Treaty of Noyon between France and Spain is signed. Francis I of France recognizes Charles's claim to Naples, and Charles V, Holy Roman Emperor, recognizes Francis's claim to Milan.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_League_of_Cambrai)

🎖️ 1624 – The French king Louis XIII appoints Cardinal Richelieu as prime minister.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_XIII)

📡 1704 – War of the Spanish Succession: Battle of Blenheim: English and Imperial forces are victorious over French and Bavarian troops.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_Spanish_Succession)

🏙️ 1898 – Spanish–American War: Spanish and American forces engage in a mock battle for Manila, after which the Spanish commander surrendered in order to keep the city out of Filipino rebel hands.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish%E2%80%93American_War)

⚙️ 2004 – One hundred fifty-six Congolese Tutsi refugees are massacred at the Gatumba refugee camp in Burundi.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo)

💥 2014 – A Cessna Citation Excel crashes in Santos, São Paulo, Brazil killing all seven people aboard, including Brazilian Socialist Party presidential candidate Eduardo Campos.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna_Citation_Excel)

🕊️ 1913 – First production in the UK of stainless steel by Harry Brearley.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stainless_steel)

⚔️ 1940 – World War II: The German Luftwaffe launches an air operation, codenamed Adlertag, during the Battle of Britain in an attempt to destroy the British Royal Air Force.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II)

🏰 1944 – World War II: German troops begin the pillage and razing of Anogeia in Crete that would continue until September 5.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Razing_of_Anogeia)
