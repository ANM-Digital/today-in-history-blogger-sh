# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 960 – Battle of Andrassos: Byzantines under Leo Phokas the Younger score a crushing victory over the Hamdanid Emir of Aleppo, Sayf al-Dawla.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Andrassos)

🏰 1278 – Trần Thánh Tông, the second emperor of the Trần dynasty, decides to pass the throne to his crown prince Trần Khâm and take up the post of Retired Emperor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%E1%BA%A7n_Th%C3%A1nh_T%C3%B4ng)

📜 1519 – Hernán Cortés enters Tenochtitlán and Aztec ruler Moctezuma welcomes him with a great celebration.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hern%C3%A1n_Cort%C3%A9s)

🛡️ 1602 – The Bodleian Library at the University of Oxford is opened to the public.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodleian_Library)

⚓ 1745 – Charles Edward Stuart invades England with an army of approximately 5,000 that would later participate in the Battle of Culloden.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Edward_Stuart)

🚀 2013 – Typhoon Haiyan, one of the strongest tropical cyclones ever recorded, strikes the Visayas region of the Philippines; the storm left at least 6,340 people dead with over 1,000 still missing, and caused $2.86 billion (2013 USD; equivalent to $3.95 billion in 2025) in damage.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Haiyan)

💼 1972 – American pay television network Home Box Office (HBO) launches.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO)

🎖️ 1939 – Venlo Incident: Two British agents of SIS are captured by the Germans.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venlo_incident)

📡 2016 – Donald Trump is elected the 45th President of the United States, defeating Hillary Clinton, the first woman ever to receive a major party's nomination.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump)

🏙️ 1837 – Mary Lyon founds Mount Holyoke Female Seminary, which later becomes Mount Holyoke College.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Lyon)

⚙️ 1987 – Remembrance Day bombing: A Provisional IRA bomb explodes in Enniskillen, Northern Ireland during a ceremony honouring those who had died in wars involving British forces. Twelve people are killed and sixty-three wounded.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remembrance_Day_bombing)

💥 1923 – Beer Hall Putsch: In Munich, Adolf Hitler leads the Nazis in an unsuccessful attempt to overthrow the German government.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beer_Hall_Putsch)

🕊️ 1963 – Finnair's Aero Flight 217 crashes near Mariehamn Airport in Jomala, Åland, killing 22 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnair)

⚔️ 1889 – Montana is admitted as the 41st U.S. state.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montana)

🏰 1920 – Rupert Bear, illustrated by Mary Tourtel makes his first appearance in print.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert_Bear)
