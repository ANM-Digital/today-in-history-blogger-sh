# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 475 – Romulus Augustulus is proclaimed Western Roman Emperor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romulus_Augustulus)

🏰 649 – The Lateran Council of 649 comes to a close, which condemns Monotheletism.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lateran_Council_of_649)

📜 802 – Empress Irene is deposed and banished to Lesbos. Conspirators place Nikephoros, the minister of finance, on the Byzantine throne.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irene_of_Athens)

🛡️ 932 – Abbasid caliph al-Muqtadir is killed while fighting against the forces of general Mu'nis al-Muzaffar. Al-Muqtadir's brother al-Qahir is chosen to succeed him.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbasid_Caliphate)

⚓ 1517 – Protestant Reformation: Martin Luther posts his 95 Theses on the door of the Castle Church in Wittenberg.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reformation)

🚀 1702 – Anglo-Dutch forces, under the Duke of Marlborough and Earl of Athlone, capture Liège during the War of the Spanish Succession.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Churchill%2C_1st_Duke_of_Marlborough)

💼 1917 – World War I: Battle of Beersheba: The "last successful cavalry charge in history".. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I)

🎖️ 1822 – Emperor Agustín de Iturbide attempts to dissolve the Congress of the Mexican Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agust%C3%ADn_de_Iturbide)

📡 1913 – Dedication of the Lincoln Highway, the first automobile highway across United States.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Highway)

🏙️ 1863 – The New Zealand Wars resume as British forces in New Zealand led by General Duncan Cameron begin their Invasion of the Waikato.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Wars)

⚙️ 1961 – In the Soviet Union, Joseph Stalin's body is removed from Lenin's Mausoleum, and is buried near the Kremlin Wall under a plain white marker.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union)

💥 1996 – TAM Transportes Aéreos Regionais Flight 402 crashes in São Paulo, Brazil, killing 99 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TAM_Transportes_A%C3%A9reos_Regionais_Flight_402)

🕊️ 1984 – Indian Prime Minister Indira Gandhi is assassinated by two Sikh security guards. Riots break out in New Delhi and other cities and around 3,000 Sikhs are killed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indira_Gandhi)

⚔️ 1895 – The strongest earthquake in the Midwestern United States since 1812 strikes near Charleston, Missouri, causing damage and killing at least two.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1895_Charleston_earthquake)

🏰 1864 – Nevada is admitted as the 36th U.S. state.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevada)
