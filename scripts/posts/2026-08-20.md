# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 14 – Agrippa Postumus, maternal grandson of the late Roman emperor Augustus, is executed by his guards while in exile.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10s)

🏰 636 – Marking the first great wave of Muslim conquests and the rapid advance of Islam outside Arabia, Arab forces led by Khalid ibn al-Walid defeat the Byzantine Empire and take control of the Levant.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslims)

📜 917 – Battle of Acheloos: Tsar Simeon I of Bulgaria decisively defeats a Byzantine army.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Achelous_(917))

🛡️ 1057 – An army under Isaac I Komnenos defeat forces loyal to rival emperor Michael VI Bringas in the battle of Hades.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_I_Komnenos)

⚓ 1119 – King Henry I of England defeats king Louis VI of France in the battle of Brémule.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_I_of_England)

🚀 1308 – At the conclusion of the interrogation of the leaders of the Knights Templar, the three papal investigators, Cardinals Bérenger Frédol, Etienne de Suisy and Landolfo Brancacci, write the "Chinon Parchment", in which they affirm that the accused Templars had confessed, done penance, and were absolved of heresy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knights_Templar)

💼 1467 – The Second Battle of Olmedo takes places as part of a succession conflict between Henry IV of Castile and his half-brother Alfonso, Prince of Asturias.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Olmedo)

🎖️ 1519 – On the third day of battle, philosopher and general Wang Yangming defeats Zhu Chenhao, ending the Prince of Ning rebellion against the reign of the Ming dynasty's Zhengde Emperor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Yangming)

📡 1648 – The Battle of Lens is the last major military confrontation of the Thirty Years' War, contributing to the signing of the Peace of Westphalia in October that year.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Lens)

🏙️ 1707 – The first Siege of Pensacola comes to an end with the failure of the British to capture Pensacola, Florida.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Pensacola_(1707))

⚙️ 1975 – Viking program: NASA launches the Viking 1 planetary probe toward Mars.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viking_program)

💥 1988 – "Black Saturday" of the Yellowstone fire in Yellowstone National Park.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_fires_of_1988)

🕊️ 1852 – Steamboat Atlantic sinks on Lake Erie after a collision, with the loss of at least 150 lives.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_(1848_ship))

⚔️ 1975 – ČSA Flight 540 crashes on approach to Damascus International Airport in Damascus, Syria, killing 126 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%8CSA_Flight_540)

🏰 1955 – Battle of Philippeville: In Morocco, a force of Berbers from the Atlas Mountains region of Algeria raid two rural settlements and kill 77 French nationals.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Philippeville)
