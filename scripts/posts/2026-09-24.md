# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 787 – The Second Council of Nicaea begins at the Church of Holy Wisdom in the city of Nicaea in Bithynia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_787)

🏰 867 – Byzantine emperor Michael III is murdered in his chamber by his junior emperor Basil I.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_III)

📜 1568 – Spanish naval forces defeat an English fleet, under the command of John Hawkins, at the Battle of San Juan de Ulúa near Veracruz.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_San_Juan_de_Ul%C3%BAa_(1568))

🛡️ 1645 – The Battle of Rowton Heath in England occurs, ending in a Parliamentarian victory over a Royalist army commanded in person by King Charles I.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Rowton_Heath)

⚓ 1789 – The United States Congress passes the Judiciary Act, creating the office of the Attorney General and federal judiciary system and ordering the composition of the Supreme Court.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary_Act_of_1789)

🚀 2009 – The G20 summit begins in Pittsburgh with 30 global leaders in attendance.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_G20_Pittsburgh_summit)

💼 1996 – Representatives of 71 nations sign the Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty at the United Nations.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comprehensive_Nuclear-Test-Ban_Treaty)

🎖️ 1852 – The first powered, passenger-carrying airship, the Giffard dirigible, travels 17 miles (27 km) from Paris to Trappes.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giffard_dirigible)

📡 1929 – Jimmy Doolittle performs the first flight without a window, proving that full instrument flying from take off to landing is possible.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Doolittle)

🏙️ 1959 – TAI Flight 307 crashes during takeoff from Bordeaux–Mérignac Airport in Bordeaux, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France, killing 55 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TAI_Flight_307)

⚙️ 1954 – AEC Routemaster, the iconic London bus was introduced.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEC_Routemaster)

💥 1911 – His Majesty's Airship No. 1, Britain's first rigid airship, is wrecked by strong winds before her maiden flight at Barrow-in-Furness.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMA_No._1)

🕊️ 1875 – The 1864 play Heath Cobblers by Aleksis Kivi premieres in Oulu, Finland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Cobblers)

⚔️ 2008 – Thabo Mbeki resigns as president of South Africa.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thabo_Mbeki)

🏰 1993 – The Cambodian monarchy is restored, with Norodom Sihanouk as king.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_Cambodia)
