# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -30 – Octavian (later known as Augustus) enters Alexandria, Egypt, executes Marcus Antonius Antyllus, and brings the city under the control of the Roman Republic. (date is O.S.). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustus)

🏰 69 – Batavian rebellion: The Batavians in Germania Inferior (Netherlands) revolt under the leadership of Gaius Julius Civilis.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_69)

📜 314 – The first synod of Arles is opened by bishop Marinus of Arles.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/314)

🛡️ 527 – Justinian I becomes the sole ruler of the Byzantine Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justinian_I)

⚓ 607 – Ono no Imoko is dispatched as envoy to the Sui court in China (Traditional Japanese date: July 3, 607).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ono_no_Imoko)

🚀 902 – Taormina, the last Byzantine stronghold in Sicily, is captured by the Aghlabid army, concluding the Muslim conquest of Sicily.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taormina)

💼 1203 – Alexios IV Angelos is crowned Byzantine co-emperor after his father Isaac II Angelos is pressured to do so by the leaders of the Fourth Crusade.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexios_IV_Angelos)

🎖️ 1469 – Louis XI of France founds the chivalric order called the Order of Saint Michael in Amboise.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_XI)

📡 1571 – The Ottoman conquest of Cyprus is concluded, by the surrender of Famagusta.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman%E2%80%93Venetian_War_(1570%E2%80%931573))

🏙️ 1620 – Speedwell leaves Delfshaven to bring pilgrims to America by way of England.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speedwell_(1577_ship))

⚙️ 1714 – George, Elector of Hanover, becomes King George I of Great Britain, marking the beginning of the Georgian era of British history.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover)

💥 1944 – World War II: The Warsaw Uprising against the Nazi German occupation breaks out in Warsaw, Poland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_Uprising)

🕊️ 1842 – The Lombard Street riot erupts in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, United States.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lombard_Street_riot)

⚔️ 1894 – The Empire of Japan and Qing China declare war on each other after a week of fighting over Korea, formally inaugurating the First Sino-Japanese War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_of_Japan)

🏰 1984 – Commercial peat-cutters discover the preserved bog body of a man, called Lindow Man, at Lindow Moss, Cheshire, England.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peat)
