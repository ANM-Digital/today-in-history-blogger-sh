# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 328 – The official opening of Constantine's Bridge built over the Danube between Sucidava (Corabia, Romania) and Oescus (Gigen, Bulgaria) by the Roman architect Theophilus Patricius.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine's_Bridge_(Danube))

🏰 1294 – Election of pope Celestine V following the death of pope Nicholas IV two years prior.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1292%E2%80%931294_papal_election)

📜 1316 – The Burgundian and Majorcan claimants of the Principality of Achaea meet in the Battle of Manolada.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchy_of_Burgundy)

🛡️ 1413 – Mehmed Çelebi and his army defeat the army of his brother Musa Çelebi in the battle of Çamurlu. Musa is captured and executed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehmed_I)

⚓ 1584 – The Maronite College is established in Rome.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontifical_Maronite_College)

🚀 1610 – John Guy sets sail from Bristol with 39 other colonists for Newfoundland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Guy_(colonial_administrator))

💼 1770 – The Battle of Chesma between the Russian Empire and the Ottoman Empire begins, resulting in one of the worst naval defeats of the Ottomans since Lepanto.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Chesma)

🎖️ 1811 – The Venezuelan Declaration of Independence is adopted by a congress of the provinces.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuelan_Declaration_of_Independence)

📡 1935 – The National Labor Relations Act, which governs labor relations in the United States, is signed into law by President Franklin D. Roosevelt.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Labor_Relations_Act_of_1935)

🏙️ 1803 – The Convention of Artlenburg is signed, leading to the French occupation of the Electorate of Hanover (which had been ruled by the British king).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convention_of_Artlenburg)

⚙️ 1833 – Lê Văn Khôi along with 27 soldiers stage a mutiny taking over the Phiên An citadel, developing into the Lê Văn Khôi revolt against Emperor Minh Mạng.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%AA_V%C4%83n_Kh%C3%B4i)

💥 2004 – The first direct Indonesian presidential election is held.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_election)

🕊️ 1989 – Iran–Contra affair: Oliver North is sentenced by U.S. District Judge Gerhard A. Gesell to a three-year suspended prison term, two years probation, $150,000 in fines and 1,200 hours community service. His convictions are later overturned.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Contra_affair)

⚔️ 2024 – Keir Starmer is appointed Prime Minister by Charles III, becoming the first Labour prime minister since Gordon Brown in 2010 and the first one to win a general election since Tony Blair at the 2005 general election. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keir_Starmer)

🏰 1954 – Elvis Presley records his first single, "That's All Right", at Sun Records in Memphis, Tennessee.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Presley)

## 🎉 July 5 is celebrated as:


- Bloody Thursday (International Longshore and Warehouse Union)

- Christian feast day:
Anthony Maria Zaccaria, priest (died 1539)

- Christian feast day:
Cyril and Methodius (a public holiday in Czech Republic and Slovakia)

- Christian feast day:
Martha, mother of Simeon Stylites the Younger

- Christian feast day:
Wexford Martyrs (Roman Catholic Church)

- Christian feast day:
Zoe of Rome (Roman Catholic Church)

- Christian feast day:
July 5 (Eastern Orthodox liturgics)

- Fifth of July (New York), historic celebration of the abolition of slavery in New York in 1827.

- Independence Day (Algeria), celebrating the independence of Algeria from France in 1962.

- Independence Day (Cape Verde), celebrating the independence of Cape Verde from Portugal in 1975.

- Independence Day (Venezuela), celebrating the independence of Venezuela from Spain in 1811; also National Armed Forces Day.

- Tynwald Day, if July 5 is on a weekend, the holiday is the following Monday. (Isle of Man)
