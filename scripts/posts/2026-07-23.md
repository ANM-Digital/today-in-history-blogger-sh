# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 685 – Election of pope John V following the death of pope Benedict II two months prior.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_John_V)

🏰 811 – Byzantine emperor Nikephoros I plunders the Bulgarian capital of Pliska and captures Khan Krum's treasury.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_Empire)

📜 1319 – A Knights Hospitaller fleet scores a crushing victory over an Aydinid fleet off Chios.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knights_Hospitaller)

🛡️ 1632 – Three hundred colonists bound for New France depart from Dieppe, France.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_France)

⚓ 1711 – The treaty of Pruth between the Ottoman and Russian Empire is signed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_the_Pruth)

🚀 1997 – Digital Equipment Corporation files antitrust charges against chipmaker Intel.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Equipment_Corporation)

💼 1881 – The Boundary Treaty of 1881 between Chile and Argentina is signed in Buenos Aires.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boundary_Treaty_of_1881_between_Chile_and_Argentina)

🎖️ 1813 – Sir Thomas Maitland is appointed as the first Governor of Malta, transforming the island from a British protectorate to a de facto colony.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Maitland_(British_Army_officer))

📡 2014 – TransAsia Airways Flight 222 crashes in Xixi village near Huxi, Penghu, during approach to Penghu Airport. Forty-eight of the 58 people on board are killed and five more people on the ground are injured.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TransAsia_Airways_Flight_222)

🏙️ 1862 – American Civil War: Henry Halleck becomes general-in-chief of the Union Army.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War)

⚙️ 1983 – Gimli Glider: Air Canada Flight 143 runs out of fuel and makes a deadstick landing at Gimli, Manitoba.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gimli_Glider)

💥 1940 – The United States' Under Secretary of State Sumner Welles issues a declaration on the U.S. non-recognition policy of the Soviet annexation and incorporation of three Baltic states: Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Under_Secretary_of_State)

🕊️ 1962 – The International Agreement on the Neutrality of Laos is signed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Agreement_on_the_Neutrality_of_Laos)

⚔️ 1995 – Comet Hale–Bopp is discovered; it becomes visible to the naked eye on Earth nearly a year later.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comet_Hale%E2%80%93Bopp)

🏰 1862 – Begin of the Hyde Park Riots.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reform_League)
