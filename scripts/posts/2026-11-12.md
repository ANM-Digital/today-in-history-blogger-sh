# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 954 – The 13-year-old Lothair III is crowned at the Abbey of Saint-Remi as king of the West Frankish Kingdom.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lothair_of_France)

🏰 1028 – Future Byzantine empress Zoe takes the throne as empress consort to Romanos III Argyros.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Byzantine_emperors)

📜 1330 – Battle of Posada ends: Wallachian Voievode Basarab I defeats the Hungarian army by ambush.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Posada)

🛡️ 1439 – Plymouth becomes the first town incorporated by the English Parliament.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plymouth)

⚓ 1614 – The treaty of Xanten ends the Jülich-Cleves Succession Crises, which nearly brought Europe to the brink of war, and divides the disputed lands between Brandenburg and Neuburg.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_J%C3%BClich_Succession)

🚀 1918 – Dissolution of Austria-Hungary: Austria becomes a republic. After the proclamation, a coup attempt by the communist Red Guard is defeated by the social-democratic Volkswehr.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_Austria-Hungary)

💼 1970 – The 1970 Bhola cyclone makes landfall on the coast of East Pakistan, becoming the deadliest tropical cyclone in history.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1970_Bhola_cyclone)

🎖️ 1971 – Vietnam War: As part of Vietnamization, U.S. President Richard Nixon sets February 1, 1972 as the deadline for the removal of another 45,000 American troops from Vietnam.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamization)

📡 1970 – The Oregon Highway Division attempts to destroy a rotting beached sperm whale with explosives, leading to the now infamous "exploding whale" incident.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_Department_of_Transportation)

🏙️ 2003 – Shanghai Transrapid sets a new world speed record of 501 kilometres per hour (311 mph) for commercial railway systems, which remains the fastest for unmodified commercial rail vehicles.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_maglev_train)

⚙️ 1961 – Terry Jo Duperrault is the sole survivor of a series of brutal murders aboard the ketch Bluebelle.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ketch)

💥 1893 – Abdur Rahman Khan accepts the Durand Line as the border between the Emirate of Afghanistan and the British Raj.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdur_Rahman_Khan)

🕊️ 1980 – The NASA space probe Voyager I makes its closest approach to Saturn and takes the first images of its rings.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA)

⚔️ 1941 – World War II: The Soviet cruiser Chervona Ukraina is destroyed during the Battle of Sevastopol.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_cruiser_Chervona_Ukraina)

🏰 2015 – Two suicide bombers detonate explosives in Bourj el-Barajneh, Beirut, killing 43 people and injuring over 200 others.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicide_attack)
