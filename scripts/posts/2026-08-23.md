# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 79 – Mount Vesuvius begins stirring, on the feast day of Vulcan, the Roman god of fire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_79)

🏰 406 – The Western Roman army under Stilicho corners and defeats the Ostrogothic army in the battle of Faesulae.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Late_Roman_army)

📜 1244 – Siege of Jerusalem: The city surrenders to the Khwarazmiyya, ending Christian control of Jerusalem for the next 672 years.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Jerusalem_(1244))

🛡️ 1328 – William Wallace is put on trial and subsequently hanged, drawn and quartered.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Wallace)

⚓ 1514 – The Battle of Chaldiran ends with a decisive victory for the Sultan Selim I, Ottoman Empire, over the Shah Ismail I, founder of the Safavid dynasty.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Chaldiran)

🚀 1600 – Battle of Gifu Castle: The eastern forces of Tokugawa Ieyasu defeat the western Japanese clans loyal to Toyotomi Hideyori, leading to the destruction of Gifu Castle and serving as a prelude to the Battle of Sekigahara.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Gifu_Castle)

💼 1703 – Edirne event: Sultan Mustafa II of the Ottoman Empire is dethroned.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edirne_Incident)

🎖️ 1898 – The Southern Cross Expedition, the first British venture of the Heroic Age of Antarctic Exploration, departs from London.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Cross_Expedition)

📡 1973 – A bank robbery gone wrong in Stockholm, Sweden, turns into a hostage crisis; over the next five days the hostages begin to sympathise with their captors, leading to the term "Stockholm syndrome".. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norrmalmstorg_robbery)

🏙️ 1839 – The United Kingdom captures Hong Kong as a base as it prepares for the First Opium War with Qing China.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Opium_War)

⚙️ 1975 – The Pontiac Silverdome opens in Pontiac, Michigan, 30 miles (48 km) northwest of Detroit, Michigan. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontiac_Silverdome)

💥 2010 – The Manila hostage crisis occurred near the Quirino Grandstand in Manila, Philippines killing 9 people including the perpetrator while injuring 9 others.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_hostage_crisis)

🕊️ 2013 – A riot at the Palmasola prison complex in Santa Cruz, Bolivia kills 31 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Palmasola_prison_riot)

⚔️ 1990 – Saddam Hussein appears on Iraqi state television with a number of Western "guests" (actually hostages) to try to prevent the Gulf War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saddam_Hussein)

🏰 1985 – Hans Tiedge, top counter-spy of West Germany, defects to East Germany.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Tiedge)
