# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -4004 – James Ussher's calculated First day of the world based on the Old Testament. Creation, he believed, occurred at what would have been nightfall on the previous day.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Ussher)

🏰 -42 – Liberators' civil war: Mark Antony and Octavian decisively defeat an army under Brutus in the second part of the Battle of Philippi, with Brutus committing suicide and ending the civil war.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberators'_civil_war)

📜 425 – Valentinian III is elevated as Roman emperor at the age of six.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentinian_III)

🛡️ 502 – The Synodus Palmaris, called by Gothic king Theoderic, absolves Pope Symmachus of all charges, thus ending the schism of Antipope Laurentius.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_502)

⚓ 1086 – Spanish Reconquista: At the Battle of Sagrajas, the Almoravids defeat the Castilians, but are unable to take advantage of their victory.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reconquista)

🚀 1157 – The Battle of Grathe Heath ends the Danish Civil War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Grathe_Heath)

💼 1295 – The first treaty forming the Auld Alliance between Scotland and France against England is signed in Paris.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auld_Alliance)

🎖️ 1448 – Scotland wins a decisive victory over England at the Battle of Sark, the last pitched battle to be fought between the two kingdoms during the Medieval period.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Sark)

📡 1641 – Irish Catholic gentry from Ulster attempt to seize control of Dublin Castle, the seat of English rule in Ireland, so as to force concessions.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Catholics)

🏙️ 1707 – The First Parliament of the Kingdom of Great Britain convenes.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Parliament_of_Great_Britain)

⚙️ 1912 – First Balkan War: The Battle of Kumanovo between the Serbian and Ottoman armies begins.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Balkan_War)

💥 1868 – Meiji Restoration: Having taken the shogunate's seat of power at Edo and declared it his new capital as Tokyo, Mutsuhito proclaims the start of the new Meiji era.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meiji_Restoration)

🕊️ 1958 – Canada's Springhill mining disaster kills seventy-five miners, while ninety-nine others are rescued.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springhill_mining_disasters)

⚔️ 2004 – A powerful earthquake and its aftershocks hit Niigata Prefecture in northern Japan, killing 35 people, injuring 2,200, and leaving 85,000 homeless or evacuated.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Ch%C5%ABetsu_earthquake)

🏰 1982 – A gunfight breaks out between police officers and members of a religious cult in Arizona. The shootout leaves two cultists dead and dozens of cultists and police officers injured.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miracle_Valley_shootout)
