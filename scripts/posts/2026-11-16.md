# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 534 – The second edition of the Code of Justinian is issued by the Byzantine jurist Tribonian and his assistants, consisting of twelve updated and revised books.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_of_Justinian)

🏰 951 – Emperor Li Jing sends a Southern Tang expeditionary force of 10,000 men under Bian Hao to conquer Chu. Li Jing removes the ruling family to his own capital in Nanjing, ending the Chu Kingdom.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Jing_(Southern_Tang))

📜 1272 – While travelling during the Ninth Crusade, Prince Edward becomes King of England upon Henry III of England's death, but he will not return to England for nearly two years to assume the throne.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_Edward's_crusade)

🛡️ 1326 – King Edward II of England is captured by forces of his wife Isabella of France. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_II)

⚓ 1491 – An auto-da-fé, held in the Brasero de la Dehesa outside of Ávila, concludes the case of the Holy Child of La Guardia with the public execution of several Jewish and converso suspects.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auto-da-f%C3%A9)

🚀 1532 – Francisco Pizarro and his men capture Inca Emperor Atahualpa at the Battle of Cajamarca.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Pizarro)

💼 1632 – King Gustavus Adolphus of Sweden was killed at the Battle of Lützen during the Thirty Years' War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustavus_Adolphus)

🎖️ 1776 – American Revolutionary War: British and Hessian units capture Fort Washington from the Patriots.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War)

📡 1966 – The Temptations release their Greatest Hits album, which goes on to be the Billboard Year-End R&B album of 1967.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Temptations)

🏙️ 1904 – English engineer John Ambrose Fleming receives a patent for the thermionic valve (vacuum tube).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Ambrose_Fleming)

⚙️ 1920 – Qantas, Australia's national airline, is founded as Queensland and Northern Territory Aerial Services Limited.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qantas)

💥 1974 – The Arecibo message is broadcast from Puerto Rico.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arecibo_message)

🕊️ 1973 – U.S. president Richard Nixon signs the Trans-Alaska Pipeline Authorization Act into law, authorizing the construction of the Alaska Pipeline.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Nixon)

⚔️ 1871 – The National Rifle Association of America receives its charter from New York State.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Rifle_Association)

🏰 1959 – Aeroflot Flight 315 crashes on approach to Lviv Airport, killing all 40 people on board.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeroflot_Flight_315_(1959))
