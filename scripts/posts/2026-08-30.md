# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 70 – Titus ends the siege of Jerusalem after destroying Herod's Temple.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_70)

🏰 1057 – Elderly Byzantine Emperor Michael VI Bringas abdicates after just one year on the throne.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Byzantine_emperors)

📜 1282 – Peter III of Aragon lands at Trapani to intervene in the War of the Sicilian Vespers.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_III_of_Aragon)

🛡️ 1363 – The five-week Battle of Lake Poyang begins, in which the forces of two Chinese rebel leaders (Chen Youliang and Zhu Yuanzhang) meet to decide who will supplant the Yuan dynasty.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Lake_Poyang)

⚓ 1464 – Paul II succeeds Pius II as pope of the Catholic Church.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Paul_II)

🚀 1535 – With the papal bull Eius qui immobilis, Pope Paul III excommunicates King Henry VIII of England from the Catholic Church for approving the Acts of Supremacy, although the bull is likely never published.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papal_bull)

💼 1721 – The Great Northern War between Sweden and Russia ends in the Treaty of Nystad.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Northern_War)

🎖️ 1896 – Philippine Revolution: After Spanish victory in the Battle of San Juan del Monte, eight provinces in the Philippines are declared under martial law by the Spanish Governor-General Ramón Blanco y Erenas.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Revolution)

📡 1992 – The 11-day Ruby Ridge standoff ends with Randy Weaver surrendering to federal authorities.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruby_Ridge_standoff)

🏙️ 1917 – Vietnamese prison guards led by Trịnh Văn Cấn mutiny at the Thái Nguyên penitentiary against local French authority.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam)

⚙️ 1808 – The convention of Cintra allows the defeated French troops to be transported out of Iberia on British ships, including their loot.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convention_of_Cintra)

💥 2002 – Rico Linhas Aéreas Flight 4823 crashes on approach to Rio Branco International Airport, killing 23 of the 31 people on board.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rico_Linhas_A%C3%A9reas_Flight_4823)

🕊️ 2021 – The last remaining American troops leave Afghanistan, ending U.S. involvement in the war.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%932021_US_troop_withdrawal_from_Afghanistan)

⚔️ 2008 – A Conviasa Boeing 737 crashes into Illiniza Volcano in Ecuador, killing all three people on board.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conviasa)

🏰 1909 – Burgess Shale fossils are discovered by Charles Doolittle Walcott.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burgess_Shale)
