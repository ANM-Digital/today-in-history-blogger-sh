# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -685 – Spring and Autumn period: Battle of Qianshi: Upon the death of the previous Duke of Qi, Gongsun Wuzhi, Duke Zhuang of Lu sends an army into the Duchy of Qi to install the exiled Qi prince Gongzi Jiu as the new Duke of Qi – but is defeated at Qianshi by Jiu's brother and rival claimant, the newly inaugurated Duke Huan of Qi.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_and_Autumn_period)

🏰 870 – Treaty of Meerssen: King Louis the German and his half-brother Charles the Bald partition the Middle Frankish Kingdom into two larger east and west divisions.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Meerssen)

📜 1164 – Hassan II of Alamut proclaims the qiyāma, according to which Ismaili practice would change from traditional islamic practice.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_II_of_Alamut)

🛡️ 1220 – Sweden is defeated by Estonian tribes in the Battle of Lihula.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonians)

⚓ 1503 – King James IV of Scotland marries Margaret Tudor, daughter of King Henry VII of England at Holyrood Abbey in Edinburgh, Scotland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_IV)

🚀 1647 – The Irish Confederate Wars and Wars of the Three Kingdoms: Battle of Dungan's Hill: English Parliamentary forces defeat Irish forces.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Confederate_Wars)

💼 1709 – Bartolomeu de Gusmão demonstrates the lifting power of hot air in an audience before the king of Portugal in Lisbon, Portugal.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolomeu_de_Gusm%C3%A3o)

🎖️ 1844 – The Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, headed by Brigham Young, is reaffirmed as the leading body of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (LDS Church).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quorum_of_the_Twelve)

📡 1956 – Marcinelle mining disaster in Belgium. 262 coal miners, including a substantial number of Italian migrant workers, were killed in one of the largest mining accidents in Belgian history.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcinelle_mining_disaster)

🏙️ 1942 – Quit India Movement is launched in India against the British rule in response to Mohandas Gandhi's call for swaraj or complete independence.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quit_India_Movement)

⚙️ 1863 – Tennessee Military Governor Andrew Johnson frees his personal slaves in Greeneville, Tennessee despite them being exempt from the Emancipation Proclamation, now commemorated as Emancipation Day in the state.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee)

💥 2013 – A suicide bombing at a funeral in the Pakistani city of Quetta kills at least 31 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_2013_Quetta_bombing)

🕊️ 1969 – At a zebra crossing in London, photographer Iain Macmillan takes the iconic photo that becomes the cover image of the Beatles' album Abbey Road.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zebra_crossing)

⚔️ 1940 – The "Aufbau Ost" directive for the mobilization of German forces for the invasion of the Soviet Union is signed by Wilhelm Keitel.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aufbau_Ost_(1940))

🏰 2010 – China Floods: A mudslide in Zhugqu County, Gansu, China, kills more than 1,400 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_China_floods)
