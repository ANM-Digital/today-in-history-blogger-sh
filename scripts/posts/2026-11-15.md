# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 655 – Battle of the Winwaed: Penda of Mercia is defeated by Oswiu of Northumbria.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Winwaed)

🏰 1315 – Growth of the Old Swiss Confederacy: The Schweizer Eidgenossenschaft ambushes the army of Leopold I in the Battle of Morgarten.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Growth_of_the_Old_Swiss_Confederacy)

📜 1532 – Spanish conquest of the Inca Empire: Commanded by Francisco Pizarro, Spanish conquistadors under Hernando de Soto meet Incan Emperor Atahualpa for the first time outside Cajamarca, arranging for a meeting in the city plaza the following day.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_conquest_of_the_Inca_Empire)

🛡️ 1688 – Prince William III of Orange lands a Dutch army at Brixham, beginning the 1688 invasion of England to challenge the rule of King James II and setting in motion the events that would lead to the Glorious Revolution.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_III_of_England)

⚓ 1705 – Rákóczi's War of Independence: The Habsburg Empire and Denmark win a military victory over the Kurucs from Hungary in the Battle of Zsibó.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%A1k%C3%B3czi's_War_of_Independence)

🚀 1978 – A chartered Douglas DC-8 crashes near Colombo, Sri Lanka, killing 183.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_DC-8)

💼 1985 – A research assistant is injured when a package from the Unabomber addressed to a University of Michigan professor explodes.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Kaczynski)

🎖️ 1928 – The RNLI lifeboat Mary Stanford capsizes in Rye Harbour with the loss of the entire 17-man crew.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_National_Lifeboat_Institution)

📡 1990 – Space Shuttle Atlantis is launched on STS-38, a classified mission for the Department of Defense.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Shuttle_Atlantis)

🏙️ 1966 – Project Gemini: Gemini 12 completes the program's final mission, when it splashes down safely in the Atlantic Ocean.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Gemini)

⚙️ 1842 – A slave revolt in the Cherokee Nation commences.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1842_Slave_Revolt_in_the_Cherokee_Nation)

💥 1969 – Cold War: The Soviet submarine K-19 collides with the American submarine USS Gato in the Barents Sea.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_War)

🕊️ 2017 – A flood a few miles outside of Athens results in the death of 25 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_West_Attica_floods)

⚔️ 1917 – Eduskunta declares itself the supreme state power of Finland, prompting its declaration of independence and secession from Russia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Finland)

🏰 1979 – A package from Unabomber Ted Kaczynski begins smoking in the cargo hold of a flight from Chicago to Washington, D.C., forcing the plane to make an emergency landing.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Kaczynski)
