# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 610 – Heraclius arrives at Constantinople, kills Byzantine Emperor Phocas, and becomes emperor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heraclius)

🏰 816 – King Louis the Pious is crowned emperor of the Holy Roman Empire by the Pope.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_the_Pious)

📜 1143 – With the signing of the Treaty of Zamora, King Alfonso VII of León and Castile recognises Portugal as a Kingdom.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Zamora)

🛡️ 1450 – Louis IX, Duke of Bavaria expels Jews from his jurisdiction.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_IX_of_Bavaria)

⚓ 1607 – Assassins attempt to kill Venetian statesman and scientist Paolo Sarpi.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Sarpi)

🚀 1789 – French Revolution: The Women's March on Versailles effectively terminates royal authority.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_March_on_Versailles)

💼 1905 – The Wright brothers pilot the Wright Flyer III in a new world record flight of 24 miles in 39 minutes.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wright_brothers)

🎖️ 1963 – The United States suspends the Commercial Import Program in response to repression of the Buddhist majority by the regime of President Ngo Dinh Diem.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commercial_Import_Program)

📡 2000 – Mass demonstrations in Serbia force the resignation of Slobodan Milošević.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overthrow_of_Slobodan_Milo%C5%A1evi%C4%87)

🏙️ 1982 – Tylenol products are recalled after bottles in Chicago laced with cyanide cause seven deaths.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tylenol)

⚙️ 1984 – Marc Garneau becomes the first Canadian in space.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Garneau)

💥 1947 – President Truman makes the first televised Oval Office address.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Oval_Office_Address)

🕊️ 1936 – The Jarrow March sets off for London.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarrow_March)

⚔️ 1962 – The first Beatles single "Love Me Do" is released in Britain.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beatles)

🏰 2011 – In the Mekong River massacre, two Chinese cargo boats are hijacked and 13 crew members murdered.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mekong_River_massacre)
