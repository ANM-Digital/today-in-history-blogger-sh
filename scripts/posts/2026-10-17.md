# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 690 – Empress Wu Zetian establishes the Zhou Dynasty of China.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wu_Zetian)

🏰 1091 – London tornado of 1091: A tornado thought to be of strength T8/F4 strikes the heart of London.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_tornado_of_1091)

📜 1173 – English Crown forces under Richard de Lucy defeat rebels under Robert de Beaumont in the battle of Fornham.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_de_Lucy)

🛡️ 1346 – The English capture King David II of Scotland at Neville's Cross and imprison him for eleven years.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_II_of_Scotland)

⚓ 1448 – The second Battle of Kosovo begins between Hungarian forces under John Hunyadi and Ottoman forces under Murad II.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kosovo_(1448))

🚀 1534 – Anti-Catholic posters appear in Paris and other cities supporting Huldrych Zwingli's position on the Mass.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affair_of_the_Placards)

💼 1604 – Kepler's Supernova is observed in the constellation of Ophiuchus.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kepler's_Supernova)

🎖️ 1713 – Great Northern War: Russia defeats Sweden in the Battle of Kostianvirta in Pälkäne.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Northern_War)

📡 1850 – Riots start, which lead to a massacre in Aleppo.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massacre_of_Aleppo_(1850))

🏙️ 1992 – Having gone to the wrong house, Japanese student Yoshihiro Hattori is killed by the homeowner in Baton Rouge, Louisiana.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Yoshihiro_Hattori)

⚙️ 2004 – A fire that lasted over 15 hours destroyed almost one third of the East Tower of the Parque Central Urban Complex in Caracas, Venezuela.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parque_Central_Complex_fire)

💥 1989 – The 6.9 Mw Loma Prieta earthquake shakes the San Francisco Bay Area and the Central Coast, killing 63.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seismic_magnitude_scales)

🕊️ 1989 – The East German Politburo votes to remove Erich Honecker from his role as General Secretary.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erich_Honecker)

⚔️ 1919 – Leeds United F.C. founded at Salem Chapel, Holbeck after the winding up of Leeds City F.C. for making illegal payments to players during World War I. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_F.C.)

🏰 2018 – The recreational use of cannabis is legalized in Canada.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis_Act_(Canada))
