# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -371 – The Battle of Leuctra shatters Sparta's reputation of military invincibility.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Leuctra)

🏰 -83 – The Temple of Jupiter on the Capitoline Hill in Rome is burned down and the Sibylline books of prophecy destroyed with it.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_of_Jupiter_Optimus_Maximus)

📜 640 – Battle of Heliopolis: The Muslim Arab army under 'Amr ibn al-'As defeat the Byzantine forces near Heliopolis (Egypt).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Heliopolis)

🛡️ 1253 – Mindaugas is crowned King of Lithuania.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindaugas)

⚓ 1348 – Pope Clement VI issues a papal bull protecting the Jews accused of having caused the Black Death.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Clement_VI)

🚀 1411 – Ming China's Admiral Zheng He returns to Nanjing after the third treasure voyage and presents the Sinhalese king, captured during the Ming–Kotte War, to the Yongle Emperor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ming_dynasty)

💼 1536 – Thomas More is executed after having been condemned on perjured evidence.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_More)

🎖️ 1614 – Raid on Żejtun: The south east of Malta, and the town of Żejtun, suffer a raid from Ottoman forces. This was the last unsuccessful attempt by the Ottomans to conquer the island of Malta.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raid_on_%C5%BBejtun)

📡 1751 – Pope Benedict XIV suppresses the Patriarchate of Aquileia and establishes from its territory the Archdiocese of Udine and Gorizia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Benedict_XIV)

🏙️ 2021 – An Antonov An-26 operating as Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky Air Flight 251 crashes on approach to Palana Airport, killing all 28 aboard.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonov_An-26)

⚙️ 1947 – Referendum held in Sylhet to decide its fate in the Partition of India.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1947_Sylhet_referendum)

💥 1892 – Three thousand eight hundred striking steelworkers engage in a day-long battle with Pinkerton agents during the Homestead Strike, leaving ten dead and dozens wounded.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike_action)

🕊️ 1957 – John Lennon and Paul McCartney meet for the first time, as teenagers at Woolton Fete, three years before forming the Beatles.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Lennon)

⚔️ 2022 – The Georgia Guidestones, a monument in the United States, are heavily damaged in a bombing, and are dismantled later the same day.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Guidestones)

🏰 1997 – The Troubles: In response to the Drumcree dispute, five days of mass protests, riots and gun battles begin in Irish nationalist districts of Northern Ireland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Troubles)
