# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 380 – Theodosius I makes his adventus, or formal entry, into Constantinople.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodosius_I)

🏰 496 – Election of pope Anastasius II following the death of pope Gelasius I a few days prior.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Anastasius_II)

📜 847 – An earthquake hits Syria, causing multiple casualties and damages in Antioch, Damascus and Mosul.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/847_Damascus_earthquake)

🛡️ 1190 – Conrad of Montferrat becomes King of Jerusalem upon his marriage to Isabella I of Jerusalem.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conrad_of_Montferrat)

⚓ 1202 – The army of the Fourth Crusade take Zara for Venice in order to pay for the debts to the Venetians.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1221)

🚀 1331 – David II is annointed as king of Scotland, but has to spent most of his reign outside Scotland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_II_of_Scotland)

💼 1429 – Hundred Years' War: Joan of Arc unsuccessfully besieges La Charité.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hundred_Years'_War)

🎖️ 1542 – Battle of Solway Moss: An English army defeats a much larger Scottish force near the River Esk in Dumfries and Galloway.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Solway_Moss)

📡 1642 – Abel Tasman becomes the first European to discover the island Van Diemen's Land (later renamed Tasmania).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abel_Tasman)

🏙️ 1750 – Tarabai, regent of the Maratha Empire, imprisons Rajaram II of Satara for refusing to remove Balaji Baji Rao from the post of peshwa.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarabai)

⚙️ 1835 – The Texas Provincial Government authorizes the creation of a horse-mounted police force called the Texas Rangers (which is now the Texas Ranger Division of the Texas Department of Public Safety).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Texas_Ranger_Division)

💥 1965 – Joseph-Désiré Mobutu seizes power in the Democratic Republic of the Congo and becomes President; he rules the country (which he renames Zaire in 1971) for over 30 years, until being overthrown by rebels in 1997.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobutu_Sese_Seko)

🕊️ 2017 – A terrorist attack on a Mosque in Al-Rawda, North Sinai, Egypt kills 311 people and injures 128.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Sinai_mosque_attack)

⚔️ 1973 – A national speed limit is imposed on the Autobahn in Germany because of the 1973 oil crisis. The speed limit lasts only four months.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autobahn)

🏰 1932 – In Washington, D.C., the FBI Scientific Crime Detection Laboratory (better known as the FBI Crime Lab) officially opens.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Bureau_of_Investigation)
