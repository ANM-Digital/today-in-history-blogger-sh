# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 19 – The Roman general Germanicus dies near Antioch. He was convinced that the mysterious illness that ended in his death was a result of poisoning by the Syrian governor Gnaeus Calpurnius Piso, whom he had ordered to leave the province.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10s)

🏰 415 – The second church structure of Hagia Sophia was inaugurated.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagia_Sophia)

📜 680 – The Battle of Karbala marks the martyrdom of Husayn ibn Ali.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Karbala)

🛡️ 732 – Charles Martel's forces defeat an Umayyad army near Tours, France.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Martel)

⚓ 1471 – Sten Sture the Elder, the Regent of Sweden, with the help of farmers and miners, repels an attack by King Christian I of Denmark.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sten_Sture_the_Elder)

🚀 1507 – The Kingdom of Hormuz recognises the sovereignty of the Portuguese king following a battle with the forces of Afonso de Albuquerque.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hormuz)

💼 1760 – In a treaty with the Dutch colonial authorities, the Ndyuka people of Suriname – descended from escaped slaves – gain territorial autonomy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ndyuka_people)

🎖️ 1957 – U.S. President Dwight D. Eisenhower apologizes to Ghanaian finance minister Komla Agbeli Gbedemah after he is refused service in a Delaware restaurant.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwight_D._Eisenhower)

📡 2018 – The National Fire and Rescue Administration is founded, replacing the China Fire Services [zh] and the People's Armed Police Forestry Corps [zh] as China's primary firefighting agency.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Fire_and_Rescue_Administration)

🏙️ 1928 – Chiang Kai-shek becomes Chairman of the Republic of China.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiang_Kai-shek)

⚙️ 1903 – The Women's Social and Political Union is founded in support of the enfranchisement of British women.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Social_and_Political_Union)

💥 1963 – The Partial Nuclear Test Ban Treaty comes into effect.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_Nuclear_Test_Ban_Treaty)

🕊️ 1920 – The Carinthian plebiscite determines that the larger part of the Duchy of Carinthia should remain part of Austria.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1920_Carinthian_plebiscite)

⚔️ 1964 – The Tokyo Summer Olympics opening ceremony is the first to be relayed live by satellites.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964_Summer_Olympics)

🏰 1967 – The Outer Space Treaty comes into force.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_Space_Treaty)
