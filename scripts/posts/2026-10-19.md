# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -202 – Second Punic War: At the Battle of Zama, Roman legions under Scipio Africanus defeat Hannibal Barca, leader of the army defending Carthage.[citation needed]. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Punic_War)

🏰 439 – The Vandals, led by King Gaiseric, take Carthage in North Africa in a surprise attack in breach of treaty.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaiseric)

📜 615 – Consecration of Pope Adeodatus I following the imperial approval of his election.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Adeodatus_I)

🛡️ 1386 – The Universität Heidelberg holds its first lecture, making it the oldest German university.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University)

⚓ 1453 – Hundred Years' War: Three months after the Battle of Castillon, England loses its last possessions in southern France.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hundred_Years'_War)

🚀 1512 – Martin Luther becomes a doctor of theology.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther)

💼 1649 – New Ross town in Ireland surrenders to Oliver Cromwell.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Ross)

🎖️ 1781 – American Revolutionary War: The siege of Yorktown comes to an end with the British surrender.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War)

📡 1950 – China defeats the Tibetan Army at Chambo.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Chamdo)

🏙️ 1988 – The British government imposes a broadcasting ban on television and radio interviews with members of Sinn Féin and eleven Irish republican and Ulster loyalist paramilitary groups.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988%E2%80%931994_British_broadcasting_voice_restrictions)

⚙️ 1805 – War of the Third Coalition: Austrian General Mack surrenders his army to Napoleon at the Battle of Ulm.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Ulm)

💥 1921 – The Portuguese Prime Minister and several officials are murdered in the Bloody Night coup.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloody_Night_(Lisbon%2C_1921))

🕊️ 1866 – In accordance with the Treaty of Vienna, Austria cedes Veneto and Mantua to France, which immediately awards them to Italy in exchange for the earlier Italian acquiescence to the French annexation of Savoy and Nice.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Vienna_(1866))

⚔️ 1813 – War of the Sixth Coalition: Napoleon is forced to retreat from Germany after the Battle of Leipzig.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Leipzig)

🏰 1900 – Max Planck discovers Planck's law of black-body radiation.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck)
