# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -3114 – The Mesoamerican Long Count calendar, used by several pre-Columbian Mesoamerican civilizations, notably the Maya, begins.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/32nd_century_BC)

🏰 -2492 – Traditional date of the defeat of Bel by Hayk, progenitor and founder of the Armenian nation.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/25th_century_BC)

📜 106 – The south-western part of Dacia (modern Romania) becomes a Roman province: Roman Dacia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_106)

🛡️ 355 – Claudius Silvanus, accused of treason, proclaims himself Roman Emperor against Constantius II.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silvanus_(magister_peditum))

⚓ 490 – Battle of Adda: The Goths under Theodoric the Great and his ally Alaric II defeat the forces of Odoacer on the Adda River, near Milan.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goths)

🚀 923 – The Qarmatians of Bahrayn capture and pillage the city of Basra.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qarmatians)

💼 1315 – The Great Famine of Europe becomes so dire that even the king of England has difficulties buying bread for himself and his entourage.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Famine_of_1315%E2%80%931317)

🎖️ 1473 – The Battle of Otlukbeli: Mehmed the Conqueror of the Ottoman Empire decisively defeats Uzun Hassan of Aq Qoyunlu.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Otlukbeli)

📡 1675 – Franco-Dutch War: Forces of the Holy Roman Empire defeat the French in the Battle of Konzer Brücke.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco-Dutch_War)

🏙️ 1786 – Captain Francis Light establishes the British colony of Penang in Malaysia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Light)

⚙️ 2003 – NATO takes over command of the peacekeeping force in Afghanistan, marking its first major operation outside Europe in its 54-year-history.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NATO)

💥 1918 – World War I: The Battle of Amiens ends.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I)

🕊️ 1934 – The first civilian prisoners arrive at the Federal prison on Alcatraz Island.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcatraz_Federal_Penitentiary)

⚔️ 1975 – East Timor: Governor Mário Lemos Pires of Portuguese Timor abandons the capital Dili, following a coup by the Timorese Democratic Union (UDT) and the outbreak of civil war between UDT and Fretilin.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timor-Leste)

🏰 2012 – At least 306 people are killed and 3,000 others injured in a pair of earthquakes near Tabriz, Iran.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_East_Azerbaijan_earthquakes)
