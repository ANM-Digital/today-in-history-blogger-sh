# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 498 – After the death of Anastasius II, Symmachus is elected Pope in the Lateran Palace, while Laurentius is elected Pope in Santa Maria Maggiore.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Anastasius_II)

🏰 545 – During the Three-Chapter Controversy, Pope Vigilius is abducted on order of emperor Justinian I in order to force him to agree with Byzantine imperial religious policy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-Chapter_Controversy)

📜 602 – Emperor Maurice loses control over Constantinople and flees the city.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_(emperor))

🛡️ 845 – The first duke of Brittany, Nominoe, defeats the Frankish king Charles the Bald at the Battle of Ballon near Redon.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brittany)

⚓ 1210 – The Castle of Termes falls to Simon de Montfort after a four-month siege during the Albigensian Crusade.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C3%A2teau_de_Termes)

🚀 1307 – Pope Clement V issues the papal bull Pastoralis Praeeminentiae which instructed all Christian monarchs in Europe to arrest all Templars and seize their assets.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Clement_V)

💼 1574 – Spanish navigator Juan Fernández discovers islands now known as the Juan Fernández Islands off Chile.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Fern%C3%A1ndez_(explorer))

🎖️ 1635 – Dutch colonial forces on Taiwan launch a pacification campaign against native villages, resulting in Dutch control of the middle and south of the island.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Formosa)

📡 1718 – Royal Navy Lieutenant Robert Maynard attacks and boards the vessels of the British pirate Edward Teach (best known as "Blackbeard") off the coast of North Carolina. The casualties on both sides include Maynard's first officer Mister Hyde and Teach himself.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Navy)

🏙️ 1943 – World War II: Cairo Conference: U.S. President Franklin D. Roosevelt, British Prime Minister Winston Churchill, and Chinese Premier Chiang Kai-shek meet in Cairo, Egypt, to discuss ways to defeat Japan.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairo_Conference)

⚙️ 2022 – A shooting at a Walmart in Chesapeake, Virginia leaves seven workers dead, including the shooter, and four others injured.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Chesapeake_shooting)

💥 2014 – While playing with a toy gun in Cleveland, 12-year-old African American Tamir Rice is killed by a white police officer.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Americans)

🕊️ 1987 – The Max Headroom signal hijacking incident takes place, in which a pirate broadcast interrupts television broadcasts in Chicago.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Headroom_signal_hijackings)

⚔️ 1942 – World War II: Battle of Stalingrad: General Friedrich Paulus sends Adolf Hitler a telegram saying that the German 6th Army is surrounded.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Stalingrad)

🏰 2010 – During the Cambodian water festival, a stampede in Koh Pich, Phnom Penh, kills 347 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bon_Om_Touk)
