# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 70 – Siege of Jerusalem: Titus, son of emperor Vespasian, storms the Fortress of Antonia north of the Temple Mount. The Roman army is drawn into street fights with the Zealots.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_70)

🏰 514 – Consecration of pope Hormisdas following the death of pope Symmachus the previous day.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Hormisdas)

📜 792 – Kardam of Bulgaria defeats Byzantine Emperor Constantine VI at the Battle of Marcellae.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kardam_of_Bulgaria)

🛡️ 911 – Rollo lays siege to Chartres.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_911)

⚓ 1189 – Richard I of England officially invested as Duke of Normandy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_I_of_England)

🚀 1230 – Treaty of San Germano is signed at San Germano between Holy Roman Emperor Frederick II and Pope Gregory IX. A Dominican named Guala is responsible for the negotiations.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_San_Germano)

💼 1398 – The Battle of Kellistown was fought on this day between the forces of the English led by Roger Mortimer, 4th Earl of March against the O'Byrnes and O'Tooles under the command of Art Óg mac Murchadha Caomhánach, the most powerful Chieftain in Leinster.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_people)

🎖️ 1592 – During the first Japanese invasion of Korea, Japanese forces led by Toyotomi Hideyoshi captured Pyongyang, although they were ultimately unable to hold it.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imjin_War)

📡 1705 – A fire in Oulu, Finland almost completely destroyed the fourth district, which covered the southern part of the city and was by far the largest of the city districts.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oulu)

🏙️ 1981 – Somali Airlines Flight 40 crashes in Balad, Somalia, killing 50 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_Airlines_Flight_40)

⚙️ 1951 – King Abdullah I of Jordan is assassinated by a Palestinian while attending Friday prayers in Jerusalem.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_I_of_Jordan)

💥 1936 – The Montreux Convention is signed in Switzerland, authorizing Turkey to fortify the Dardanelles and Bosphorus but guaranteeing free passage to ships of all nations in peacetime.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreux_Convention_Regarding_the_Regime_of_the_Straits)

🕊️ 2005 – The Civil Marriage Act legalizes same-sex marriage in Canada.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Marriage_Act)

⚔️ 1941 – Soviet leader Joseph Stalin consolidates the Commissariats of Home Affairs and National Security to form the NKVD and names Lavrentiy Beria its chief.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union)

🏰 1866 – Austro-Prussian War: Battle of Lissa: The Austrian Navy, led by Admiral Wilhelm von Tegetthoff, defeats the Italian Navy near the island of Vis in the Adriatic Sea.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austro-Prussian_War)
