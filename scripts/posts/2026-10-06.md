# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -105 – Cimbrian War: Defeat at the Battle of Arausio of the Roman army of the mid-Republic.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cimbrian_War)

🏰 -69 – Third Mithridatic War: The military of the Roman Republic subdues Armenia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Mithridatic_War)

📜 23 – Rebels decapitate Wang Mang, only Emperor of the Xin Dynasty,  after his capital was sacked during a peasant rebellion.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20s)

🛡️ 404 – Byzantine Empress Eudoxia dies from the miscarriage of her seventh pregnancy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_404)

⚓ 618 – Transition from Sui to Tang: Wang Shichong decisively defeats Li Mi at the Battle of Yanshi.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transition_from_Sui_to_Tang)

🚀 891 – Election of Pope Formosus following the death of pope Stephen V in the previous month.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Formosus)

💼 1539 – Spain's DeSoto expedition takes over the Apalachee capital of Anhaica for their winter quarters.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hernando_de_Soto)

🎖️ 1600 – Euridice, the earliest surviving opera, receives its première performance, beginning the Baroque period.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euridice_(Peri))

📡 1762 – Seven Years' War: The British capture Manila from Spain and occupy it.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Years'_War)

🏙️ 1944 – World War II: Units of the 1st Czechoslovak Army Corps enter Czechoslovakia during the Battle of the Dukla Pass.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_Czechoslovak_Army_Corps_in_the_Soviet_Union)

⚙️ 1915 – World War I: Entente forces land in Thessaloniki, to open the Macedonian front against the Central Powers.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allies_of_World_War_I)

💥 1976 – Cubana de Aviación Flight 455 is destroyed by two bombs, placed on board by an anti-Castro militant group.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubana_de_Aviaci%C3%B3n_Flight_455)

🕊️ 1898 – Phi Mu Alpha Sinfonia, the largest American music fraternity, is founded at the New England Conservatory of Music.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phi_Mu_Alpha_Sinfonia)

⚔️ 1908 – The Bosnian crisis erupts when Austria-Hungary formally annexes Bosnia and Herzegovina.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnian_Crisis)

🏰 1903 – The High Court of Australia sits for the first time.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Court_of_Australia)
