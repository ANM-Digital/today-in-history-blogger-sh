# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 118 – Hadrian, who became emperor a year previously on Trajan's death, makes his entry into Rome.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_118)

🏰 381 – The end of the First Council of Christian bishops convened in Constantinople by the Roman emperor Theodosius I.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Council_of_Constantinople)

📜 455 – The elderly noble Avitus is proclaimed Roman emperor by Gothic king Theoderic II in Toulouse.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avitus)

🛡️ 551 – A major earthquake strikes Beirut, triggering a devastating tsunami that affects the coastal towns of Byzantine Phoenicia, causing thousands of deaths.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/551_Beirut_earthquake)

⚓ 660 – Korean forces under general Kim Yu-sin of Silla defeat the army of Baekje in the Battle of Hwangsanbeol.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Yu-sin)

🚀 869 – The 8.4–9.0 MwSanriku earthquake strikes the area around Sendai in northern Honshu, Japan. Inundation from the tsunami extended several kilometers inland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/869_J%C5%8Dgan_earthquake)

💼 969 – The Fatimid general Jawhar leads the Friday prayer in Fustat in the name of Caliph al-Mu'izz li-Din Allah, thereby symbolically completing the Fatimid conquest of Egypt.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatimid_Caliphate)

🎖️ 1357 – Emperor Charles IV assists in laying the foundation stone of Charles Bridge in Prague.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_IV%2C_Holy_Roman_Emperor)

📡 1401 – Timur attacks the Jalairid Sultanate and destroys Baghdad.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timur)

🏙️ 1540 – King Henry VIII of England annuls his marriage to his fourth wife, Anne of Cleves.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_VIII)

⚙️ 1609 – Bohemia is granted freedom of religion through the Letter of Majesty by the Holy Roman Emperor, Rudolf II.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohemia)

💥 1701 – A Bourbon force under Nicolas Catinat withdraws from a smaller Habsburg force under Prince Eugene of Savoy in the Battle of Carpi.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Bourbon)

🕊️ 1815 – Charles Maurice de Talleyrand-Périgord becomes the first Prime Minister of France.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Maurice_de_Talleyrand-P%C3%A9rigord)

⚔️ 1932 – The state of São Paulo revolts against the Brazilian Federal Government, starting the Constitutionalist Revolution.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Paulo_(state))

🏰 1926 – Chiang Kai-shek accepts the post of commander-in-chief of the National Revolutionary Army, marking the beginning of the Northern Expedition to unite China under the rule of the Nationalist government.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiang_Kai-shek)
