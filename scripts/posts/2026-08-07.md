# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 461 – Roman Emperor Majorian is beheaded near the river Iria in north-west Italy following his arrest and deposition by the magister militum Ricimer.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majorian)

🏰 610 – Byzantine–Sasanian War of 602–628: Sassanid troops under general Shahrbaraz conquer the town of Zenobia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine%E2%80%93Sasanian_War_of_602%E2%80%93628)

📜 768 – Pope Stephen III is elected to office, and quickly seeks Frankish protection against the Lombard threat, since the Byzantine Empire is no longer able to help.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Stephen_III)

🛡️ 936 – Coronation of King Otto I of Germany.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_the_Great)

⚓ 1461 – The Ming dynasty Chinese military general Cao Qin stages a coup against the Tianshun Emperor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ming_dynasty)

🚀 1679 – The brigantine Le Griffon becomes the first ship to sail the upper Great Lakes of North America.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigantine)

💼 1714 – The Battle of Gangut: The first important victory of the Russian Navy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Gangut)

🎖️ 1962 – Canadian-born American pharmacologist Frances Oldham Kelsey is awarded the U.S. President's Award for Distinguished Federal Civilian Service for her refusal to authorize thalidomide.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frances_Oldham_Kelsey)

📡 1947 – The Bombay Municipal Corporation formally takes over the Bombay Electric Supply and Transport (BEST).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brihanmumbai_Municipal_Corporation)

🏙️ 1989 – The National Cold Fusion Institute opened in Salt Lake City.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cold_Fusion_Institute)

⚙️ 1989 – U.S. Congressman Mickey Leland (D-TX) and 15 others die in a plane crash in Ethiopia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mickey_Leland)

💥 1930 – The last confirmed lynching of black people in the Northern United States occurs in Marion, Indiana; two men, Thomas Shipp and Abram Smith, are murdered.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynching)

🕊️ 1927 – The Peace Bridge opens between Fort Erie, Ontario and Buffalo, New York.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_Bridge)

⚔️ 2020 – Air India Express Flight 1344 overshoots the runway at Calicut International Airport in the Malappuram district of Kerala, India, and crashes, killing 21 of the 190 people on board.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_India_Express_Flight_1344)

🏰 1970 – California judge Harold Haley is taken hostage in his courtroom and killed during an effort to free George Jackson from police custody.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Haley)
