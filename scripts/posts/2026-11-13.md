# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 1002 – English king Æthelred II orders the killing of all Danes in England, known today as the St. Brice's Day massacre.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%86thelred_the_Unready)

🏰 1160 – Louis VII of France marries Adela of Champagne.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_VII_of_France)

📜 1642 – First English Civil War: Battle of Turnham Green: The Royalist forces withdraw in the face of the Parliamentarian army and fail to take London.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_English_Civil_War)

🛡️ 1715 – Jacobite rising in Scotland: Battle of Sheriffmuir: The forces of the Kingdom of Great Britain halt the Jacobite advance, although the action is inconclusive.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobite_rising_of_1715)

⚓ 1992 – The High Court of Australia rules in Dietrich v The Queen that although there is no absolute right to have publicly funded counsel, in most circumstances a judge should grant any request for an adjournment or stay when an accused is unrepresented.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Court_of_Australia)

🚀 1916 – World War I: Prime Minister of Australia Billy Hughes is expelled from the Labor Party over his support for conscription.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I)

💼 1809 – A British flotilla arrived at Ras Al Khaimah and launched an amphibious assault on the town, as a part of the Persian Gulf campaign of 1809.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ras_Al_Khaimah)

🎖️ 1851 – The Denny Party lands at Alki Point, before moving to the other side of Elliott Bay to what would become Seattle.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denny_Party)

📡 1893 – 13 November stabbing is committed by Léon Léauthier during the Ère des attentats. This is an influential event in the birth of modern terrorism.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13_November_1893_stabbing)

🏙️ 1914 – Zaian War: Berber tribesmen inflict the heaviest defeat of French forces in Morocco at the Battle of El Herri.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaian_War)

⚙️ 1918 – World War I: Allied troops occupy Constantinople, the capital of the Ottoman Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allies_of_World_War_I)

💥 1982 – The Vietnam Veterans Memorial is dedicated in Washington, D.C. after a march to its site by thousands of Vietnam War veterans.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_Veterans_Memorial)

🕊️ 1966 – In response to Fatah raids against Israelis near the West Bank border, Israel launches an attack on the village of As-Samu.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatah)

⚔️ 1982 – Ray Mancini defeats Duk Koo Kim in a boxing match held in Las Vegas. Kim's subsequent death (on November 18) leads to significant changes in the sport.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Mancini)

🏰 1956 – The Supreme Court of the United States affirmed a lower court ruling that invalidated Alabama laws requiring segregated buses, thus ending the Montgomery bus boycott.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_the_United_States)
