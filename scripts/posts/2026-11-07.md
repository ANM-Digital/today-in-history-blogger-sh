# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 335 – Athanasius, 20th pope of Alexandria, is banished to Trier on the charge that he prevented a grain fleet from sailing to Constantinople.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athanasius_of_Alexandria)

🏰 680 – The Sixth Ecumenical Council commences in Constantinople.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Council_of_Constantinople)

📜 921 – Treaty of Bonn: The Frankish kings Charles the Simple and Henry the Fowler sign a peace treaty or 'pact of friendship' (amicitia) to recognize their borders along the Rhine.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Bonn)

🛡️ 1185 – The Byzantine army under Alexios Branas defeats the Norman army in the battle of Demetritzes and captures its leaders counts Baldwin and Richard of Acerra.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexios_Branas)

⚓ 1426 – Lam Sơn uprising: Lam Sơn rebels emerge victorious against the Ming army in the Battle of Tốt Động – Chúc Động taking place in Đông Quan, in now Hanoi.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lam_S%C6%A1n_uprising)

🚀 1504 – Christopher Columbus returns from his fourth and last voyage.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Columbus)

💼 1619 – Elizabeth Stuart is crowned Queen of Bohemia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Stuart%2C_Queen_of_Bohemia)

🎖️ 1723 – O Ewigkeit, du Donnerwort, BWV 60, a dialogue cantata by Johann Sebastian Bach for Leipzig, was first performed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O_Ewigkeit%2C_du_Donnerwort%2C_BWV_60)

📡 1917 – The October Revolution, which gets its name from the Julian calendar date of 25 October, occurs, according to the Gregorian calendar; on this date, the Bolsheviks storm the Winter Palace.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_Revolution)

🏙️ 1991 – Magic Johnson announces that he is HIV-positive and retires from the NBA.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_Johnson)

⚙️ 1885 – The completion of Canada's first transcontinental railway is symbolized by the Last Spike ceremony at Craigellachie, British Columbia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_spike_(Canadian_Pacific_Railway))

💥 1916 – Boston Elevated Railway Company's streetcar No. 393 smashes through the warning gates of the open Summer Street drawbridge in Boston, Massachusetts, plunging into the frigid waters of Fort Point Channel, killing 46 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Elevated_Railway)

🕊️ 2004 – Iraq War: The interim government of Iraq calls for a 60-day state of emergency as U.S. forces storm the insurgent stronghold of Fallujah.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq_War)

⚔️ 1914 – The German colony of Kiaochow Bay and its centre at Tsingtao are captured by Japanese forces. This leaves Germany without a far east base.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Empire)

🏰 1972 – United States presidential election: U.S. President Richard Nixon is re-elected in the largest landslide victory at the time.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_United_States_presidential_election)
