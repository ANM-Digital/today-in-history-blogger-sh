# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 694 – At the Seventeenth Council of Toledo, Egica, a king of the Visigoths of Hispania, accuses Jews of aiding Muslims, sentencing all Jews to slavery.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seventeenth_Council_of_Toledo)

🏰 1180 – The Battle of Fujigawa: Minamoto forces (30,000 men) under Minamoto no Yoritomo defeat Taira no Koremori during a night attack near the Fuji River but he escapes safely with the routed army.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Fujigawa)

📜 1225 – Frederick II, Holy Roman Emperor, marries Yolanda of Jerusalem in Brindisi.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_II%2C_Holy_Roman_Emperor)

🛡️ 1307 – Knights Templar officer Hugues de Pairaud is forced to confess during the Trials of the Knights Templar. He was persecuted on the charges of false idolism and sodomy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knights_Templar)

⚓ 1431 – The Battle of Ilava: The Hungarians defeat the Hussite army.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Ilava)

🚀 1520 – More than 50 people are sentenced and executed in the Stockholm Bloodbath.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_Bloodbath)

💼 1620 – The Bohemian King Frederick I flees Prague to Vratislav one day after the defeat of his troops in the Battle of White Mountain.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wroc%C5%82aw)

🎖️ 1719 – In a treaty between Sweden and Hanover at the close of the Great Northern War, Sweden cedes the  Duchies of Bremen and Verden (in northern Germany) to Hanover.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaties_of_Stockholm_(Great_Northern_War))

📡 1926 – Paul Hindemith's Cardillac premieres at the Staatsoper in Dresden, conducted by Fritz Busch.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Hindemith)

🏙️ 1851 – Kentucky marshals abduct abolitionist minister Calvin Fairbank from Jeffersonville, Indiana, and take him to Kentucky to stand trial for helping a slave escape.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky)

⚙️ 1943 – An agreement for the founding of the United Nations Relief and Rehabilitation Administration is signed by 44 countries in the White House, Washington, D.C.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House)

💥 1998 – Capital punishment in the United Kingdom, already abolished for murder, is completely abolished for all remaining capital offences.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_punishment_in_the_United_Kingdom)

🕊️ 1887 – The United States receives rights to Pearl Harbor, Hawaii.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearl_Harbor)

⚔️ 1867 – The Tokugawa shogunate hands back power to the Emperor of Japan, starting the Meiji Restoration.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokugawa_shogunate)

🏰 1870 – The Battle of Coulmiers ends in a Pyrrhic victory for the French army during the Franco-German War of 1870.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Coulmiers)
