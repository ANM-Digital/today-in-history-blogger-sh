# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 961 – Al-Hakam II becomes caliph of Cordoba upon the death of his father Abd al-Rahman III.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Hakam_II)

🏰 1066 – Following the death of Harold II at the Battle of Hastings, Edgar the Ætheling is proclaimed King of England by the Witan; he is never crowned, and concedes power to William the Conqueror two months later.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Hastings)

📜 1211 – Battle of the Rhyndacus: The Latin emperor Henry of Flanders defeats the Nicaean emperor Theodore I Laskaris.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Rhyndacus_(1211))

🛡️ 1529 – The Siege of Vienna ends when Austria routs the invading Ottoman forces, ending its European expansion.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Vienna_(1529))

⚓ 1651 – Qing forces capture the island of Zhoushan. Zhu Yihai, Prince of Lu, resident of the island and regent of the Southern Ming, flees to Kinmen.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qing_dynasty)

🚀 1781 – The Battle of Raft Swamp marks the last battle fought in North Carolina during the American Revolutionary War with a Patriot victory. It occurred four days before the British surrender at Yorktown.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Raft_Swamp)

💼 1864 – American Civil War: The Union garrison of Glasgow, Missouri surrenders to Confederate forces.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War)

🎖️ 1970 – During the construction of Australia's West Gate Bridge, a span of the bridge falls and kills 35 workers. The incident is the country's worst industrial accident to this day.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Gate_Bridge)

📡 1939 – The New York Municipal Airport (later renamed LaGuardia Airport) is dedicated.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaGuardia_Airport)

🏙️ 1997 – The Cassini probe launches from Cape Canaveral on its way to Saturn.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassini%E2%80%93Huygens)

⚙️ 1888 – The "From Hell" letter allegedly sent by Jack the Ripper is received by investigators.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/From_Hell_letter)

💥 2013 – The 7.2 Mw Bohol earthquake strikes the Philippines. At least 215 were killed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seismic_magnitude_scales)

🕊️ 2016 – One hundred and ninety-seven nations amend the Montreal Protocol to include a phase-out of hydrofluorocarbons.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal_Protocol)

⚔️ 2018 – 13-year-old American girl, Jayme Closs, is kidnapped from her Barron, Wisconsin home after her parents were both murdered.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidnapping_of_Jayme_Closs)

🏰 1990 – Soviet Union leader Mikhail Gorbachev is awarded the Nobel Peace Prize for his efforts to lessen Cold War tensions and open up his nation.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gorbachev)
