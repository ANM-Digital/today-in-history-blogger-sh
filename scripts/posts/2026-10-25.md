# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 473 – Emperor Leo I acclaims his grandson Leo II as Caesar of the East Roman Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_I_(emperor))

🏰 1147 – Seljuk Turks defeat German crusaders under Conrad III at the Battle of Dorylaeum.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Dorylaeum_(1147))

📜 1415 – Hundred Years' War: Henry V of England, with his men-at-arms and archers, defeats the larger French army in the Battle of Agincourt.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Agincourt)

🛡️ 1616 – Dutch sea-captain Dirk Hartog makes the second recorded landfall by a European on Australian soil, at the later-named Dirk Hartog Island off the West Australian coast.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirk_Hartog)

⚓ 1747 – War of the Austrian Succession: A British fleet under Admiral Edward Hawke defeats the French at the Second Battle of Cape Finisterre.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Cape_Finisterre)

🚀 1989 – The first leg of the 1989 Supertaça Cândido de Oliveira is held at the Estádio da Luz in Lisbon, Portugal.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Superta%C3%A7a_C%C3%A2ndido_de_Oliveira)

💼 2023 – A mass shooting occurs in two locations in Lewiston, Maine. 18 people are killed and 13 more injured.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Lewiston_shootings)

🎖️ 1995 – A commuter train slams into a school bus in Fox River Grove, Illinois, killing seven students.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_Fox_River_Grove_bus%E2%80%93train_collision)

📡 1949 – The Battle of Guningtou in the Taiwan Strait begins.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Guningtou)

🏙️ 1812 – War of 1812: The American frigate, USS United States, commanded by Stephen Decatur, captures the British frigate HMS Macedonian.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_United_States_(1797))

⚙️ 1968 – A Fairchild F-27 crashes into Moose Mountain while on approach to Lebanon Municipal Airport in Lebanon, New Hampshire, killing 32 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairchild_F-27)

💥 2001 – Microsoft releases Windows XP, which becomes one of Microsoft's most successful operating systems.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft)

🕊️ 1917 – Old Style date of the October Revolution in Russia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Style_and_New_Style_dates)

⚔️ 1968 – Soyuz 2 is launched.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soyuz_2)

🏰 1940 – Benjamin O. Davis Sr. is named the first African American general in the United States Army.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_O._Davis_Sr.)
