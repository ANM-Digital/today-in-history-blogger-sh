# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 657 – First Fitna: In the Battle of Siffin, troops led by Ali ibn Abu Talib clash with those led by Muawiyah I.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Fitna)

🏰 811 – Battle of Pliska: Byzantine Emperor Nikephoros I is killed and his heir Staurakios is seriously wounded.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Pliska)

📜 920 – Rout of an alliance of Christian troops from Navarre and Léon against the Muslims at the Battle of Valdejunquera.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navarre)

🛡️ 1184 – Erfurt latrine disaster: At a Hoftag, a meeting with local notables, organized by Henry VI, the building collapses and many of the nobles in attendance drown in the sewage pits below.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erfurt_latrine_disaster)

⚓ 1309 – Henry VII is recognized King of the Romans (Holy Roman Emperor) by Pope Clement V.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_VII%2C_Holy_Roman_Emperor)

🚀 1509 – Krishnadevaraya ascends to the throne as Emperor of Vijayanagara, marking the beginning of the regeneration of the Vijayanagara Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krishnadevaraya)

💼 1703 – During the Bavarian Rummel the rural population of Tyrol drove the Bavarian Prince-Elector Maximilian II Emanuel out of North Tyrol with a victory at the Pontlatzer Bridge and thus prevented the Bavarian Army, which was allied with France, from marching as planned on Vienna during the War of the Spanish Succession.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Rummel)

🎖️ 1953 – Soldiers from the 2nd Battalion, Royal Australian Regiment repel a number of Chinese assaults against a key position known as The Hook during the Battle of the Samichon River, just hours before the Armistice Agreement is signed, ending the Korean War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Battalion%2C_Royal_Australian_Regiment)

📡 1890 – In Buenos Aires, Argentina the Revolución del Parque takes place, forcing President Miguel Ángel Juárez Celman's resignation.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires)

🏙️ 1891 – France annexes Tahiti.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tahiti)

⚙️ 1945 – World War II: HMS Vestal is the last British Royal Navy ship to be sunk in the war.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Vestal_(J215))

💥 1937 – Spanish Civil War: End of the Battle of Brunete with the Nationalist victory.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Brunete)

🕊️ 1958 – Explorer program: Explorer 4 is launched.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explorers_Program)

⚔️ 1944 – World War II: The Red Army enters Lviv, a major city in western Ukraine, capturing it from the Nazis. Only 300 Jews survive out of 160,000 living in Lviv prior to occupation.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Army)

🏰 2016 – Hillary Clinton becomes the first female nominee for president of the United States by a major political party at the Democratic National Convention in Philadelphia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillary_Clinton)
