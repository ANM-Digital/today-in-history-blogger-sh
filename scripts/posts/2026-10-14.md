# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 996 – Al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah becomes caliph. He is the first Fatimid ruler to have been born in Egypt and arguably the most controversial member of the dynasty.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Hakim_bi-Amr_Allah)

🏰 1066 – The Norman conquest of England begins with the Battle of Hastings.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Conquest)

📜 1322 – Robert the Bruce of Scotland defeats King Edward II of England at the Battle of Old Byland, forcing Edward to accept Scotland's independence.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_the_Bruce)

🛡️ 1586 – Mary, Queen of Scots, goes on trial for conspiracy against Queen Elizabeth I of England.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary%2C_Queen_of_Scots)

⚓ 1656 – The General Court of the Massachusetts Bay Colony enacts the first punitive legislation against the Religious Society of Friends.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_General_Court)

🚀 1758 – Seven Years' War: Frederick the Great suffers a rare defeat at the Battle of Hochkirch.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Years'_War)

💼 1809 – The Treaty of Schönbrunn is signed, ending the War of the Fifth Coalition, the final successful war in Napoleon Bonaparte's military career.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Sch%C3%B6nbrunn)

🎖️ 1943 – World War II: The Second Philippine Republic, a puppet state of Japan, is inaugurated with José P. Laurel as its president.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Philippine_Republic)

📡 1980 – The 6th Congress of the Workers' Party ended, having anointed North Korean President Kim Il Sung's son Kim Jong Il as his successor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6th_Congress_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea)

🏙️ 1968 – Jim Hines becomes the first man ever to break the so-called "ten-second barrier" in the 100-meter sprint with a time of 9.95 seconds.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Hines)

⚙️ 1973 – In the Thammasat student uprising, over 100,000 people protest in Thailand against the military government. Seventy-seven are killed and 857 are injured by soldiers.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1973_Thai_popular_uprising)

💥 1994 – Yasser Arafat, Yitzhak Rabin and Shimon Peres receive the Nobel Peace Prize for their role in the establishment of the Oslo Accords and the framing of future Palestinian self government.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yasser_Arafat)

🕊️ 1884 – George Eastman receives a U.S. Government patent on his new paper-strip photographic film.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Eastman)

⚔️ 2023 – Australians vote to reject a constitutional amendment that would have established an Indigenous Voice to Parliament.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Australian_Indigenous_Voice_referendum)

🏰 1923 – After the Irish Civil War the 1923 Irish hunger strikes were undertaken by thousands of Irish republican prisoners protesting the continuation of their internment without trial.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Civil_War)
