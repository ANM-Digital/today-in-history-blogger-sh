[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Montecatini)

💼 1475 – Hundred Years' War: The Treaty of Picquigny formally ends the war between the kingdoms of France and England.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hundred_Years'_War)

🎖️ 1521 – The Ottoman Turks capture Nándorfehérvár (Belgrade).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Turks)

⚙️ 1943 – World War II: German-occupied Denmark scuttles most of its navy; Germany dissolves the Danish government.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark_in_World_War_II)
