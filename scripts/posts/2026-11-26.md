# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 783 – The Asturian queen Adosinda is held at a monastery to prevent her nephew from retaking the throne from Mauregatus.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Asturias)

🏰 1161 – Battle of Caishi: A Song dynasty fleet fights a naval engagement with Jin dynasty ships on the Yangtze river during the Jin–Song Wars.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Caishi)

📜 1346 – Having been elected German king at Rhens on 11 July 1346, Charles IV is crowned King of Germany by bishop Walram of Cologne in Bonn.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_monarchs_of_Germany)

🛡️ 1476 – Vlad the Impaler defeats Basarab Laiota with the help of Stephen the Great and Stephen V Báthory and becomes the ruler of Wallachia for the third time.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vlad_the_Impaler)

⚓ 1778 – In the Hawaiian Islands, Captain James Cook becomes the first European to visit Maui.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaiian_Islands)

🚀 1920 – Ukrainian War of Independence: The Red Army launches a surprise attack against the Makhnovshchina.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_War_of_Independence)

💼 2025 – The Wang Fuk Court fire, a catastrophic fire in Tai Po, Hong Kong, leaves at least 168 dead and 79 injured.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Fuk_Court_fire)

🎖️ 2000 – George W. Bush is certified the winner of Florida's electoral votes by Katherine Harris, going on to win the United States presidential election, despite losing in the national popular vote.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_W._Bush)

📡 1998 – The Khanna rail disaster takes 212 lives in Khanna, Ludhiana, India.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Khanna_rail_collision)

🏙️ 2004 – Ruzhou School massacre: A man stabs and kills eight people and seriously wounds another four in a school dormitory in Ruzhou, China.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mass_stabbing_incidents_(before_2010))

⚙️ 1983 – Brink's-Mat robbery: In London, 6,800 gold bars worth nearly £26 million are stolen from the Brink's-Mat vault at Heathrow Airport.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brink's-Mat_robbery)

💥 2008 – Mumbai attacks, a series of coordinated terrorist attacks took place in Mumbai killing 166 citizens by 10 terrorists of Lashkar-e-Taiba, a Pakistan based extremist Islamist terrorist organisation.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Mumbai_attacks)

🕊️ 1991 – National Assembly of Azerbaijan abolishes the autonomous status of Nagorno-Karabakh Autonomous Oblast of Azerbaijan and renames several cities with Azeri names.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_(Azerbaijan))

⚔️ 1998 – Tony Blair becomes the first Prime Minister of the United Kingdom to address the Oireachtas, the parliament of the Republic of Ireland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Blair)

🏰 1944 – World War II: Germany begins V-1 and V-2 attacks on Antwerp, Belgium.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Germany)
