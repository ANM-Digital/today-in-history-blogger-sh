# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 681 – Pope Honorius I is posthumously excommunicated by the Sixth Ecumenical Council.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Honorius_I)

🏰 1400 – Owain Glyndŵr is declared Prince of Wales by his followers.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owain_Glynd%C5%B5r)

📜 1620 – Pilgrims set sail for Virginia from Plymouth, England in the Mayflower.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilgrims_(Plymouth_Colony))

🛡️ 1701 – James Francis Edward Stuart, sometimes called the "Old Pretender", becomes the Jacobite claimant to the thrones of England and Scotland on the death of his father, James II.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Francis_Edward_Stuart)

⚓ 1979 – Eight people escape from East Germany to the west in a homemade hot air balloon.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Germany_balloon_escape)

🚀 1978 – The 7.4 Mw Tabas earthquake affects the city of Tabas, Iran with a maximum Mercalli intensity of IX (Violent). At least 15,000 people are killed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_Tabas_earthquake)

💼 1994 – The British government lifts the broadcasting ban imposed against members of Sinn Féin and Irish paramilitary groups in 1988.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988%E2%80%931994_British_broadcasting_voice_restrictions)

🎖️ 1880 – The Cornell Daily Sun prints its first issue in Ithaca, New York.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cornell_Daily_Sun)

📡 1810 – With the Grito de Dolores, Father Miguel Hidalgo begins Mexico's fight for independence from Spain.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grito_de_Dolores)

🏙️ 2022 – The death of Mahsa Amini occurred in Tehran, Iran, sparking worldwide protests.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_of_Mahsa_Amini)

⚙️ 1893 – Settlers make a land run for prime land in the Cherokee Strip in Oklahoma.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_Run_of_1893)

💥 1945 – World War II: The Japanese occupation of Hong Kong comes to an end.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_occupation_of_Hong_Kong)

🕊️ 2015 – A 8.3 Mw earthquake strikes the Chilean city of Illapel, killing 15 people, injuring at least 34, leaving at least six missing, and causing extensive damage. One person also dies in Argentina.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Illapel_earthquake)

⚔️ 1992 – Black Wednesday: The British pound is forced out of the European Exchange Rate Mechanism by currency speculators and is forced to devalue against the German mark.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Wednesday)

🏰 2021 – Inspiration4, the first private orbital crewed spaceflight, is launched from the Kennedy Space Center.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspiration4)
