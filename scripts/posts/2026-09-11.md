# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 9 – The Battle of the Teutoburg Forest ends: The Roman Empire suffers the greatest defeat of its history and the Rhine is established as the border between the Empire and the so-called barbarians for the next four hundred years.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/0s)

🏰 911 – William I of Aquitaine gifts abbot Berno a group of lands close to Mâcon to found a new monastery. Contrary to other Benedictine monasteries, William sets the monastery outside of the control of any feudal lord.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_911)

📜 1185 – Isaac II Angelos kills Stephen Hagiochristophorites and then appeals to the people, resulting in the revolt that deposes Andronikos I Komnenos and places Isaac on the throne of the Byzantine Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_II_Angelos)

🛡️ 1275 – An earthquake occurred in the south of Great Britain, notably causing multiple fatalities as well as destroying St Michael's Church on Glastonbury Tor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1275_British_earthquake)

⚓ 1390 – Lithuanian Civil War (1389–1392): The Teutonic Knights begin a five-week siege of Vilnius.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Civil_War_(1389%E2%80%931392))

🚀 1541 – Santiago, Chile, is attacked by indigenous warriors, led by Michimalonco, to free eight indigenous chiefs held captive by the Spaniards.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago)

💼 1609 – Henry Hudson arrives on Manhattan Island and meets the indigenous people living there.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Hudson)

🎖️ 1708 – Charles XII of Sweden stops his march to conquer Moscow outside Smolensk, marking the turning point in the Great Northern War. The army is defeated nine months later in the Battle of Poltava, and the Swedish Empire ceases to be a major power.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_XII_of_Sweden)

📡 1943 – World War II: German troops occupy Corsica and Kosovo-Metohija ending the Italian occupation of Corsica.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II)

🏙️ 1992 – Hurricane Iniki strikes the island of Kauaʻi, Hawaii, killing 7 and causing $3.1bn in economic damages.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Iniki)

⚙️ 1916 – The Quebec Bridge's central span collapses, killing 11 men. The bridge previously collapsed completely on August 29, 1907.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quebec_Bridge)

💥 2015 – A crane collapses onto the Masjid al-Haram mosque in Saudi Arabia, killing 111 people and injuring 394 others.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mecca_crane_collapse)

🕊️ 1965 – Indo-Pakistani War: The Indian Army captures the town of Burki, just southeast of Lahore.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India%E2%80%93Pakistan_war_of_1965)

⚔️ 1813 – War of 1812: British troops arrive in Mount Vernon and prepare to march to and invade Washington, D.C.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_1812)

🏰 1967 – China's People's Liberation Army (PLA) launched an attack on Indian posts at Nathu La, Sikkim, India, which resulted in military clashes.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Liberation_Army)
