# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 54 – Roman emperor Claudius dies from poisoning under mysterious circumstances. He is succeeded by his adopted son Nero, rather than by Britannicus, his son with Messalina.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/50s)

🏰 409 – Vandals and Alans cross the Pyrenees and appear in Hispania.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vandals)

📜 1259 – The Provisions of Westminster, a law reform programme resulting out of the conflict of King Henry III of England and his barons, are approved.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisions_of_Westminster)

🛡️ 1307 – Hundreds of the Knights Templar in France are arrested at dawn by King Philip the Fair, and later confess under torture to heresy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knights_Templar)

⚓ 1479 – Hungarian forces under Stephen V Báthory decisively defeat an invading Ottoman army in the battle of Breadfield.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_V_B%C3%A1thory)

🚀 1644 – A Swedish–Dutch fleet defeats the Danish fleet at Fehmarn and captures about 1,000 prisoners.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Fehmarn_(1644))

💼 1710 – Port Royal, the capital of French Acadia, falls in a siege by British forces.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Port_Royal_(1710))

🎖️ 1885 – The Georgia Institute of Technology is founded in Atlanta, Georgia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech)

📡 1993 – At least 60 people die in eastern Papua New Guinea when a series of earthquakes rock the Finisterre Range, triggering massive landslides.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Finisterre_earthquakes)

🏙️ 1911 – Prince Arthur, Duke of Connaught and Strathearn, becomes the first Governor General of Canada of royal descent.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Arthur%2C_Duke_of_Connaught_and_Strathearn)

⚙️ 1908 – Margaret Travers Symons bursts into the UK parliament and becomes the first woman to speak there.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Travers_Symons)

💥 1962 – The Pacific Northwest experiences a cyclone the equal of a Category 3 hurricane, with winds above 150 mph. Forty-six people die.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbus_Day_storm_of_1962)

🕊️ 1843 – In New York City, B'nai B'rith, the oldest Jewish service organization in the world, is founded.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B'nai_B'rith)

⚔️ 1972 – Aeroflot Flight 217 crashes outside Moscow, killing 174.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeroflot_Flight_217)

🏰 2016 – The Maldives announces its decision to withdraw from the Commonwealth of Nations.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maldives)
