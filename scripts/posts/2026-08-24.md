# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 367 – Gratian, son of Roman Emperor Valentinian I, is named co-Augustus at the age of eight by his father.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gratian)

🏰 410 – The Visigoths under King Alaric I begin to pillage Rome.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visigoths)

📜 1185 – Sack of Thessalonica by the Normans.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sack_of_Thessalonica_(1185))

🛡️ 1200 – King John of England, signer of the first Magna Carta, marries Isabella of Angoulême in Angoulême Cathedral.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John%2C_King_of_England)

⚓ 1349 – Six thousand Jews are killed in Mainz after being blamed for the bubonic plague.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainz)

🚀 1482 – The town and castle of Berwick-upon-Tweed is captured from Scotland by an English army.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_invasion_of_Scotland_(1482))

💼 1516 – The Ottoman Empire under Selim I defeats the Mamluk Sultanate and captures present-day Syria at the Battle of Marj Dabiq.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Empire)

🎖️ 1608 – The first official English representative to India lands in Surat.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surat)

📡 1743 – The War of the Hats: The Swedish army surrenders to the Russians in Helsinki, ending the war and starting Lesser Wrath.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo-Swedish_War_(1741%E2%80%931743))

🏙️ 2010 – Henan Airlines Flight 8387 crashes at Yichun Lindu Airport in Yichun, Heilongjiang, China, killing 44 out of the 96 people on board.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henan_Airlines_Flight_8387)

⚙️ 2014 – A magnitude 6.0 earthquake strikes the San Francisco Bay Area; it is the largest in that area since 1989.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_South_Napa_earthquake)

💥 1992 – Hurricane Andrew makes landfall in Homestead, Florida as a Category 5 hurricane, causing up to $25 billion (1992 USD) in damages.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Andrew)

🕊️ 1989 – Colombian drug barons declare "total war" on the Colombian government.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombia)

⚔️ 1944 – World War II: Allied troops begin the attack on Paris.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberation_of_Paris)

🏰 1820 – Constitutionalist insurrection at Oporto, Portugal.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutionalism)
