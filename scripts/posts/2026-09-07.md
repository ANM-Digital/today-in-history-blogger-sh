# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 878 – Louis the Stammerer is crowned as king of West Francia by Pope John VIII.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_the_Stammerer)

🏰 1159 – Cardinal Rolando Bandinelli is elected Pope Alexander III, prompting the election of Cardinal Octaviano Monticelli as Antipope Victor IV the same day.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Alexander_III)

📜 1228 – Holy Roman Emperor Frederick II lands in Acre, Israel, and starts the Sixth Crusade, which results in a peaceful restoration of the Kingdom of Jerusalem.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_II%2C_Holy_Roman_Emperor)

🛡️ 1303 – Guillaume de Nogaret takes Pope Boniface VIII prisoner on behalf of Philip IV of France.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillaume_de_Nogaret)

⚓ 1565 – Arrival of the so-called Grande Soccorso ("great relief") by Philip II of Spain to the Great Siege of Malta.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_II_of_Spain)

🚀 1620 – The town of Kokkola (Swedish: Karleby) is founded by King Gustavus Adolphus of Sweden.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kokkola)

💼 1706 – War of the Spanish Succession: Siege of Turin ends, leading to the withdrawal of French forces from North Italy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_Spanish_Succession)

🎖️ 1920 – Two newly purchased Savoia flying boats crash in the Swiss Alps en route to Finland where they were to serve with the Finnish Air Force, killing both crews.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAI-Marchetti)

📡 1970 – Vietnam Television is established.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_Television)

🏙️ 1977 – The Torrijos–Carter Treaties between Panama and the United States on the status of the Panama Canal are signed. The United States agrees to transfer control of the canal to Panama at the end of the 20th century.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torrijos%E2%80%93Carter_Treaties)

⚙️ 2010 – A Chinese fishing trawler collides with two Japanese Coast Guard patrol boats in disputed waters near the Senkaku Islands.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Senkaku_boat_collision_incident)

💥 1921 – In Atlantic City, New Jersey, the first Miss America Pageant, a two-day event, is held.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_City%2C_New_Jersey)

🕊️ 1921 – The Legion of Mary, the largest apostolic organization of lay people in the Catholic Church, is founded in Dublin, Ireland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Mary)

⚔️ 1909 – Eugène Lefebvre crashes a new French-built Wright biplane during a test flight at Port-Aviation (often called "Juvisy Airfield") at Viry-Châtillon, south of Paris, becoming the first aviator in the world to lose his life piloting a powered heavier-than-air craft.[better source needed]. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eug%C3%A8ne_Lefebvre)

🏰 1943 – A fire at the Gulf Hotel in Houston kills 55 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_Hotel_fire)
