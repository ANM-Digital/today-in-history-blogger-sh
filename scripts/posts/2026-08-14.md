# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ -74 – A group of officials, led by the Western Han minister Huo Guang, present articles of impeachment against the new emperor, Liu He, to the imperial regent, Empress Dowager Shangguan.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_dynasty)

🏰 1040 – King Duncan I is killed in battle against his first cousin and rival Macbeth. The latter succeeds him as King of Scotland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duncan_I_of_Scotland)

📜 1136 – Abbasid Caliph al-Rashid Billah flees from Bagdhad, which had been besieged by an army of the Seljuk Turks under Sultan Ghiyath ad-Din Mas'ud for fifty days.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Rashid_Billah)

🛡️ 1264 – After tricking the Venetian galley fleet into sailing east to the Levant, the Genoese capture an entire Venetian trade convoy at the Battle of Saseno.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Venice)

⚓ 1352 – War of the Breton Succession: Anglo-Bretons defeat the French in the Battle of Mauron.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_Breton_Succession)

🚀 1592 – The first sighting of the Falkland Islands by John Davis.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falkland_Islands)

💼 1720 – The Spanish military Villasur expedition is defeated by Pawnee and Otoe warriors near present-day Columbus, Nebraska.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villasur_expedition)

🎖️ 1996 – Greek Cypriot refugee Solomos Solomou is shot and killed by a Turkish security officer while trying to climb a flagpole in order to remove a Turkish flag from its mast in the United Nations Buffer Zone in Cyprus.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Solomos_Solomou)

📡 2005 – Helios Airways Flight 522, en route from Larnaca, Cyprus to Prague, Czech Republic via Athens, crashes in the hills near Grammatiko, Greece, killing 121 passengers and crew.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helios_Airways_Flight_522)

🏙️ 1980 – Lech Wałęsa leads strikes at the Gdańsk, Poland shipyards.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lech_Wa%C5%82%C4%99sa)

⚙️ 1933 – Loggers cause a forest fire in the Coast Range of Oregon, later known as the first forest fire of the Tillamook Burn;  destroying 240,000 acres (970 km2) of land.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildfire)

💥 1880 – Construction of Cologne Cathedral, the most famous landmark in Cologne, Germany, is completed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne_Cathedral)

🕊️ 2021 – A magnitude 7.2 earthquake strikes southwestern Haiti, killing at least 2,248 people and causing a humanitarian crisis.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Haiti_earthquake)

⚔️ 1920 – The 1920 Summer Olympics, having started four months earlier, officially open in Antwerp, Belgium, with the newly adopted Olympic flag and the Olympic oath being raised and taken at the Opening Ceremony for the first time in Olympic history.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1920_Summer_Olympics)

🏰 1959 – Founding and first official meeting of the American Football League.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Football_League)
