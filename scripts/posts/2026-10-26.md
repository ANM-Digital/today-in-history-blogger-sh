# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 1185 – The Uprising of Asen and Peter begins on the feast day of St. Demetrius of Thessaloniki and ends with the creation of the Second Bulgarian Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uprising_of_Asen_and_Peter)

🏰 1341 – The Byzantine civil war of 1341–1347 formally begins with the proclamation of John VI Kantakouzenos as Byzantine Emperor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_civil_war_of_1341%E2%80%931347)

📜 1520 – Charles V is crowned as Holy Roman Emperor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_V%2C_Holy_Roman_Emperor)

🛡️ 1640 – The Treaty of Ripon is signed, restoring peace between Covenanter Scotland and King Charles I of England.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Ripon)

⚓ 1774 – American Revolution: The First Continental Congress adjourns in Philadelphia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution)

🚀 1937 – Nazi Germany begins expulsions of 18,000 Polish Jews.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Germany)

💼 1995 – Mossad agents assassinate Palestinian Islamic Jihad leader Fathi Shaqaqi in his hotel in Malta.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mossad)

📡 1859 – The Royal Charter Storm kills at least eight hundred people in the British Isles.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Charter_Storm)

🏙️ 1905 – King Oscar II recognizes the dissolution of the union between Norway and Sweden.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_II)

⚙️ 2001 – The United States passes the USA PATRIOT Act into law.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriot_Act)

💥 1955 – After the last Allied troops have left the country, and following the provisions of the Austrian Independence Treaty, Austria declares that it will never join a military alliance.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_State_Treaty)

🕊️ 1871 – Liberian President Edward James Roye is deposed in a coup d'état.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_James_Roye)

⚔️ 1991 – Three months after the end of the Ten-Day War, the last soldier of the Yugoslav People's Army leaves the territory of the Republic of Slovenia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ten-Day_War)

🏰 1912 – First Balkan War: The Ottomans lose the cities of Thessaloniki and Skopje.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Balkan_War)
