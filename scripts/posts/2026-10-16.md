# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 456 – Ricimer defeats Avitus at Piacenza and becomes master of the Western Roman Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricimer)

🏰 690 – Empress Wu Zetian ascends to the throne of the Tang dynasty and proclaims herself ruler of the Chinese Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wu_Zetian)

📜 912 – Abd ar-Rahman III becomes the eighth Emir of Córdoba.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abd_al-Rahman_III)

🛡️ 1311 – The Council of Vienne convenes for the first time.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Vienne)

⚓ 1590 – Prince Gesualdo of Venosa murders his wife and her lover.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlo_Gesualdo)

🚀 1736 – Mathematician William Whiston's predicted comet fails to strike the Earth.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Whiston)

💼 1909 – William Howard Taft and Porfirio Díaz hold the first summit between a U.S. and a Mexican president. They narrowly escape assassination.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Howard_Taft)

🎖️ 1836 – Great Trek: Afrikaner voortrekkers repulse a Matabele attack, but lose their livestock.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Vegkop)

📡 1943 – Holocaust in Italy: Raid on the Roman Ghetto.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raid_on_the_Roman_Ghetto)

🏙️ 1919 – Adolf Hitler delivers his first public address at a meeting of the German Workers' Party.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Workers'_Party)

⚙️ 1859 – Origins of the American Civil War: Abolitionist John Brown and his supporters launch a raid on Harpers Ferry, Virginia (now West Virginia).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Origins_of_the_American_Civil_War)

💥 1984 – Desmond Tutu is awarded the Nobel Peace Prize.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desmond_Tutu)

🕊️ 1905 – The Partition of Bengal in India takes place.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_of_Bengal_(1905))

⚔️ 1991 – George Hennard runs amok in Killeen, Texas, killing 23 and wounding 20.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luby's_shooting)

🏰 1841 – Queen's University is founded in the Province of Canada.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston)
