# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 654 – Pope Eugene I elected to succeed Martinus I.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Eugene_I)

🏰 955 – Battle of Lechfeld: Otto I, Holy Roman Emperor defeats the Magyars, ending 50 years of Magyar invasion of the West.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Lechfeld)

📜 1030 – The Battle of Azaz ends with a humiliating retreat of the Byzantine emperor, Romanos III Argyros, against the Mirdasid rulers of Aleppo. The retreat degenerates into a rout, in which Romanos himself barely escapes capture.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Azaz_(1030))

🛡️ 1258 – Manfred, illegitimate son of Frederick II, is crowned king of Sicily.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_II%2C_Holy_Roman_Emperor)

⚓ 1316 – The Second Battle of Athenry takes place near Athenry during the Bruce campaign in Ireland.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Athenry)

🚀 1512 – The naval Battle of Saint-Mathieu, during the War of the League of Cambrai, sees the simultaneous destruction of the Breton ship La Cordelière and the English ship The Regent.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Saint-Mathieu)

💼 1628 – The Swedish warship Vasa sinks on her maiden voyage off Stockholm.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasa_(ship))

🎖️ 1741 – King Marthanda Varma of Travancore defeats the Dutch East India Company at the Battle of Colachel, effectively bringing about the end of the Dutch colonial rule in India.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marthanda_Varma)

📡 2012 – The Marikana massacre begins near Rustenburg, South Africa, resulting in the deaths of 47 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marikana_massacre)

🏙️ 1904 – Russo-Japanese War: The Battle of the Yellow Sea between the Russian and Japanese battleship fleets takes place.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo-Japanese_War)

⚙️ 1944 – World War II: The Battle of Guam in the Pacific comes to an effective end.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II)

💥 2019 – Philip Manshaus shoots his stepsister and attacks a mosque in the Bærum mosque shooting.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_B%C3%A6rum_murder_and_mosque_attack)

🕊️ 1949 – An amendment to the National Security Act of 1947 enhances the authority of the United States Secretary of Defense over the Army, Navy and Air Force, and replaces the National Military Establishment with the Department of Defense.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Act_of_1947)

⚔️ 1999 – One person is killed and five people are injured in the Los Angeles Jewish Community Center shooting.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_Los_Angeles_Jewish_Community_Center_shooting)

🏰 1954 – At Massena, New York, the groundbreaking ceremony for the Saint Lawrence Seaway is held.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massena%2C_New_York)
