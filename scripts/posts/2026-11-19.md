# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 461 – Libius Severus is declared emperor of the Western Roman Empire. The real power is in the hands of the magister militum Ricimer.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libius_Severus)

🏰 636 – The Rashidun Caliphate defeats the Sasanian Empire at the Battle of al-Qādisiyyah in Iraq.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashidun_Caliphate)

📜 1341 – Coronation of John V Palaiologos as Byzantine Emperor during the Byzantine civil war of 1341–1347.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_V_Palaiologos)

🛡️ 1646 – The current Saint Peter's Basilica is consecrated in Rome, replacing an earlier basilica on the same site.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Peter's_Basilica)

⚓ 1794 – The United States and the Kingdom of Great Britain sign Jay's Treaty, which attempts to resolve some of the lingering problems left over from the American Revolutionary War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Treaty)

🚀 1967 – The establishment of TVB, the first wireless commercial television station in Hong Kong.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TVB)

💼 1911 – The Doom Bar in Cornwall claims two ships, Island Maid and Angele, the latter killing the entire crew except the captain.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_Bar)

🎖️ 1952 – Greek Field Marshal Alexander Papagos becomes the 152nd Prime Minister of Greece.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandros_Papagos)

📡 1985 – Pennzoil wins a US$10.53 billion judgment against Texaco, in the largest civil verdict in the history of the United States, stemming from Texaco executing a contract to buy Getty Oil after Pennzoil had entered into an unsigned, yet still binding, buyout contract with Getty.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennzoil)

🏙️ 1988 – Serbian communist representative and future Serbian and Yugoslav president Slobodan Milošević publicly declares that Serbia is under attack from Albanian separatists in Kosovo as well as internal treachery within Yugoslavia and a foreign conspiracy to destroy Serbia and Yugoslavia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbia)

⚙️ 1979 – Iran hostage crisis: Iranian leader Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini orders the release of 13 female and black American hostages being held at the US Embassy in Tehran.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_hostage_crisis)

💥 2022 – A gunman kills five and injures 17 at Club Q, a gay nightclub in Colorado Springs, Colorado.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Club_Q_shooting)

🕊️ 2010 – The first of four explosions takes place at the Pike River Mine in New Zealand. Twenty-nine people are killed in the nation's worst mining disaster since 1914.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pike_River_Mine_disaster)

⚔️ 1999 – John Carpenter becomes the first person to win the top prize in the TV game show Who Wants to Be a Millionaire?. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Carpenter_(game_show_contestant))

🏰 1863 – American Civil War: U.S. President Abraham Lincoln delivers the Gettysburg Address at the dedication ceremony for the military cemetery at Gettysburg, Pennsylvania.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War)
