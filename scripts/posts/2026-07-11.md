# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 472 – Western Roman Emperor Anthemius is killed by troops of the Roman Gothic general Ricimer.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_emperor)

🏰 813 – Byzantine emperor Michael I, under threat by conspiracies, abdicates in favor of his general Leo the Armenian, and becomes a monk (under the name Athanasius).. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_I_Rangabe)

📜 911 – Signing of the Treaty of Saint-Clair-sur-Epte between Charles the Simple and Rollo of Normandy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_911)

🛡️ 1174 – Baldwin IV, 13, becomes King of Jerusalem, with Raymond III, Count of Tripoli as regent and William of Tyre as chancellor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldwin_IV_of_Jerusalem)

⚓ 1276 – Election of pope Adrian V under the influence of Charles I of Anjou following the death of pope Gregory X earlier that year. Adrian V dies the following month.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_1276_conclave)

🚀 1302 – Battle of the Golden Spurs (Guldensporenslag in Dutch): A coalition around the Flemish cities defeats the king of France's royal army.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Golden_Spurs)

💼 1405 – Ming admiral Zheng He sets sail to explore the world for the first time.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ming_dynasty)

🎖️ 1576 – While exploring the North Atlantic Ocean in an attempt to find the Northwest Passage, Martin Frobisher sights Greenland, mistaking it for the hypothesized (but non-existent) island of "Frisland".. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Ocean)

📡 1616 – Samuel de Champlain returns to Quebec.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_de_Champlain)

🏙️ 1708 – A coalition army of Dutch, English and German troops under the duke of Marlborough defeats a French army under the duke of Burgundy and the Marshal Vendome in the battle of Oudenarde.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Churchill%2C_1st_Duke_of_Marlborough)

⚙️ 1921 – Former president of the United States William Howard Taft is sworn in as 10th chief justice of the U.S. Supreme Court, becoming the only person ever to hold both offices.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_United_States)

💥 1960 – Congo Crisis: The State of Katanga breaks away from the Democratic Republic of the Congo.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congo_Crisis)

🕊️ 1914 – The US Navy launches the USS Nevada (BB-36) as its first standard-type battleship.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Nevada_(BB-36))

⚔️ 1947 – The Exodus 1947 heads to Palestine from France.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Exodus)

🏰 1962 – First transatlantic satellite television transmission.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_television)
