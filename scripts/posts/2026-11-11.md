# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 109 – Roman emperor Trajan celebrates his newly constructed naumachia with a display of ships and mock battles.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_109)

🏰 308 – At Carnuntum, Emperor emeritus Diocletian confers with Galerius, Augustus of the East, and Maximianus, the recently returned former Augustus of the West, in an attempt to end the civil wars of the Tetrarchy.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnuntum)

📜 1028 – Constantine VIII dies, ending his uninterrupted reign as emperor or co-emperor of the Byzantine Empire of 66 years.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine_VIII)

🛡️ 1100 – Henry I of England marries Matilda of Scotland, the daughter of Malcolm III of Scotland and a direct descendant of the Saxon king Edmund Ironside; Matilda is crowned on the same day.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_I_of_England)

⚓ 1215 – The Fourth Council of the Lateran meets, defining the doctrine of transubstantiation, the process by which bread and wine are, by that doctrine, said to transform into the body and blood of Christ.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Council_of_the_Lateran)

🚀 1417 – The election of pope Martin V at the Council of Constance ends the Western Schism.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Martin_V)

💼 1500 – Treaty of Granada: Louis XII of France and Ferdinand II of Aragon agree to divide the Kingdom of Naples between them.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Granada_(1500))

🎖️ 1606 – The peace treaty of Zsitvatorok ends the Long Turkish War between the Habsburg and the Ottoman empires. It stabilises the borderlands in Hungary and the Ottoman Sultan recognises the Holy Roman Emperor as his equal.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_of_Zsitvatorok)

📡 1724 – Joseph Blake, alias Blueskin, a highwayman known for attacking "Thief-Taker General" (and thief) Jonathan Wild at the Old Bailey, is hanged in London.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Blake_(criminal))

🏙️ 1919 – The Industrial Workers of the World attack an Armistice Day parade in Centralia, Washington, ultimately resulting in the deaths of five people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_Workers_of_the_World)

⚙️ 1967 – Vietnam War: In a propaganda ceremony in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, three American prisoners of war are released by the Viet Cong and turned over to "new left" antiwar activist Tom Hayden.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_War)

💥 2024 – A vehicle-ramming attack in Zhuhai, China, kills 38 people and injures 48.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Zhuhai_car_attack)

🕊️ 1965 – United Air Lines Flight 227 crashes at Salt Lake City International Airport, killing 43.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Air_Lines_Flight_227)

⚔️ 1992 – The General Synod of the Church of England votes to allow women to become priests.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Synod_of_the_Church_of_England)

🏰 1839 – The Virginia Military Institute is founded in Lexington, Virginia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Military_Institute)
