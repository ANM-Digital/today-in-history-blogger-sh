# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 130 – Emperor Hadrian establishes the city of Antinoöpolis on the Nile in honour of his companion Antinous, creating a new Hellenizing foundation in Roman Egypt.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_130)

🏰 637 – Arab–Byzantine wars: Antioch surrenders to the Rashidun Caliphate after the Battle of the Iron Bridge.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab%E2%80%93Byzantine_wars)

📜 701 – Election of Pope John VI following the death of Pope Sergius I in the previous month.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_John_VI)

🛡️ 942 – Election of Pope Marinus II following the death of Pope Stephen VIII in the same month.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Marinus_II)

⚓ 1137 – Ranulf of Apulia defeats Roger II of Sicily at the Battle of Rignano, securing his position as duke until his death two years later.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranulf_II_of_Alife)

🚀 1270 – The Eighth Crusade ends by an agreement between Charles I of Anjou (replacing his deceased brother King Louis IX of France) and the Hafsid dynasty of Tunis, Tunisia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eighth_Crusade)

💼 1340 – Reconquista: Portuguese and Castilian forces halt a Muslim invasion at the Battle of Río Salado.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reconquista)

🎖️ 1657 – Anglo-Spanish War: Spanish forces fail to retake Jamaica at the Battle of Ocho Rios.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Spanish_War_(1654%E2%80%931660))

📡 1953 – President Eisenhower approves the top-secret document NSC 162/2 concerning the maintenance of a strong nuclear deterrent force against the Soviet Union.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwight_D._Eisenhower)

🏙️ 1888 – The Rudd Concession is granted by Matabeleland to agents of Cecil Rhodes.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudd_Concession)

⚙️ 1836 – Louis Napoleon launches the unsuccessful Strasbourg Coup to overthrow the July Monarchy in France. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleon_III)

💥 1961 – The Soviet Union detonates the Tsar Bomba, the most powerful explosive device ever detonated.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union)

🕊️ 1968 – A squad of 120 North Korean Army commandos land in boats along a 25-mile long section of the eastern coast of South Korea in a failed attempt to overthrow the dictatorship of Park Chung Hee and bring about the reunification of Korea.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_People's_Army)

⚔️ 2005 – The rebuilt Dresden Frauenkirche (destroyed in the firebombing of Dresden during World War II) is reconsecrated after a thirteen-year rebuilding project.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frauenkirche%2C_Dresden)

🏰 2023 – First rescue of a prisoner during the Israeli invasion of Gaza.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rescue_of_Ori_Megidish)
