📜 1100 – Election of Antipope Theodoric.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipope_Theodoric)

⚓ 1331 – Stefan Dušan declares himself king of Serbia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Du%C5%A1an)

💼 1655 – Warsaw falls without resistance to a small force under the command of Charles X Gustav of Sweden during The Deluge, making it the first time the city is captured by a foreign army.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw)

🎖️ 1726 – J. S. Bach leads the first performance of Geist und Seele wird verwirret, BWV 35, a solo cantata for alto voice.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Sebastian_Bach)

📡 1892 – The Pledge of Allegiance is first recited.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pledge_of_Allegiance)

🏙️ 1813 – At the final stage of the Peninsular War, British-Portuguese troops capture the town of Donostia (now San Sebastián), resulting in a rampage and eventual destruction of the town.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peninsular_War)

⚙️ 1888 – In London, the body of Jack the Ripper's second murder victim, Annie Chapman, is found.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Chapman)

💥 2023 – The 2023 Rugby World Cup, the tenth men's Rugby World Cup is held in France. The opening ceremony, directed and written by Jean Dujardin, Olivier Ferracci and Nora Matthey, took place at the Stade de France in Saint-Denis, before the opening match between France and New Zealand, which saw the host nation winning 27 to 13.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Rugby_World_Cup)

🕊️ 2016 – NASA launches OSIRIS-REx, its first asteroid sample return mission. The probe visited 101955 Bennu and returned with samples in September 2023.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OSIRIS-REx)

⚔️ 1925 – Rif War: Spanish forces including troops from the Foreign Legion under Colonel Francisco Franco landing at Al Hoceima, Morocco.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rif_War)
