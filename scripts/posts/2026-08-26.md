# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 683 – The Battle of al-Harrah concludes, with Yazid I's army killing 11,000 people of the city of Medina.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_al-Harra)

🏰 1071 – The Seljuq Turks defeat the Byzantine army at the Battle of Manzikert, and soon gain control of most of Anatolia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seljuk_dynasty)

📜 1278 – Ladislaus IV of Hungary and Rudolf I of Germany defeat Ottokar II of Bohemia in the Battle on the Marchfeld near Dürnkrut in (then) Moravia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladislaus_IV_of_Hungary)

🛡️ 1303 – Chittorgarh falls to the Delhi Sultanate.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Chittorgarh_(1303))

⚓ 1444 – Battle of St. Jakob an der Birs: A vastly outnumbered force of Swiss Confederates is defeated by the Dauphin Louis (future Louis XI of France) and his army of 'Armagnacs' near Basel.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_St._Jakob_an_der_Birs)

🚀 1542 – A year and a half after leaving Guayaquil on the Pacific coast, Francisco de Orellana finishes his overland journey across South America and reaches the mouth of the Amazon River on the Atlantic coast.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guayaquil)

💼 1642 – Dutch–Portuguese War: Second Battle of San Salvador: The Dutch force the Spanish garrison at San Salvador (modern day Keelung, Taiwan) to surrender, ending the short-lived Spanish colony on Formosa and replacing it with a new Dutch administration.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch%E2%80%93Portuguese_War)

🎖️ 1748 – The first Lutheran denomination in North America, the Pennsylvania Ministerium, is founded in Philadelphia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lutheranism)

📡 2009 – Kidnapping victim Jaycee Dugard is discovered alive in California after being missing for over 18 years. Her captors, Phillip and Nancy Garrido, are apprehended.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidnapping_of_Jaycee_Dugard)

🏙️ 2011 – The Boeing 787 Dreamliner, Boeing's all-new composite airliner, receives certification from the EASA and the FAA.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_787_Dreamliner)

⚙️ 2014 – The Jay Report into the Rotherham child sexual exploitation scandal is published.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotherham_child_sexual_exploitation_scandal)

💥 1940 – World War II: Chad becomes the first French colony to join the Allies under the administration of Félix Éboué, France's first black colonial governor.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II)

🕊️ 1833 – The great 1833 Kathmandu–Bihar earthquake causes major damage in Nepal, northern India and Tibet, a total of 500 people perish.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1833_Bihar%E2%80%93Nepal_earthquake)

⚔️ 1883 – The 1883 eruption of Krakatoa begins its final, paroxysmal, stage.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1883_eruption_of_Krakatoa)

🏰 1998 – The first flight of the Boeing Delta III ends in disaster 75 seconds after liftoff, resulting in the loss of the Galaxy X  communications satellite.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_III)
