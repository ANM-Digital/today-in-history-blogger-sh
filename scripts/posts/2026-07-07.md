# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 1124 – The city of Tyre falls to the Venetian Crusade after a siege of nineteen weeks.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyre%2C_Lebanon)

🏰 1456 – A retrial verdict acquits Joan of Arc of heresy 25 years after her execution.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rehabilitation_trial_of_Joan_of_Arc)

📜 1520 – Spanish conquistadores defeat a larger Aztec army at the Battle of Otumba.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conquistador)

🛡️ 1667 – An English fleet completes the destruction of a French merchant fleet off Fort St Pierre, Martinique during the Second Anglo-Dutch War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Pierre%2C_Martinique)

⚓ 1770 – The Battle of Larga between the Russian Empire and the Ottoman Empire takes place.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Larga)

🚀 2011 – A man goes on a killing spree in Grand Rapids, Michigan, killing 7 and wounding 2 before killing himself.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Grand_Rapids_shootings)

💼 1946 – Howard Hughes nearly dies when his XF-11 reconnaissance aircraft prototype crashes in a Beverly Hills neighborhood.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Hughes)

🎖️ 2021 – Haitian crisis: Haitian President Jovenel Moïse is assassinated in his residence in the capital of Port-au-Prince.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_crisis_(2018%E2%80%93present))

📡 1962 – Alitalia Flight 771 crashes in Junnar, Maharashtra, India, killing 94 people.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alitalia_Flight_771)

🏙️ 1981 – US President Ronald Reagan nominates Sandra Day O'Connor to become the first female member of the Supreme Court of the United States.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_United_States)

⚙️ 1953 – Ernesto "Che" Guevara sets out on a trip through Bolivia, Peru, Ecuador, Panama, Costa Rica, Nicaragua, Honduras, and El Salvador.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Che_Guevara)

💥 1911 – The United States, UK, Japan, and Russia sign the North Pacific Fur Seal Convention of 1911 banning open-water seal hunting, the first international treaty to address wildlife preservation issues.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Pacific_Fur_Seal_Convention_of_1911)

🕊️ 1834 – In New York City, four nights of rioting against abolitionists began.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_anti-abolitionist_riots_(1834))

⚔️ 1892 – The Katipunan is established, the discovery of which by Spanish authorities initiated the Philippine Revolution.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katipunan)
