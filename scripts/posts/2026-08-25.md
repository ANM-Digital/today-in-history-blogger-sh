# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 383 – Roman Emperor Gratian is murdered at a banquet after having surrendered to the forces of rival emperor Magnus Maximus.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_383)

🏰 450 – Marcian is proclaimed Eastern Roman emperor at Hebdomon and marries empress Pulcheria.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcian)

📜 766 – Emperor Constantine V humiliates nineteen high-ranking officials, after discovering a plot against him. He executes the leaders, Constantine Podopagouros and his brother Strategios.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine_V)

🛡️ 1248 – The Dutch city of Ommen receives city rights and fortification rights from Otto III, the Archbishop of Utrecht.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ommen)

⚓ 1537 – The Honourable Artillery Company, the oldest surviving regiment in the British Army, and the second most senior, is formed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honourable_Artillery_Company)

🚀 1609 – Galileo Galilei demonstrates his first telescope to Venetian lawmakers.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galileo_Galilei)

💼 1758 – Seven Years' War: Frederick II of Prussia defeats the Russian army at the Battle of Zorndorf.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Years'_War)

🎖️ 1985 – Bar Harbor Airlines Flight 1808 crashes near Auburn/Lewiston Municipal Airport in Auburn, Maine, killing all eight people on board including peace activist and child actress Samantha Smith.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar_Harbor_Airlines_Flight_1808)

📡 2017 – Hurricane Harvey makes landfall in Texas as a powerful Category 4 hurricane, the strongest hurricane to make landfall in the United States since 2004.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Harvey)

🏙️ 1961 – President Jânio Quadros of Brazil resigns after just seven months in power, initiating a political crisis that culminates in a military coup in 1964.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%A2nio_Quadros)

⚙️ 1894 – Kitasato Shibasaburō discovers the infectious agent of the bubonic plague and publishes his findings in The Lancet.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitasato_Shibasabur%C5%8D)

💥 1835 – The first Great Moon Hoax article is published in The New York Sun, announcing the discovery of life and civilization on the Moon.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Moon_Hoax)

🕊️ 1989 – Voyager 2 spacecraft makes its closest approach to Neptune, the last planet in the Solar System at the time, due to Pluto being within Neptune's orbit from 1979 to 1999.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voyager_2)

⚔️ 1950 – To avert a threatened strike during the Korean War, President Truman orders Secretary of the Army Frank Pace to seize control of the nation's railroads.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Pace)

🏰 1967 – George Lincoln Rockwell, founder of the American Nazi Party, is assassinated by a former member of his group.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lincoln_Rockwell)
