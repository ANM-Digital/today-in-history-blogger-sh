# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 768 – Carloman I and Charlemagne are crowned kings of the Franks.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carloman_I)

🏰 1238 – James I of Aragon founds the Kingdom of Valencia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_I_of_Aragon)

📜 1410 – The first known mention of the Prague astronomical clock.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague_astronomical_clock)

🛡️ 1594 – Pressed by a food embargo, the five Catholic cantons of Central Switzerland declare war on the Protestant canton of Zurich, starting the Second War of Kappel.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Switzerland)

⚓ 1604 – Kepler's Supernova is the most recent supernova to be observed within the Milky Way.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kepler's_Supernova)

🚀 1701 – The Collegiate School of Connecticut (later renamed Yale University) is chartered in Old Saybrook, Connecticut.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University)

💼 1874 – The Universal Postal Union is created by the Treaty of Bern.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Postal_Union)

🎖️ 1936 – Boulder Dam (later Hoover Dam) begins to generate electricity and transmit it to Los Angeles.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoover_Dam)

📡 1919 – The Cincinnati Reds win the World Series, resulting in the Black Sox Scandal.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati_Reds)

🏙️ 1962 – Uganda becomes an independent Commonwealth realm.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uganda)

⚙️ 1911 – An accidental bomb explosion triggers the Wuchang Uprising against the Qing dynasty, beginning the Xinhai Revolution.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuchang_Uprising)

💥 1913 – The steamship SS Volturno catches fire in the mid-Atlantic.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Volturno_(1906))

🕊️ 1981 – President François Mitterrand abolishes capital punishment in France.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran%C3%A7ois_Mitterrand)

⚔️ 2007 – The Dow Jones Industrial Average reaches its all-time high of 14,164 points before rapidly declining due to the 2008 financial crisis.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dow_Jones_Industrial_Average)

🏰 1970 – The Khmer Republic is proclaimed in Cambodia.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_Republic)
