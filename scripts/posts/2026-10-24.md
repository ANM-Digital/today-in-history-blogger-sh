# 🏛️ Today in History


⚔️ 69 – In the Second Battle of Bedriacum, troops loyal to Vespasian defeat those of Emperor Vitellius.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_69)

🏰 1260 – Chartres Cathedral is dedicated in the presence of King Louis IX of France.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chartres_Cathedral)

📜 1360 – The Treaty of Brétigny is ratified, marking the end of the first phase of the Hundred Years' War.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Br%C3%A9tigny)

🛡️ 1590 – John White, the governor of the second Roanoke Colony, returns to England after an unsuccessful search for the "lost" colonists.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_White_(colonist_and_artist))

⚓ 1641 – Felim O'Neill of Kinard, the leader of the Irish Rebellion, issues his Proclamation of Dungannon, justifying the uprising and declaring continued loyalty to King Charles I of England.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felim_O'Neill_of_Kinard)

🚀 1795 – Poland is completely consumed by Russia, Prussia and Austria.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Partition_of_Poland)

💼 1945 – The United Nations Charter comes into effect.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charter_of_the_United_Nations)

🎖️ 1912 – First Balkan War: The Battle of Kumanovo concludes with the Serbian victory against the Ottoman Empire.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kumanovo)

📡 2018 – The world's longest sea crossing, the Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macau Bridge, opens for public traffic.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong%E2%80%93Zhuhai%E2%80%93Macau_Bridge)

🏙️ 1944 – World War II: Japan's center force is temporarily repulsed in the Battle of Leyte Gulf.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II)

⚙️ 1929 – "Black Thursday" on the New York Stock Exchange, the first day of the panic in the markets that contributes to the Great Depression.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Street_crash_of_1929)

💥 2014 – The China National Space Administration launches an experimental lunar mission, Chang'e 5-T1, which will loop behind the Moon and return to Earth.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_National_Space_Administration)

🕊️ 1861 – The first transcontinental telegraph line across the United States is completed.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_transcontinental_telegraph)

⚔️ 1931 – The George Washington Bridge opens to public traffic over the Hudson River.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_Bridge)

🏰 1944 – World War II: The USS Shark (SS-314) was lost with all 87 hands in the Bashi Straits after torpedoing the Japanese freighter Arisan Maru.. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Shark_(SS-314))
